# ISO/TS 82304-2

ISO/TS 82304-2 is an international technical specification, publisht unner ISO Technical Committee 215 (Health Informatics), that defines a structurit method for assessin the quality o health an wellness applications — coverin usability, technical robustness an reliability, interoperability, content quality, an data security an privacy — for products that faa ootside the scope o full medical device regulation but still materially affect a user's health decisions or behaviour. It's there tae fill a specific gap: the muckle majority o consumer-facin health an wellness apps (fitness trackers, symptom diaries, wellness coachin apps) arenae regulatit as medical devices, yet nae common, structurit wey existit afore tae assess or compare their basic quality an safety.

## Why it maitters

App stores host hunders o thoosands o health an wellness apps o enormously variable quality, an afore a common technical specification existit, a patient, clinician, or health system hid nae structurit, comparable wey tae judge ae app's basic quality an safety again anither ayont star ratings an marketin claims — a gap that maitters because a puirly designit health app can still dae real hairm (inaccurate content, puir data security, misleadin claims) even wioot meetin the regulatory threshold o a medical device. ISO/TS 82304-2 is deliberately structurit roond domains a non-specialist reviewer can assess consistently, which haes made it the technical basis for several national an commercial health app quality-labellin an curation services, giein health systems an app libraries a defensible, standardised wey tae include or exclude apps frae a recommendit list raither than relyin on ad hoc judgement.

## Hoo it's applied

```
Assessment is organized around defined quality domains, evaluated
by structured review rather than a single numeric formula:

Usability                        — clarity, accessibility, and ease
                                    of use for the intended user group
Technical robustness/reliability — stability, performance, and
                                    freedom from technical defects
Interoperability                 — ability to exchange data with other
                                    systems where relevant to the app's
                                    function
Content quality and safety       — accuracy, currency, and absence of
                                    harmful or misleading health claims
Security and privacy             — data protection practice and
                                    transparency about data use

Each domain is scored via structured review criteria and combined
into an overall quality assessment, which several health app
quality-labelling schemes use as the technical basis for a public
quality label or curated-library inclusion decision.
```

## Warked example

A health system's digital app library programme wants tae curate a recommendit list o wellness apps for patients raither than leavin app selection entirely tae app-store search. Ilka candidate app is assessit again the ISO/TS 82304-2 domains: a sleep-trackin app scores weel on usability an technical robustness, adequately on content quality, but is flaggit durin the security an privacy review for sharin user data wi third-pairty advertisers wioot clear disclosure — a finding significant enough tae exclude the app frae the recommendit list despite its itherwise strang usability score. This domain-bi-domain outcome is mair actionable for baith the curation team an, if shared, the app's ain developer than a single blendit quality score wid be, sin it identifies precisely which aspect needs remedyin afore the app could be reconsiderit.

## Data sources an caveats

Assessment again ISO/TS 82304-2 is typically cairriet oot bi a trained reviewer or an accreditit assessment service, follaein the specification's structurit review criteria for ilka domain, an several national an commercial initiatives (health app quality-labellin an curation organizations, some wirkin unner formal national health system endorsement) uise the standard as the technical basis for their ain public-facin app quality labels — meanin an app's "certifiet" or "labelled" status in practice aften reflects a specific labellin scheme's implementation o the standard, no necessarily an identical process athort ilka scheme, sae the specific assessin organization an its methodology shuid be checkit an disclosed alangside ony quality label citit. The specification assesses the quality an basic safety characteristics o an app as software; it's nae a substitute for medical device regulatory clearance whaur an app's claims or functions actually meet a medical device threshold, an uisin it as ane wid be a category mistak.

## Pitfalls

- **Takkin a quality label as regulatory clearance**: an app assessit an labelled unner ISO/TS 82304-2 haesnae thereby gotten medical device regulatory approval; the twa serve different purposes an shuid niver be mixed up in hoo an app is described or merketit.
- **Assumin aa labellin schemes based on the standard are equivalent**: different organizations implement ISO/TS 82304-2-based assessment wi their ain specific review processes an rigour; check which organization did an assessment an hoo, raither than takkin ony "ISO/TS 82304-2-based" label as interchangeable wi ony ither.
- **Assessin only usability while neglectin security an privacy**: usability issues are the maist veesible tae an end user an the easiest tae assess informally, which can lead reviewers tae under-weicht the less veesible but potentially mair consequential security an privacy domain.
- **Takkin the assessment as a yin-aff, permanent certification**: an app's content, security practices, an third-pairty data-sharin arrangements can aa chynge efter an initial assessment; a credible quality-labellin programme re-assesses periodically raither than takkin an initial pass as permanent.
