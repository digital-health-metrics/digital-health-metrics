# Digital Health Metrics

Reference book of digital health metric definitions: wetin each metric mean, how to calculate am, worked example, data sources and caveats, and common pitfalls, dem organize am for people wey dey build, run, and evaluate digital health products and services.

You be new here? Start with [patient portal adoption rate](topiks/rate-wey-patient-dey-use-portal/) and [appointment no-show rate](topiks/rate-wey-patient-no-show-for-appointment/) — two metrics wey dey show up for almost every digital health business case.

## Patient engagement and access

- [Patient portal adoption rate](topiks/rate-wey-patient-dey-use-portal/) — registration, activation, and active use; three different rates wey people dey mix up as one too often
- [Telehealth visit rate](topiks/rate-of-telehealth-visit/) — di share of care wey dem deliver remotely, and why dem no suppose ever report video and telephone as one number
- [Appointment no-show rate](topiks/rate-wey-patient-no-show-for-appointment/) — di oldest operational metric for healthcare, and one of di best-evidenced targets for digital reminders
- [Patient engagement consistency rate](topiks/rate-of-patient-engagement-consistency/) — how regularly patient dey interact with digital tool over time, as different from whether dem don use am at all
- [User retention rate](topiks/rate-of-user-retention/) — di cohort retention curve wey dey separate product wey get sustainable use pattern from one wey dey ride wave of novelty
- [DAU/MAU stickiness ratio](topiks/dau-mau-stik-reshio/) — di standard product-analytics measure of engagement intensity across whole user base
- [Patient Net Promoter Score](topiks/pesho-net-promota-skor/) — di widely used, and widely criticized, single-question satisfaction metric
- [System Usability Scale score](topiks/sistem-yuzabiliti-skel-skor/) — standardized 10-item questionnaire wey dey quantify how usable software actually be

## Digital care operations and safety

- [Clinical alert override rate](topiks/rate-wey-dem-override-clinical-alert/) — di standard signal for alert fatigue for clinical decision support
- [Digital referral turnaround time](topiks/time-wey-digital-referral-take/) — di process metric wey dey show whether e-referral system dey actually save time
- [Time to intervention](topiks/time-to-intervention/) — how quickly clinical team dey respond to automated health alert
- [Bed-day reduction](topiks/how-bed-day-don-reduce/) — inpatient bed days wey dem save by moving recovery go virtual ward, wey dem always report together with safety metric

## Clinical outcomes and quality

- [Biometric improvement rate](topiks/rate-of-biometric-improvement/) — di share of patients wey achieve clinically meaningful change for tracked biometric like HbA1c or BMI
- [Biometric stabilization rate](topiks/rate-of-biometric-stabilization/) — sustained control inside target range, as different from one-off improvement
- [Triage routing accuracy](topiks/triaj-routin-akyureshi/) — whether AI or digital triage tool correctly route patient to di right level of care
- [Medication adherence rate](topiks/rate-of-medication-adherence/) — di proportion of days wey patient get access to dia medication as dem prescribe am
- [Hospital readmission rate](topiks/rate-of-hospital-readmission/) — di metric wey tie most directly to payer economics and value-based care contracts
- [ePROM completion rate](topiks/rate-of-eprom-completion/) — di completion rate for electronic patient-reported outcome measures, and why declining rate fit itself be clinical signal

## Marketing and growth economics

- [True customer acquisition cost](topiks/tru-kostoma-akwayshon-kos/) — di fully loaded cost to acquire one new patient, wey people commonly undercount by 30-50%
- [LTV to CAC ratio](topiks/ltv-to-cac-reshio/) — lifetime value against true acquisition cost; 3:1 na di widely cited sustainable baseline
- [Marketing efficiency ratio](topiks/makertin-efishensi-reshio/) — total revenue over total marketing spend, independent check against platform-reported ROAS

## Digital health equity

- [Digital access rate](topiks/rate-of-digital-access/) — di precondition metric for every other digital health measure for dis book
- [Digital literacy rate](topiks/rate-of-digital-literacy/) — whether patients wey get access fit actually use am unaided

## Technical and operational infrastructure

- [Physician burnout rate](topiks/rate-of-doctor-burnout/) — dem dey track am together with clinician-facing digital tool burden, because badly designed software na documented contributor
- [Device uptime rate](topiks/rate-wey-device-dey-work/) — di foundational infrastructure metric wey dey underneath every remote monitoring programme

## Financial and economic value

- [Cost per episode of care](topiks/cost-for-one-episode-of-care/) — di standard unit of financial comparison for value-based care contracts
- [Emergency department diversion rate](topiks/rate-of-ed-diversion/) — patient contacts wey dem safely redirect away from di ED, wey dem always report together with missed-emergency safety metric
- [Return on investment (ROI) and value on investment (VOI)](topiks/roi-an-voi/) — whether digital investment pay for itself financially, and whether e worth to do if we account for everything wey matter

## Evaluation frameworks

- [RE-AIM framework](topiks/re-aim-framework/) — Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, and Maintenance; five dimensions instead of one metric
- [WHO Digital Health Assessment Framework](topiks/who-dijital-helt-asesment-framework/) — WHO guidance to assess feasibility, safety, and equity across national-scale deployments
- [ISO/TS 82304-2](topiks/iso-ts-82304-2/) — di international technical specification wey dey behind most health and wellness app quality labels
