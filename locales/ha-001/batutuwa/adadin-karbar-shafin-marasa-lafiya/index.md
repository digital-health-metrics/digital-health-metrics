# Adadin Karɓar Shafin Marasa Lafiya

Adadin karɓar shafin marasa lafiya yana auna kaso na marasa lafiyar da suka cancanta waɗanda suka yi rajista, kuma suke amfani da gaske, da shafin marasa lafiya na kan layi (misali NHS App, Patient Access, ko shafin da aka haɗa da EHR kamar MyChart) don duba bayanai, ba da alƙawari, ko aika saƙo ga ƙungiyar kulawarsu. Shi ne alamar matakin shiga ta hulɗar dijital: mara lafiyar da bai taɓa kunna asusu ba ba zai iya amfana daga kowane sabis na dijital da aka gina a kan shafin ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Shafi yana ƙirƙirar daraja ne kawai da zarar mara lafiya ya yi amfani da shi, don haka ya kamata ƙungiyoyi su bibiyi karɓa a matsayin mazurari maimakon adadi ɗaya: rajista, kunnawa (aiki na farko mai ma'ana), da amfani mai aiki (amfani a cikin taga ta baya) adadi uku ne daban-daban da ake rikita su sau da yawa. Ana yawan matsa wa ƙungiyoyin sabis na dijital su bayar da rahoton adadi ɗaya, mai kyau na kanun labarai, kuma yana buƙatar ladabi don dagewa kan rarrabuwa mafi wahala, mafi gaskiya. Ƙarancin karɓa ko wanda ba a rarraba daidai ba kuma alamar daidaito ce: marasa lafiya tsofaffi, masu ƙarancin ilimin dijital, waɗanda ba sa jin harshen mafi rinjaye, ko ba su da amintaccen broadband ko wayar hannu mai wayo ba su da yuwuwar a ƙidaya su a mai lissafi a tsari, don haka ƙaruwar matsakaicin adadin karɓa na iya ɓoye faɗaɗar giɓi ga marasa lafiyar da galibi suka fi buƙatar hulɗa da ayyuka.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

A bayar da rahoton duka matakai uku, ba rajista kaɗai ba, kuma a bayyana mai raba a fili koyaushe:

```
Registration rate = patients with a portal account created / eligible patient population × 100
Activation rate   = patients who completed a first meaningful action (viewed a result,
                     booked a slot, sent a message) / patients with an account × 100
Active-use rate   = patients who logged in at least once in the trailing 12 months /
                     eligible patient population × 100
```

Yawanci ana ayyana yawan marasa lafiyar da suka cancanta a matsayin marasa lafiya masu aƙalla ganawa ɗaya da ƙungiyar a cikin lokacin duba baya da aka ayyana (galibi watanni 24), waɗanda suka kai shekaru da matsayin yarda da aka yarda su riƙe asusunsu.

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wata hanyar sadarwa ta kulawar farko tana hidimtawa marasa lafiya 50,000 da suka cika ma'anar cancanta. Daga cikinsu, 32,000 sun yi rajista a shafin (adadin rajista 64%). Daga cikin rajista 32,000, 27,000 sun kammala aƙalla aiki ɗaya mai ma'ana kamar duba sakamakon gwaji (adadin kunnawa 84% na masu rajista). A cikin watanni 12 da suka gabata, 21,000 daga cikin 50,000 na farko da suka cancanta sun shiga aƙalla sau ɗaya (adadin amfani mai aiki 42%). Bayar da rahoton adadin rajista na 64% kaɗai zai ƙara hulɗa ta gaske sosai; adadin amfani mai aiki na 42% shi ne lambar da ya kamata ta jagoranci shawarwarin rarraba albarkatu na shirin shafin.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Nazarin shafi yawanci yana zuwa daga dandalin mai siyarwa kansa (abubuwan shiga, amfani da fasali) ko daga rajistar bincike na tarihin lafiya na lantarki da ke ƙasa, kuma ya kamata ƙungiyoyi su yi shakku kan allunan mai siyarwa da ke nuna ƙididdigar rajista kaɗai. Ya kamata a yi wa shiga ta wakili (iyaye ko mai kulawa da ke sarrafa asusu a madadin mara lafiya) alama a kuma bayar da rahotonta daban, domin yana canza wanene "mai amfani" a zahiri. Zaɓin mai raba yana da muhimmanci sosai: ƙidaya a kan jimillar jerin marasa lafiya da aka yi wa rajista maimakon yawan jama'a masu cancanta da za a iya tuntuɓa da gaske koyaushe zai ƙididdige karɓa ƙasa, yayin da ƙidaya a kan marasa lafiyar da aka gayyata a ƙwazo kaɗai koyaushe zai ƙara shi, don haka ya kamata a gyara ma'anar cancanta a kuma buga ta tare da kowane adadi da aka bayar da rahoto.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ƙidaya rajista a matsayin karɓa**: asusun da aka ƙirƙira amma ba a taɓa amfani da shi ba ba shi da daraja kusan; a bayar da rahoton kunnawa da amfani mai aiki tare da rajista, ba maimakonsa ba.
- **Yin watsi da keɓancewar dijital**: adadin karɓa na haɗaka na iya ƙaruwa yayin da giɓi tsakanin ƙungiyoyin da aka fi kuma aka fi ƙarancin haɗa su a dijital ke faɗaɗa; a rarraba koyaushe ta shekaru, talauci, harshe, da nakasa inda gudanar da bayanai ya ba da dama.
- **Kwatanta ƙungiyoyi masu ma'anonin cancanta daban-daban**: shirin shafi da ke gayyatar marasa lafiya masu adireshin imel da aka rubuta kawai zai bayar da rahoton adadi mafi girma fiye da wanda ke auna a kan duka jerin da aka yi wa rajista, ba tare da bambancin aiki na gaske ba.
- **Ɗaukar shiga na sau ɗaya a matsayin hulɗa mai ci gaba**: duba baya na watanni 12 ya zama ruwan dare, amma gajeriyar taga (misali kwanaki 90) tana ba da gargaɗi na farko na raguwar amfani.

## Tushe

- NHS England, ƙididdigar amfani da rajista na NHS App (wallafe-wallafen nhs.uk / digital.nhs.uk)
- ONC / HealthIT.gov, ma'aunin Promoting Interoperability Program, ciki har da ma'aunin samun damar marasa lafiya na View, Download, Transmit (VDT)
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan karɓar shafin marasa lafiya da bambance-bambancen lafiyar dijital, misali nazarin da aka buga a Journal of the American Medical Informatics Association (JAMIA)

Duba kuma: [adadin rashin zuwa alƙawari](../adadin-rashin-zuwa-alkawari/), wanda tsara alƙawari da kai ta shafi da tunatarwa ke shafa kai tsaye.
