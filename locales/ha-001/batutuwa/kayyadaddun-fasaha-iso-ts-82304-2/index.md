# ISO/TS 82304-2

ISO/TS 82304-2 ƙayyadaddun fasaha ne na ƙasa da ƙasa, da aka buga ƙarƙashin ISO Technical Committee 215 (Health Informatics), wanda ke ayyana hanya mai tsari don tantance ingancin manhajojin lafiya da jin daɗi — wanda ya ƙunshi saukin amfani, ƙarfin fasaha da aminci, haɗin kai, ingancin abun ciki, da tsaron bayanai da sirri — ga samfuran da ba su shiga cikin faɗin cikakken ka'idar na'urorin lafiya ba amma har yanzu suna shafar shawarwari ko halayyar mai amfani game da lafiyarsa sosai. Yana wanzuwa don cike takamaiman giɓi: yawancin manhajojin lafiya da jin daɗi da ake nufi ga masu amfani (masu bibiyar motsa jiki, littattafan alamomin cuta, manhajojin horon jin daɗi) ba a ka'ida su a matsayin na'urorin lafiya ba, amma babu wata hanya ta gama gari, mai tsari da ta wanzu a baya don tantancewa ko kwatanta ingancinsu na tushe da amincinsu.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Shagunan manhaja suna ɗauke da ɗaruruwan dubban manhajojin lafiya da jin daɗi masu bambancin inganci sosai, kuma kafin wani ƙayyadaddun fasaha na gama gari ya wanzu, mara lafiya, likita, ko tsarin kiwon lafiya ba shi da wata hanya mai tsari, wadda za a iya kwatantawa don yin hukunci kan ingancin tushe da amincin manhaja ɗaya idan aka kwatanta da wata fiye da taurarin kimantawa da iƙirarin tallace-tallace — giɓi mai muhimmanci domin manhajar lafiya da aka ƙera ba daidai ba na iya haifar da cutarwa ta gaske (abun ciki mara daidai, rashin tsaron bayanai, iƙirari masu ruɗarwa) ko da ba ta cika ƙofar ka'ida ta na'urar lafiya ba. An tsara ISO/TS 82304-2 da gangan a kewayen fannoni da mai bita wanda ba ƙwararre ba zai iya tantancewa daidai, wanda ya sa ya zama tushen fasaha na wasu hidimomin lakabin ingancin manhajar lafiya da tsarawa na ƙasa da na kasuwanci, yana ba tsarin kiwon lafiya da ɗakunan karatun manhaja hanya mai kariya, ta daidaito, ta haɗawa ko cire manhajoji daga jerin da aka ba da shawara maimakon dogara da hukunci na kai-tsaye.

## Yadda ake aiwatar da shi

```
Assessment is organized around defined quality domains, evaluated
by structured review rather than a single numeric formula:

Usability                        — clarity, accessibility, and ease
                                    of use for the intended user group
Technical robustness/reliability — stability, performance, and
                                    freedom from technical defects
Interoperability                 — ability to exchange data with other
                                    systems where relevant to the app's
                                    function
Content quality and safety       — accuracy, currency, and absence of
                                    harmful or misleading health claims
Security and privacy             — data protection practice and
                                    transparency about data use

Each domain is scored via structured review criteria and combined
into an overall quality assessment, which several health app
quality-labelling schemes use as the technical basis for a public
quality label or curated-library inclusion decision.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Shirin ɗakin karatun manhaja na dijital na wani tsarin kiwon lafiya yana son tsara jerin manhajojin jin daɗi da aka ba da shawara ga marasa lafiya maimakon barin zaɓin manhaja gaba ɗaya ga binciken shagon manhaja. Ana tantance kowace manhaja ta ɗan takara bisa fannonin ISO/TS 82304-2: manhajar bibiyar barci tana samun maki mai kyau a saukin amfani da ƙarfin fasaha, isasshe a ingancin abun ciki, amma an yi mata alama yayin bitar tsaro da sirri saboda raba bayanan mai amfani da masu talla na ɓangare na uku ba tare da bayyanawa a fili ba — binciken da ya isa ya cire manhajar daga jerin da aka ba da shawara duk da makin saukin amfaninta mai ƙarfi. Wannan sakamakon fanni-da-fanni ya fi dacewa da aiki ga ƙungiyar tsarawa da, idan an raba, mai haɓaka manhajar kansa fiye da makin inganci na haɗaka ɗaya, domin yana gano daidai wane ɓangare ne ke buƙatar gyara kafin a sake la'akari da manhajar.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Yawanci mai bita da aka horar ko hidimar tantancewa da aka amince da ita ne ke gudanar da tantancewa bisa ISO/TS 82304-2, bin ma'aunin bitar da aka tsara na ƙayyadaddun ga kowane fanni, kuma wasu shirye-shirye na ƙasa da na kasuwanci (ƙungiyoyin lakabin ingancin manhajar lafiya da tsarawa, wasu suna aiki ƙarƙashin amincewar tsarin kiwon lafiya na ƙasa a hukumance) suna amfani da ma'aunin a matsayin tushen fasaha don lakabin ingancin manhaja nasu na jama'a — ma'ana matsayin manhaja na "an tabbatar" ko "an yi mata lakabi" a aikace galibi yana nuna aiwatar da wani takamaiman tsarin lakabi na ma'aunin, ba lallai ne tsari iri ɗaya a kowane tsari ba, don haka ya kamata a duba takamaiman ƙungiyar tantancewa da hanyarta a kuma bayyana su tare da kowane lakabin inganci da aka ambata. Ƙayyadaddun yana tantance halayen inganci da aminci na tushe na manhaja a matsayin software; ba madadin amincewar ka'idar na'urar lafiya ba ne inda iƙirari ko ayyukan manhaja suka cika ƙofar na'urar lafiya da gaske, kuma amfani da shi a matsayin hakan zai zama kuskuren rukuni.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ɗaukar lakabin inganci a matsayin amincewar ka'ida**: manhajar da aka tantance aka kuma yi wa lakabi ƙarƙashin ISO/TS 82304-2 ba ta sami amincewar ka'idar na'urar lafiya ta haka ba; su biyun suna da dalilai daban-daban kuma kada a taɓa rikita su a yadda ake bayyana ko tallata manhaja.
- **Zaton duk tsare-tsaren lakabi bisa ma'aunin daidai suke**: ƙungiyoyi daban-daban suna aiwatar da tantancewa bisa ISO/TS 82304-2 da nasu hanyoyin bita da tsauri; a duba wace ƙungiya ce ta gudanar da tantancewa da yadda, maimakon ɗaukar kowane lakabi "bisa ISO/TS 82304-2" a matsayin mai musanyawa da kowane.
- **Tantance saukin amfani kaɗai tare da watsi da tsaro da sirri**: matsalolin saukin amfani su ne mafi bayyana ga mai amfani na ƙarshe kuma mafi sauƙin tantancewa a kai-tsaye, wanda zai iya sa masu bita su ba da ƙarancin nauyi ga fannin tsaro da sirri wanda ba shi da bayyana amma yana iya zama mai sakamako fiye.
- **Ɗaukar tantancewa a matsayin takardar shaida ta dindindin, ta sau ɗaya**: abun ciki na manhaja, ayyukan tsaro, da shirye-shiryen raba bayanai na ɓangare na uku duk na iya canzawa bayan tantancewa ta farko; shirin lakabin inganci mai aminci yana sake tantancewa lokaci-lokaci maimakon ɗaukar nasarar farko a matsayin ta dindindin.
