# Adadin Karkatar da Marasa Lafiya daga Ɗakin Gaggawa

Adadin karkatar da marasa lafiya daga ɗakin gaggawa (ED) yana auna kaso na hulɗar marasa lafiya da kayan aikin triage na dijital ko kulawar kama-da-wane ke sarrafawa waɗanda da yiwuwar za su haifar da ziyarar ED ba tare da wannan ɗan tsakani ba, amma a maimakon haka an sarrafa su lafiya ta hanyar ƙaramin mataki — shawarar kula da kai, alƙawarin kulawar farko, ko ziyarar kulawar gaggawa da aka tsara. Wani takamaiman ɓangare ne mai daraja mai girma na ingancin raba marasa lafiya ta triage (duba wannan batu) wanda ke mai da hankali gaba ɗaya kan guje wa amfani da ɗakin gaggawa, wanda shi ne sakamakon da ya fi alaƙa kai tsaye da kuɗin kiwon lafiya da sauƙin ƙarfin ED.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ɗakunan gaggawa suna cikin wuraren kulawa mafi tsada ga kowace ganawa kuma ana yawan amfani da su don matsalolin da za a iya sarrafa su lafiya a wani wuri, don haka ikon kayan aikin triage na dijital na karkatar da shari'o'in da suka dace daga ED cikin aminci yana ɗaya daga cikin mafi daraja a kasuwanci da aiki — kuma ɗaya daga cikin mafi sauƙin isarwa ga mai biyan kuɗi ko tsarin kiwon lafiya da ke tantance dawowar jarin kayan aikin. Amma karkatarwa tana da daraja ne kawai idan ta kasance lafiya: kayan aikin da ke karkatar da marasa lafiya da ƙarfi daga ED har ya rasa gaggawa na gaske ya inganta ɓangaren da bai dace ba na musayar gaba ɗaya, shi ya sa dole a bayar da rahoton adadin karkatar da ED koyaushe tare da ma'aunin aminci da ke bibiyar gaggawar da aka rasa ko aka jinkirta a tsakanin marasa lafiyar da aka karkatar, ba a bayar da rahotonsa shi kaɗai a matsayin nasarar inganci tsantsa ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
ED diversion rate = patient contacts safely redirected away from ED to
                     an appropriate lower-acuity pathway / total patient
                     contacts assessed as potentially ED-bound × 100

"Safely redirected" requires confirmation, via follow-up or linked
health record data, that the patient's condition did not in fact
require emergency care within a defined follow-up window (e.g. 72
hours) — a diversion decision is not validated as safe merely because
the patient did not immediately go to the ED afterward.

Report alongside:
  Missed-emergency rate = diverted patients who did require emergency
                           care within the follow-up window / total
                           diverted patients × 100
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani sabis na triage na dijital yana tantance hulɗar marasa lafiya 3,000 a wata da algorithm ɗinsa na asibiti ya ɗauka a matsayin masu yiwuwar zuwa ED idan ba a tsoma baki ba. Daga cikinsu, 1,800 an tura su zuwa ƙaramin mataki (adadin karkatarwa na 60%). Bibiyar ƙungiyar da aka karkatar a awanni 72 ta amfani da bayanan tarihin lafiya da aka haɗa ta gano cewa 45 daga cikin marasa lafiya 1,800 da aka karkatar sun je ED a cikin wannan taga (adadin gaggawar da aka rasa na 45 / 1,800 × 100 = 2.5%). Bayar da rahoton adadin karkatarwa na 60% ba tare da adadin gaggawar da aka rasa na 2.5% ba zai gabatar da rabin musayar aminci-inganci ne kaɗai wanda ke ƙayyade ko halayyar karkatarwar kayan aikin an daidaita ta yadda ya kamata.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Tabbatar da cewa mara lafiyar da aka karkatar bai buƙaci kulawar gaggawa ba daga baya ya dogara ne kan bayanan da aka haɗa — ko dai rajistar ED na tsarin kiwon lafiya ɗaya, musayar bayanan lafiya na yanki, ko kiran bibiya ko bincike na marasa lafiya mai tsari — kuma shirin karkatarwa da ke aiki ba tare da ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan tushen bayanai ba ba zai iya tabbatar da amincinsa na gaske ba, sai dai ya ɗauka bisa rashin ƙorafi. Adadin karkatarwa da ya dace da ƙimar gaggawar da aka rasa da za a yarda da ita shawarwarin manufofin asibiti ne, ba na ƙididdiga kawai ba, kuma ya kamata shugabancin asibiti ya kafa su da gangan maimakon a bar su su fito a matsayin sakamakon gefe na duk wata ƙofa da algorithm na triage ke amfani da ita ta tsohuwa. Ya kamata a bayar da rahoton adadin karkatarwa ta alamar cuta ko rukunin ƙorafi da aka gabatar, domin adadin karkatarwa da ya dace ya bambanta sosai ta yanayi (ƙaramin rauni da ciwon ƙirji suna buƙatar ƙofofin karkatarwa daban sosai).

## Kura-kurai

- **Bayar da rahoton adadin karkatarwa ba tare da ma'aunin aminci na gaggawar da aka rasa ba**: babban adadin karkatarwa da aka samu ta rashin triage na gaggawa na gaske ba nasara ba ne; dole ne a bayar da rahoton ma'aunin biyu tare koyaushe.
- **Zaton rashin ziyarar ED yana nufin karkatarwar ta kasance lafiya**: mara lafiya na iya zuwa ED na wani tsarin asibiti daban, wanda ba a haɗa ba, ko kuma yana iya samun sakamako mai cutarwa na gaske ba tare da zuwa kowane ED ba; a tabbatar da aminci ta bayanan da aka haɗa ko bibiya mai tsari, ba rashin ziyarar ED a tsarin ɗaya kaɗai ba.
- **Saita ƙofar karkatarwa don ƙara adadin karkatarwa kawai**: algorithm ko manufa da aka daidaita don ƙara karkatarwa ba tare da ƙuntatawar aminci da ta dace ba za ta musanya amincin marasa lafiya da adadin inganci mai kyan gani.
- **Haɗa adadin karkatarwa a kowane irin ƙorafi**: adadin karkatarwa da ya dace ya bambanta sosai ta ƙorafin da aka gabatar; adadin haɗaka ɗaya ba zai iya nuna ko kayan aikin yana aiki lafiya da inganci ga takamaiman yanayin da suka fi muhimmanci a asibiti ba.

## Tushe

- Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality (AHRQ), bincike kan amfani da ɗakin gaggawa da karkatar da wurin kulawa da ya dace
- NHS England, jagora kan NHS 111 da ma'aunin aminci da tasiri na triage na gaggawa na dijital
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan sakamakon karkatar da ED ta triage na dijital da kulawar kama-da-wane, misali nazarin da aka buga a Annals of Emergency Medicine da npj Digital Medicine

Duba kuma: [ingancin raba marasa lafiya ta triage](../ingancin-raba-marasa-lafiya-ta-triage/), ma'aunin daidaito mai faɗi wanda wannan ɓangare ne na musamman mai muhimmancin aminci.
