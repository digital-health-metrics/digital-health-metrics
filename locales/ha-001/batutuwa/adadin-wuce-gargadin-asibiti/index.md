# Adadin Wuce Gargaɗin Asibiti

Adadin wuce gargaɗin asibiti yana auna kaso na gargaɗin tallafin yanke shawara na asibiti (CDS), kamar gargaɗin mu'amalar magani da magani, gargaɗin rashin lafiyar jiki, da duba kewayen sashi da tsarin shigar da umarnin likita ta kwamfuta (CPOE) ke samarwa, waɗanda likita ke watsi da su ko ya wuce su maimakon ya yi aiki da su. Shi ne daidaitaccen alamar ƙididdiga da ake amfani da ita don ganowa da sarrafa "gajiyawar gargaɗi": halin da aka rubuta sosai na likitoci su saba da gargaɗi har ba sa jin su da zarar yawan gargaɗin marasa daraja ya yi yawa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Adadin wuce gargaɗin mu'amalar magani da aka buga yakan kama daga kusan rabi zuwa fiye da casa'in cikin ɗari, kuma babban adadi ba lallai ne gazawar aminci ba ce: yawancin gargaɗin da ke katse aiki suna fitowa ne don mu'amalar da ba ta da muhimmanci a asibiti a cikin yanayi, ko kuma suna maimaita gargaɗin da likita ya riga ya yi aiki da shi a baya a cikin rukunin umarni ɗaya, don haka tsarin da aka daidaita da kyau da gangan yana fitar da ƙananan gargaɗi masu daraja mafi girma maimakon ƙoƙarin saukar da adadin wucewa zuwa sifili. Abin da ke da muhimmanci da gaske ga aminci shi ne yanayin a tsawon lokaci, rarrabawa a matakan tsanani, da ko likitoci suna rubuta dalili idan sun wuce gargaɗi mai tsanani; ƙaruwar adadin wucewa kan mu'amaloli masu tsanani masu goyon bayan shaida damuwa ce ta gudanarwa ta gaske ko da matsakaicin duk gargaɗi yana kama da kwanciyar hankali.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Override rate = alerts overridden / total alerts fired × 100

Segment by:
  - severity tier (e.g. contraindicated, major, moderate)
  - alert type (drug-drug interaction, allergy, duplicate therapy, dose range)
  - whether an override reason was documented

A "documented override rate" tracks the share of overrides that carry a
recorded justification, which is a governance measure in its own right.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Tsarin CPOE na wani asibiti yana fitar da gargaɗin mu'amalar magani da magani 10,000 a wata, wanda aka wuce 8,700, wanda ke ba da adadin wucewa gaba ɗaya na 87%. Rarraba ta tsanani yana nuna cewa daga cikin gargaɗin "an hana amfani" 500, an wuce 60 (12%), yayin da daga cikin gargaɗin "matsakaici" 6,000, an wuce 5,700 (95%). Adadin matakin matsakaici ya yi daidai da ma'aunin da aka buga kuma ba shi kaɗai ba dalili ne na damuwa; adadin matakin "an hana amfani" yana buƙatar nazarin shari'a ɗaya-ɗaya, kuma gaskiyar cewa 340 kawai daga cikin wucewa 500 a wannan mataki suna da dalilin da aka rubuta shi ne binciken gudanarwa da ya fi buƙatar mataki.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Rajistar bincike na tarihin lafiya na lantarki, ko tsarin gargaɗin mai samar da CDS kansa, yana rubuta kowane abin da ya faru na gargaɗi da aka fitar da amsar gargaɗi, ciki har da ko likita ya shigar da hujja ta rubutu kyauta ko ta tsari. Kwatanta adadin wucewa tsakanin ƙungiyoyi, ko ma tsakanin sassa a ƙungiya ɗaya, yana buƙatar duba cewa ƙa'idodin gargaɗi da rarraba tsanani da ke ƙasa iri ɗaya ne; asibitin da ke da tsarin ƙa'idoji da aka daidaita sosai zai nuna ƙaramin adadin wucewa saboda dalilai da ba su da alaƙa da halayyar likitoci.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ɗaukar ɗanyen adadin wucewa a matsayin makin aminci ɗaya**: yana haɗa wucewa mai hujja mai kyau na gargaɗi marasa daraja da wucewa marasa aminci na mu'amaloli masu haɗari da gaske; koyaushe a rarraba ta tsanani.
- **Rashin ɗaukar dalilin wucewa**: ba tare da dalili da aka rubuta ba, ba shi yiwuwa a bambanta "wannan gargaɗin kuskure ne" da "wannan gargaɗin daidai ne kuma likita ya yi hukunci mara aminci", wanda shi ne ainihin bambancin da ke da muhimmanci ga amincin marasa lafiya.
- **Kumbura ƙa'idodin gargaɗi a tsawon lokaci**: ƙara gargaɗi don "a kasance cikin aminci" ba tare da yankewa marasa daraja ba shi ne sanadin kai tsaye na ƙaruwar adadin wucewa da gajiyawar gargaɗi; ya kamata gudanar da gargaɗi ya haɗa da bita akai-akai da kawar da ƙa'idodin da ba sa aiki sosai, ba kawai sa ido ba.
- **Kwatanta adadi tsakanin tsare-tsare masu ƙirar katsewa daban-daban**: gargaɗi mai katsewa na tilas yana haifar da halayyar wucewa daban da na wanda ba ya toshewa, don haka su biyun ba ma'auni ne da za a iya kwatantawa kai tsaye ba.

## Tushe

- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan gajiyawar gargaɗin tallafin yanke shawara na asibiti, waɗanda aka buga sosai a mujallu ciki har da JAMIA da npj Digital Medicine
- ONC / HealthIT.gov, jagorar amincin fasahar lafiya kan tallafin yanke shawara na asibiti
- Institute for Safe Medication Practices (ISMP), jagora kan ƙirar gargaɗin CDS da gudanarwa
