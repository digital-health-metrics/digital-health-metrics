# Adadin Ziyarar Lafiya ta Nesa

Adadin ziyarar lafiya ta nesa (telehealth) shi ne kaso na jimillar ganawar sabis da ake isarwa daga nesa, ta bidiyo ko waya, maimakon ido-da-ido. Ma'aunin haɗin hanyar isarwa ne, ba ma'aunin aiki ba: yana gaya muku yadda ake isar da kulawa, wanda ke da muhimmanci ga shirin ƙarfi, samun dama, da dacewar asibiti, daban gaba ɗaya da yawan kulawar da ake isarwa gabaɗaya.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Yawan kulawar da ake isarwa daga nesa ya canza tsarin aiki na sabis da yawa bayan saurin faɗaɗa shawarwari na kama-da-wane a lokacin annobar COVID-19, kuma ƙungiyoyi suna buƙatar hanya mai ɗorewa don sa ido kan ko wannan sauyi yana ci gaba, yana komawa zuwa ƙa'idodin kafin annoba, ko ana jagorantarsa a ƙwazo ta manufa. Telehealth ba madadin ziyarar ido-da-ido iri ɗaya ba ne: dacewa tana bambanta ta ƙwarewa, ta nau'in shawara (duba magani yana aiki daban da jarrabawar jiki), da fifikon mara lafiya, don haka adadi "daidai" hukunci ne na asibiti da aiki, ba manufa da za a ƙara ba. Masu ba da kuɗi da masu ka'ida kuma suna amfani da wannan adadi, tare da ma'aunin sakamako da aminci, don yanke shawarar manufar biyan kuɗi da duba cewa ba a maye gurbin kulawa daga nesa kawai a kan shari'o'in da ke buƙatar a gansu ido-da-ido ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Telehealth visit rate = telehealth encounters / (telehealth encounters + in-person encounters) × 100

Report separately by modality where possible:
  Video rate     = video encounters / total encounters × 100
  Telephone rate = telephone-only encounters / total encounters × 100

Denominator should count completed encounters only (see pitfalls), for a
defined service, specialty, and time period.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani sabis na lafiyar hankali na al'umma yana rubuta hulɗar marasa lafiya na waje 4,000 da aka kammala a cikin kwata: 1,200 ido-da-ido, 1,600 ta bidiyo, da 1,200 ta waya. Adadin ziyarar telehealth shi ne (1,600 + 1,200) / 4,000 × 100 = 70%, tare da adadin bidiyo na 40% da adadin waya kaɗai na 30%. Bayar da rahoton adadin haɗaka na 70% kaɗai zai ɓoye cewa babban kaso na "telehealth" a nan sauti kaɗai ne, wanda yawanci yake ɗauke da bayanin haɗarin asibiti da ƙwarewar mara lafiya daban da bidiyo.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Yawanci ana rubuta nau'in ganawa ko dai a matsayin filin tsari a tarihin lafiya na lantarki (nau'in ziyara ko wuri) ko a ɗauka daga lambobin lissafin kuɗi, kamar lambar wurin sabis ko mai gyara telehealth a kan da'awa. Ayyukan lambobi sun bambanta sosai tsakanin ƙungiyoyi har ma tsakanin likitoci a ƙungiya ɗaya, don haka kwatancen adadi tsakanin wurare ya kamata ya fara tabbatar da cewa ana ba "telehealth" lamba iri ɗaya a kowane. Ziyarar da ta fara da bidiyo amma ta koma waya saboda matsalar fasaha ya kamata a ba ta lamba daidai gwargwado (galibi a matsayin hanyar da ta ɗauki yawancin abun cikin asibiti), kuma ya kamata a rubuta wannan ƙa'ida maimakon a bar ta ga hukuncin mutum ɗaya-ɗaya.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ƙidaya ziyarar da aka yi ƙoƙari maimakon waɗanda aka kammala**: alƙawarin telehealth da ya kasa haɗuwa aka sake ba shi ba zai ƙara mai raba telehealth sau biyu ba.
- **Ɗaukar bidiyo da waya a matsayin masu musanyawa**: suna da tasirin asibiti da na daidaito daban (waya ba ta da tantance gani amma ta fi sauƙin samu ga marasa lafiya marasa wayar hannu mai wayo, amintaccen bayanai, ko wuri na sirri don bidiyo); a bayar da rahotonsu daban koyaushe idan zai yiwu.
- **Yin watsi da alaƙar rashin zuwa**: halayyar rashin zuwa galibi tana bambanta ta hanyar shawara; duba [adadin rashin zuwa alƙawari](../adadin-rashin-zuwa-alkawari/) kafin a yanke ƙarshe game da "ingantaccen samun dama" daga ƙaruwar adadin telehealth kaɗai.
- **Ɗaukar babban adadi a matsayin mai kyau a kansa**: ga wasu yanayi da nau'ukan shawara, adadin telehealth da ya dace ƙarami ne ta ƙirar asibiti, ba ta gazawar balagar dijital ba.

## Tushe

- Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), bayanan amfani da telehealth na Medicare da wallafe-wallafen manufa
- NHS England, ƙididdigar ayyukan marasa lafiya na waje da na al'umma, ciki har da rarrabuwar halarta ta kama-da-wane/nesa
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan yanayin amfani da telehealth da sakamako na musamman ga hanya
