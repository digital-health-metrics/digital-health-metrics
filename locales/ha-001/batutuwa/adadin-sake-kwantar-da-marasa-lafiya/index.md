# Adadin Sake Kwantar da Marasa Lafiya

Adadin sake kwantar da marasa lafiya a asibiti shi ne kaso na marasa lafiyar da aka sallama waɗanda aka sake kwantar da su a asibiti, ba tare da shiri ba, a cikin lokacin da aka ayyana bayan sallama — galibi kwanaki 30. Ga lafiyar dijital, shi ne ma'aunin da ya fi dacewa kai tsaye da tattalin arzikin masu biyan kuɗi da kwangilolin kulawa bisa ƙima: shirin sa ido daga nesa, bibiyar bayan sallama, ko sauyawar kulawa ta dijital wanda ba zai iya nuna tasiri mai aminci kan sake kwantarwa ba ba zai yi yuwuwar samun tallafin biyan kuɗi mai ci gaba ba, komai kyawun adadin hulɗarsa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Sake kwantarwa ba tare da shiri ba yana da tsada, yana kawo cikas ga mara lafiya, kuma a yawancin tsarin kiwon lafiya yanzu ana hukunta shi kai tsaye: tsare-tsare kamar Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program na Amurka suna rage biyan kuɗi ga asibitoci masu adadin sake kwantarwa fiye da yadda ake tsammani ga takamaiman yanayi, shi ya sa asibitoci ke ba da umarni da gaske ga shirye-shiryen dijital na bayan sallama da sa ido daga nesa da aka nufa don rage su. Wani muhimmin kaso na sake kwantarwa ana ɗaukarsu masu yiwuwar kauce musu — saboda rashin isassun umarnin sallama, alƙawuran bibiya da aka rasa, rashin fahimtar magani, ko ƙara muni na alamomi da ba a magance su ba wanda kyakkyawan matakin dijital zai iya kama da wuri — wanda shi ne ainihin giɓin da kayan aikin kulawa na sauyawa na dijital ke nufi. Ya kamata a karanta adadin sake kwantarwa koyaushe tare da haɗin shari'a: shirin da ke hidimtawa yawan jama'a masu rashin lafiya sosai, masu rikitarwa zai sami ƙarin adadin tushe a tsari fiye da wanda ke hidimtawa yawan jama'a masu lafiya, ba tare da la'akari da ingancin shirin ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
30-day readmission rate = unplanned readmissions within 30 days of discharge
                           / total index discharges × 100

Exclude from the numerator: planned readmissions (e.g. a scheduled
follow-up procedure), and transfers that are a continuation of the same
episode of care rather than a new admission.

Risk-adjust where possible, using an accepted case-mix or comorbidity
index, before comparing rates across different patient populations or
time periods.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani asibiti yana sallamar marasa lafiya 1,200 masu gazawar zuciya a cikin kwata. Daga cikinsu, 210 an sake kwantar da su a cikin kwanaki 30, wanda 15 sake kwantarwa ne da aka tsara don wani tsari da aka shirya kuma an cire su. Adadin sake kwantarwa na kwanaki 30 ba tare da shiri ba shi ne (210 − 15) / 1,200 × 100 = 16.25%. An gabatar da shirin sa ido daga nesa ga ƙaramin rukuni na 400 daga cikin waɗannan marasa lafiya (an zaɓa bisa haɗarin asibiti, ba bazuwar ba), kuma adadin sake kwantarwarsu ba tare da shiri ba shi ne 14%, idan aka kwatanta da 18% ga marasa lafiya 800 da ba a yi wa rajista ba. Saboda rajista ya dogara ne kan haɗarin asibiti maimakon rabon bazuwar, wannan bambanci shaida ce mai nuni ba mai tabbatarwa ba ta tasirin shirin, kuma ya kamata a fassara shi tare da nazarin daidaita haɗari maimakon a ɗauke shi kamar yadda yake.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Ana ɗaukar bayanan sake kwantarwa yawanci daga rafin shigar-sallama-sauya (ADT) na asibitin kansa don sake kwantarwa a wuri ɗaya, amma mara lafiyar da aka sake kwantarwa a wani asibiti daban ba zai bayyana a wannan rafin kwata-kwata ba, don haka bibiyar sake kwantarwa ta asibiti ɗaya tana ƙididdige ƙasa a tsari da ainihin adadin sake kwantarwa sai an ƙara mata bayanan musayar bayanan lafiya na yanki, bayanan da'awar masu biyan kuɗi, ko rumbun bayanan duk masu biyan kuɗi na matakin jiha. Danganta ga shirin dijital yana buƙatar kulawa: marasa lafiyar da suka zaɓi shiga shirin sa ido daga nesa na son rai ba kasafai suke zama samfurin bazuwar na yawan wadanda aka sallama ba, don haka kwatancen sauƙi na adadin sake kwantarwa na waɗanda aka yi wa rajista da waɗanda ba a yi ba zai kasance yana da rikici ta daidai tasirin zaɓi da ya sa wasu marasa lafiya suka fi yuwuwar yin rajista tun farko.

## Kura-kurai

- **Kwatanta adadi ɗanye, ba a daidaita haɗari ba, tsakanin yawan jama'a**: shirin da ke hidimtawa yawan jama'a masu rashin lafiya sosai zai nuna ƙarin adadin sake kwantarwa ɗanye fiye da wanda ke hidimtawa masu lafiya ko da shirin kansa ya fi inganci; koyaushe a daidaita haɗari kafin a kwatanta.
- **Ƙididdige ƙasa sake kwantarwa zuwa wasu wurare**: dogara da bayanan ADT na asibiti ɗaya kaɗai zai rasa sake kwantarwa a wani wuri, yana ƙididdige ƙasa da ainihin adadin, musamman a yankunan da ke da tsarin asibitoci masu gasa da yawa.
- **Son zuciya na zaɓi a rajistar shirin na son rai**: marasa lafiyar da suka zaɓi shiga shirin bibiya na dijital galibi sun bambanta a tsari (a ilimin lafiya, tallafin zamantakewa, ko himma) da waɗanda ba su shiga ba, wanda ke rikita duk wani kwatancen sauƙi na kafin/bayan ko na waɗanda aka yi wa rajista/waɗanda ba a yi ba.
- **Ƙidaya kowane dawowa wurin ɗaya a matsayin sake kwantarwa**: sake kwantarwa da aka tsara, da aka shirya (misali tsari na mataki na biyu da aka shirya) ba alamar gazawar sallama ba ce kuma ya kamata a cire shi daga mai lissafi, ba a haɗa shi da dawowa marasa shiri na gaske ba.
