# Tsarin RE-AIM

RE-AIM tsarin tantancewa ne na lafiyar jama'a wanda ke tantance ɗan tsakani na lafiyar dijital ta girma biyar daban-daban — Isa (Reach), Tasiri (Effectiveness), Karɓa (Adoption), Aiwatarwa (Implementation), da Dorewa (Maintenance) — maimakon ta kowane ma'auni ɗaya. Yana wanzuwa don gyara wani takamaiman kuskure na gama gari a tantance lafiyar dijital: shirin da ke bayar da rahoton bayanan tasiri masu burgewa daga ƙaramin rukunin gwaji mai himma amma ba a taɓa karɓarsa a babban sikeli ba, ko ba a ci gaba da shi ba bayan lokacin gwajinsa da aka ba da kuɗi, bai nuna ainihin darajar lafiyar jama'a ba, komai kyawun adadin sakamakonsa na kanun labarai idan an duba su shi kaɗai.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ana yawan tantance gwajin lafiyar dijital da tasiri kaɗai — shin ma'aunin sakamako ya inganta ga mutanen da suka yi amfani da shi — yayin da ake barin sauran girma huɗu ba a auna ko ba a bayar da rahoto ba, wanda ke karkatar da tushen shaida a tsari zuwa ayyukan da ke aiki sosai a yanayi mai kyau, ƙaramin sikeli amma ba sa faɗi komai game da ko suna aiki a babban sikeli, suna isa ga yawan jama'ar da suka fi buƙatarsu, ko suna rayuwa fiye da kuɗinsu na farko da himma. An haɓaka RE-AIM musamman don tilasta wa masu tantancewa su bayar da rahoto kan ingancin waje (shin wannan zai yi aiki a wani wuri, ga wane) tare da ingancin ciki (shin wannan ya yi aiki a nan), wanda yake da muhimmanci sosai ga duk wanda ke yanke shawarar ba da kuɗi, ba da umarni, ko faɗaɗa ɗan tsakani na lafiyar dijital bisa sakamakon nazari ko gwaji ɗaya. Domin kowane ɗayan girma biyar za a iya auna shi a matakin mutum ɗaya-ɗaya da kuma matakin ƙungiya ko wuri, RE-AIM kuma yana bayyana musayar — misali, ɗan tsakani mai tasiri sosai da ƙwararrun ma'aikata da aka zaɓa sosai ke isarwa (tasiri mai ƙarfi, wakilcin karɓa mai rauni) — wanda ma'auni na haɗaka ɗaya zai ɓoye gaba ɗaya.

## Yadda ake aiwatar da shi

```
Reach          = proportion of the eligible target population who
                  participate, and how representative participants are
                  of that eligible population (not just raw
                  participant count)

Effectiveness  = impact on primary outcomes, including quality-of-life
                  and unintended negative consequences, not only the
                  single headline outcome measure

Adoption       = proportion and representativeness of settings and
                  staff/delivery agents who adopt the intervention,
                  relative to all eligible settings and staff

Implementation = consistency and fidelity of delivery as intended,
                  time and cost required to deliver it, and adaptations
                  made along the way

Maintenance    = extent to which the intervention becomes part of
                  routine practice long-term, at both the individual
                  (sustained individual-level effects) and
                  setting/organizational (institutionalization) level
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

An gwada shirin rigakafin ciwon sukari na dijital a asibitocin kulawar farko 20 a yanki mai jimillar asibitoci 200 da suka cancanta. An tantance Isa a matsayin marasa lafiya 1,200 daga cikin ƙiyasin marasa lafiya 15,000 da suka cancanta a asibitocin gwaji 20 (8%), tare da gano cewa mahalarta sun ɗan ƙanƙanta kuma sun fi ilimin dijital fiye da cikakken yawan da suka cancanta — giɓin wakilci da ya cancanci a lura maimakon yin watsi da shi. Tasiri yana nuna raguwa mai muhimmanci a asibiti a matsakaicin HbA1c a tsakanin waɗanda suka kammala, amma babban adadin barin shirin yana nufin wannan tasiri ya raunana idan aka tantance shi bisa niyyar-magani a duk marasa lafiyar da aka yi wa rajista, ba waɗanda suka kammala kaɗai ba. Karɓa shi ne asibitoci 20 daga cikin 200 da suka cancanta (10%), waɗanda suka taru a asibitoci masu ingantattun kayayyakin dijital fiye da matsakaici — sake, damuwa ce ta wakilci. Tantance amincin aiwatarwa ya gano yawancin asibitoci sun isar da shirin kusan yadda aka ƙera shi, ko da yake asibitoci biyu sun rage tsarin shiga sosai a ƙarƙashin matsin lokaci. Tantance Dorewa shekara ɗaya bayan kuɗin gwajin ya ƙare ya gano asibitoci 6 kawai daga cikin 20 na asali ne suke ci gaba da aiwatar da shirin — sakamakon da, idan aka bayar da rahoto tare da adadin tasirin da ya yi alƙawari da farko, yana ba da hoto mafi cikakke kuma mafi gaskiya na yuwuwar shirin a zahiri fiye da bayanan tasiri kaɗai.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Kowane girma na RE-AIM yawanci yana ɗaukar tushen bayanai daban: Isa da Karɓa daga rajistar rajista da cancanta, Tasiri daga bayanan asibiti ko sakamako (galibi bayanan da aka riga aka yi amfani da su don lissafa takamaiman ma'auni a wani wuri a wannan littafi, kamar adadin inganta ma'aunin halittar jiki), Aiwatarwa daga jerin dubawa na aminci, rajistar isarwa, ko lura kai tsaye, da Dorewa daga tantancewar bibiya da aka gudanar bayan lokacin tantancewa na farko — ma'ana cikakken tantancewar RE-AIM ba za a iya kammala shi a lokaci ɗaya ba kuma yana buƙatar a tsara tantancewa tun daga farko, ba a ƙara shi a baya ba. Saboda bayar da rahoton duka girma biyar da gaskiya (ciki har da sakamako masu rauni a wasu) ya fi aiki kuma ba shi da kyau fiye da bayar da rahoton tasiri kaɗai, tantance lafiyar dijital da aka buga yana ƙarancin bayar da rahoton Isa, Karɓa, da Dorewa idan aka kwatanta da Tasiri — tsarin son zuciya na wallafawa da aka sani wanda duk wanda ke amfani da shaida mai lakabin RE-AIM don sanar da shawara ya kamata ya lura da shi.

## Kura-kurai

- **Bayar da rahoton tasiri kaɗai da kiran shi tantancewar RE-AIM**: amfani da sunan RE-AIM yayin da ake bayar da rahoton ɗaya kawai daga girma biyar yana kayar da manufar tsarin kuma bai kamata a siffanta shi a matsayin tantancewar RE-AIM ba.
- **Auna Isa da Karɓa a matsayin ƙidaya ɗanye maimakon kaso na yawan da suka cancanta**: babbar ƙidayar mahalarta ɗanya na iya wakiltar ƙaramin kaso mara wakilci na waɗanda ɗan tsakanin ya kamata ya isa gare su; a lissafa koyaushe a kan cikakken mai raba da ya cancanta.
- **Tantance Dorewa da wuri**: za a iya yin hukunci kan hukumantarwa da tasiri mai ɗorewa bayan kuɗin farko ko tasirin sabon abu ya ɓace; tantancewar da aka yi nan da nan bayan gwaji ya ƙare ba za ta iya magana kan Dorewa ba komai yadda sauran girma huɗu suke.
- **Ɗaukar girma biyar a matsayin masu nauyi iri ɗaya ko waɗanda za a iya haɗawa zuwa maki ɗaya**: RE-AIM da gangan bayanin girma da yawa ne, ba lamba ɗaya ba; haɗa girman zuwa maki mai haɗaka ɗaya yana watsar da daidai bayyanar musayar da aka ƙera tsarin don kiyayewa.
