# Adadin Kammala ePROM

Adadin kammala ePROM yana auna kaso na Ma'aunin Sakamakon da Marasa Lafiya ke Bayarwa ta Lantarki (ePROMs) da aka tsara — daidaitattun tambayoyin da aka tabbatar waɗanda ke ɗaukar labarin mara lafiya na kansa game da alamominsa, aikinsa, ko ingancin rayuwarsa, da ake isarwa ta dijital maimakon takarda — waɗanda aka kammala da gaske. Ma'aunin ingancin bayanai ne kamar yadda yake na hulɗa: darajar asibiti da ta bincike ta shirin PROM ta dogara gaba ɗaya kan samun adadin kammalawa mai isa don amsoshin da aka tattara su wakilci duka yawan marasa lafiyar da aka yi wa rajista, ba kawai ƙungiyar da ta fi hulɗa ko mafi ƙarancin alamomi ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Sakamakon da marasa lafiya ke bayarwa shi ne ƙarin kai tsaye, wanda mara lafiya ya shaida, na bayanan da likita ya rubuta ko na'ura ta auna, yana ɗaukar ɓangarorin lafiya — ciwo, aiki, ingancin rayuwa — da duba tarihi ko karatun ma'aunin halittar jiki ba zai iya ɗauka ba; dijital ɗin tattara PROM yana wanzuwa musamman don mai da wannan bayanai mai rahusa kuma sauƙi a tattara a babban sikeli fiye da yadda gudanar da takarda ta taɓa ba da dama. Amma shirin PROM mai ƙarancin adadin kammalawa yana haɗarin takamaiman son zuciya mai tsanani: marasa lafiyar da suke jin ba su da lafiya galibi ba su da yuwuwar kammala dogon tambayoyi, don haka raguwar adadin kammalawa kanta na iya zama alamar gargaɗi ta farko ta ƙara muni na lafiyar yawan jama'a, kuma ƙarancin adadin kammalawa gaba ɗaya na iya sa amsoshin da aka tattara su yi kama da mafi kyau fiye da ƙwarewar ainihin yawan jama'a kawai saboda ba a wakilci marasa lafiya masu alamomi sosai a abin da aka kammala. Shi ya sa ya kamata a bayar da rahoton adadin kammalawa koyaushe tare da makin PROM kansu, ba a ɗauke shi a matsayin ƙaramin dalla-dalla na aiki ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
ePROM completion rate = ePROMs fully completed / ePROMs sent or scheduled × 100

Report separately for:
  Initial completion rate     (first questionnaire in a monitoring
                               sequence)
  Longitudinal completion rate (subsequent questionnaires in an ongoing
                               monitoring sequence, which typically
                               declines over time and should be tracked
                               as a trend, not a single figure)

A "partially completed" questionnaire should be defined and reported
separately from both "fully completed" and "not started".
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani asibitin ciwon daji yana aika ePROM na nauyin alamomi da aka tabbatar ga marasa lafiya 400 kafin kowace ziyarar bibiya ta wata-wata. A wata na farko, marasa lafiya 340 sun kammala tambayoyin gaba ɗaya (adadin kammalawa 85%), 30 sun kammala wani ɓangare, kuma 30 ba su fara ba. A wata na shida na jerin sa ido iri ɗaya, cikakkun amsoshi sun faɗi zuwa 260 daga cikin ƙungiyar marasa lafiya 400 iri ɗaya (65%), raguwa mai ma'ana ta tsawon lokaci wadda za a rasa gaba ɗaya idan aka bayar da rahoton adadin 85% na watan farko kaɗai a matsayin ma'aunin gaba ɗaya na tsaye. Bincika waɗanne marasa lafiya ne ke tsayawa (ta tsananin alamomi, matakin cuta, ko shekaru) na iya bayyana ko raguwar tana nuna gajiyawar bincike, ƙara muni na alamomi da ke sa tambayoyin su yi wahalar kammalawa, ko shingen samun fasaha.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Bayanan kammalawa suna zuwa daga rajistar isarwa da amsa na dandalin ePROM kansa, wanda zai iya bambance yanayin "ba a fara ba", "an kammala wani ɓangare", da "an kammala gaba ɗaya" — bambancin da ya kamata a kiyaye a koyaushe kuma a bayar da rahoto maimakon rushewa zuwa adadi biyu na an kammala/ba a kammala ba, tun da kammalawa ta ɓangare galibi tana nuna takamaiman wuri a cikin tambayoyin da marasa lafiya ke wahala ko barin hulɗa. Ya kamata a fassara adadin kammalawa tare da yadda ake isar da tambayoyin (hanyar saƙon rubutu, sanarwar manhaja, ko hanyar isarwa da ke buƙatar shiga shafi), domin gogayyar isarwa kanta tana shafar kammalawa ba tare da la'akari da abun cikin tambayoyin ko ainihin yanayin mara lafiya ba. Ya kamata a yi amfani da kayan aiki da aka tabbatar (maimakon saitin tambayoyi na kai-tsaye) don PROM kansa koyaushe, tun da adadin kammalawa ga kayan aikin da ba a tabbatar ba ba ya faɗi komai mai aminci game da amfanin asibiti na bayanan da suka haifar ko da kammalawa ta yi yawa.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ɗaukar raguwar adadin kammalawa a matsayin matsalar isarwa kawai**: raguwa ta tsawon lokaci a kammalawa na iya nuna ƙara muni na alamomin marasa lafiya na gaske (marasa lafiya da ba su da lafiya da za su iya kammala bincike) maimakon gajiyawar bincike ko matsalar fasaha, kuma wannan bambanci yana da muhimmanci sosai ga fassarar asibiti.
- **Rushe kammalawa ta ɓangare da cikakkiya zuwa rukuni ɗaya**: tambayoyin da aka kammala wani ɓangare suna da ingancin bayanai daban sosai da waɗanda aka kammala gaba ɗaya; a bayar da rahoto daban, a kuma bincika inda a cikin tsarin tambayoyin marasa lafiya ke yawan barin shi.
- **Bayar da rahoton adadin kammalawa ba tare da bayar da rahoton haɗarin son zuciya na amsa ba**: matsakaicin adadin kammalawa ya kamata ya sa a bincika ko masu amsawa sun bambanta a tsari (a tsananin alamomi, shekaru, ilimin dijital) da waɗanda ba su amsa ba, tun da makin PROM da aka lissafa daga masu amsawa kawai na iya wakiltar yawan jama'a gaba ɗaya ba daidai ba.
- **Amfani da tambayoyi da ba a tabbatar ba ko na gida**: adadin kammalawa ba shi da ma'ana a matsayin alamar ingancin bayanai idan kayan aikin da ake kammalawa bai tabbatu a asibiti ba don yanayin da yawan jama'ar da ake aunawa.

## Tushe

- International Consortium for Health Outcomes Measurement (ICHOM), haɓaka saitin ma'auni da jagorar aiwatar da PROM
- U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), jagora kan ma'aunin sakamakon da marasa lafiya ke bayarwa a gwajin asibiti da gabatarwar tsari
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan aiwatar da PROM na lantarki da adadin kammalawa, misali nazarin da aka buga a Quality of Life Research da Journal of Medical Internet Research (JMIR)

Duba kuma: [makin Net Promoter na marasa lafiya](../makin-net-promoter-na-marasa-lafiya/), ma'auni mai alaƙa amma daban da marasa lafiya ke bayarwa wanda ke auna gamsuwa maimakon sakamakon asibiti.
