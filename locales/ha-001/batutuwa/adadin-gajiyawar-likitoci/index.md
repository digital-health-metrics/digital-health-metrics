# Adadin Gajiyawar Likitoci

Adadin gajiyawar likitoci yana auna kaso na likitocin da ke bayar da rahoton alamomin gajiyawa masu muhimmanci — galibi ana tantance su a matsayin gajiyawar motsin rai, rabuwa da kai, ko ƙarancin jin nasarar kai ta hanyar kayan aikin bincike da aka tabbatar — kuma, musamman ga lafiyar dijital, ana bibiyarsa tare da ma'aunin nauyin kayan aikin dijital da ke fuskantar likita kamar lokacin da ake kashewa kan takarda ko rubuta tarihin lafiya na lantarki (EHR). Yana cikin tsarin ma'aunin lafiyar dijital domin software na asibiti da aka ƙera ba daidai ba mai ba da gudummawa ne ga gajiyawa, wanda aka rubuta kuma ake iya aunawa, kuma bai kamata a taɓa tantance nasarar kayan aikin lafiyar dijital da ma'aunin da ke fuskantar marasa lafiya kaɗai ba tare da watsi da tasirinsa ga likitocin da dole su sarrafa shi ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ana gabatar da kayan aikin lafiyar dijital sau da yawa da bayyanannen manufar rage nauyin gudanarwa na likita, amma tsarin aiki na tarihin lafiya na lantarki da aka ƙera ba daidai ba, yawan gargaɗin asibiti marasa daraja (duba adadin wuce gargaɗin asibiti), ko mu'amalar telehealth mara kyau na iya ƙara gajiyawa kamar yadda zai iya rage ta — kuma kayan aikin da ke inganta ma'aunin hulɗar da ke fuskantar marasa lafiya yayin da yake ƙara nauyin rubutu na likita a ɓoye bai isar da sakamako mai kyau na net ga tsarin kulawa gaba ɗaya ba. An danganta gajiyawa sosai a cikin wallafe-wallafen asibiti da kurakuran likita, canjin ma'aikata, da raguwar ingancin kulawa, don haka tana aiki a matsayin alamar farko ta matsalolin aminci da dorewar ma'aikata na ƙasa, ba kawai wani ƙaramin al'amari na gamsuwar wurin aiki ba. Duk wani shirin lafiyar dijital da ke iƙirarin rage nauyin asibiti ya kamata ya iya nuna wannan iƙirari a kan ma'aunin tushe da aka auna, maimakon bayyana shi a matsayin niyyar ƙira.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Physician burnout rate = clinicians scoring above the validated instrument's
                          burnout threshold / total clinicians surveyed × 100

Common validated instruments: Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI), the
Professional Fulfillment Index, or a single-item burnout screening
question validated against a fuller instrument.

Report alongside a digital-burden proxy where available:
  EHR time-in-system per patient encounter
  Documentation time occurring outside scheduled clinical hours
  ("pajama time")
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani tsarin asibiti yana bincikar likitoci 300 ta amfani da Maslach Burnout Inventory kafin gabatar da kayan aikin rubutun asibiti na muhalli da aka nufa don rage lokacin rubuta bayanai. A matakin farko, likitoci 135 (45%) suna samun maki sama da ƙofar gajiyawa, kuma bayanan rajistar bincike na EHR sun nuna matsakaicin mintuna 58 na lokacin rubutu ga kowane likita da ke faruwa a wajen awanni na asibiti da aka tsara kowace rana. Watanni shida bayan fara amfani da kayan aikin, binciken maimaitawa na likitoci iri ɗaya ya gano 108 (36%) sama da ƙofar gajiyawa, tare da raguwar lokacin rubutu bayan awanni zuwa mintuna 34 kowace rana. Motsin da ke da alaƙa a duka adadin gajiyawa da wakili na haƙiƙa da aka samo daga EHR yana ƙarfafa hujjar cewa kayan aikin yana ba da gudummawa ga ingantuwa, ko da yake ya kamata kwatancen na kafin/bayan na hukuma ya yi la'akari da sauran canje-canjen nauyin aiki na lokaci ɗaya a cikin lokacin.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Bayanan binciken gajiyawa suna zuwa daga kayan aiki da aka tabbatar da ake gudanarwa akai-akai (kowace shekara ko fiye da haka), kuma adadin amsa yana da muhimmanci: ƙarancin adadin amsa yana haɗarin son zuciya na rashin amsa, inda likitocin da suka fi gajiyawa (waɗanda ke da mafi ƙarancin ƙarfin kammala ƙarin bincike) ake wakilta su da ƙasa a tsari, yana ƙididdige ainihin adadin ƙasa. Wakilai da aka samo daga EHR na nauyin dijital — lokaci a cikin tsari, lokacin rubutu bayan awanni, adadin dannawa ga kowace ganawa — suna da amfani a matsayin ƙari na haƙiƙa, da ake samu a ci gaba ga bayanan bincike na lokaci-lokaci, amma ya kamata a tabbatar da su a kan gajiyawar da aka bayar da rahoto a bincike ga ƙungiya da aka bayar kafin a ɗauke su a matsayin amintaccen alamar gajiyawa ta kansa, tun da alaƙar lokaci a cikin tsari da ainihin gajiyawa na iya bambanta ta ƙwarewa da salon aiki na mutum.

## Kura-kurai

- **Dogara da wakilai da aka samo daga EHR kaɗai**: lokaci a cikin tsari da ƙidayar dannawa suna da alaƙa da gajiyawa a haɗaka amma ba abu ɗaya da gajiyawa kanta ba ne, kuma suna iya ruɗarwa ga likitoci ɗaya-ɗaya ko ƙwarewa masu buƙatun rubutu daban na gaske.
- **Ƙarancin adadin amsa yana ɓoye ainihin adadin**: likitocin da gajiyawa ta fi shafa galibi su ne mafi ƙarancin ƙarfin amsa bincike na son rai, wanda ke karkatar da sakamakon ƙarancin adadin amsa zuwa adadi da ake ganin yana da lafiya fiye da yadda yake.
- **Danganta canjin gajiyawa ga kayan aiki ɗaya ba tare da la'akari da abubuwan rikici ba**: gajiyawa tana shafar abubuwa da yawa na lokaci ɗaya (matakan ma'aikata, yawan marasa lafiya, canjin ƙungiya); kwatancen kafin/bayan kewayen fara amfani da kayan aiki ɗaya ya kamata ya sarrafa waɗannan idan zai yiwu maimakon zaton sanadi ɗaya.
- **Ɗaukar gajiyawa a matsayin batun juriya ta mutum kawai**: binciken gajiyawa a koyaushe yana gano nauyin aiki, ƙirar tsari, da abubuwan ƙungiya a matsayin manyan masu haifarwa; tsara ta a matsayin matsalar likita ɗaya kaɗai yana karkatar da tsoma baki daga kayan aikin dijital da tsarin aiki da galibi su ne ainihin tushen dalili.

## Tushe

- Maslach Burnout Inventory (MBI), kayan aikin bincike da aka tabbatar da jagorar bayar da maki
- American Medical Association (AMA), binciken gajiyawar likitoci da shirin inganta aiki na STEPS Forward
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan saukin amfani da EHR, nauyin rubutu, da gajiyawar likitoci, misali nazarin da aka buga a JAMIA da Annals of Internal Medicine

Duba kuma: [adadin wuce gargaɗin asibiti](../adadin-wuce-gargadin-asibiti/), gajiyawar gargaɗi kasancewa ɗaya daga cikin masu ba da gudummawa na musamman, masu aunuwa ga gajiyawar likitoci da kayan aikin dijital za su iya magancewa kai tsaye.
