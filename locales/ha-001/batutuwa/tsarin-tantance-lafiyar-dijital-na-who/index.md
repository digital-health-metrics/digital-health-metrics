# Tsarin Tantance Lafiyar Dijital na WHO

Hukumar Lafiya ta Duniya (WHO) tana buga tarin jagora don tantance ɗan tsakani na lafiyar dijital kafin, lokacin, da bayan turawa a matakin ƙasa, wanda ya ƙunshi hanyar sa ido da tantancewa, daidaitaccen rarrabuwar nau'ukan ɗan tsakani, da kayan aiki don yin hukunci ko shirin lafiyar dijital a shirye yake don faɗaɗawa — tare da musamman kuma daidaitaccen girmamawa kan daidaito, yiwuwar aiwatarwa, da aminci, yana nuna wa'adin WHO na kare lafiyar jama'a a ƙasashe masu bambancin kayayyakin more rayuwa na dijital da na tsarin lafiya sosai. Ba kamar jerin dubawa guda ɗaya mai suna ba, ya fi dacewa a fahimce shi a matsayin iyali mai daidaito na wallafe-wallafen WHO waɗanda ƙungiyoyin da ke tantance turawar lafiyar dijital ta ƙasa ko yanki yawanci suke ɗauka tare.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ɗan tsakani na lafiyar dijital da ke aiki da kyau a yanayin gwaji mai wadatar albarkatu na iya kasawa, ko ya haifar da cutarwa a ƙwazo, idan aka tura shi a ƙasa a cikin yawan jama'a masu bambancin matakan haɗi, ilimin dijital, harshe, da ƙarfin tsarin lafiya sosai — wanda shi ne daidai giɓin da jagorar WHO ke wanzuwa don rufewa, tun da babban wa'adin WHO shi ne daidaiton lafiyar jama'a a ƙasashe membobi masu bambancin albarkatu sosai, ba muradun kowane tsarin kiwon lafiya ko mai siyarwa ɗaya ba. Tsarin WHO kuma yana ɗaukar aminci da yiwuwar aiwatarwa a matsayin la'akari na shinge, ba tunani na baya da za a tantance da zarar ɗan tsakani ya riga ya yi aiki ba: dole a nuna cewa kayan aikin lafiyar dijital ba ya ƙara rashin daidaito ko gabatar da sabon haɗarin aminci kafin a yi la'akari da faɗaɗawa, ba kawai a nuna yana da tasiri gaba ɗaya ba. Ga kowace ƙungiya da ke shirin turawar lafiyar dijital ta ƙasa ko ƙasashe da yawa, daidaita tantancewa da jagorar WHO galibi kuma larura ce ta aiki, tun da shi ne tsarin da ma'aikatun lafiya da masu ba da kuɗi na ƙasashe da yawa suka fi yuwuwar ambata ko buƙata.

## Yadda ake aiwatar da shi

```
WHO guidance is applied across several linked evaluation activities,
not one single scored checklist:

Classification    — categorizing the intervention against WHO's
                     Classification of Digital Health Interventions,
                     to ensure like-for-like comparison with similar
                     interventions elsewhere

Monitoring and
evaluation design — following WHO's monitoring and evaluation guidance
                     for digital health interventions, covering study
                     design, outcome selection, and data quality

Scale-readiness
assessment        — assessing groundwork, partnerships, financial
                     sustainability, technical architecture, and
                     operational capacity against WHO/ITU-aligned
                     scale-up assessment toolkits

Equity and safety
review            — an explicit, gating assessment of whether the
                     intervention could worsen access or outcome
                     inequities, or introduce clinical or data-privacy
                     safety risk, before scale-up proceeds
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Ma'aikatar lafiya tana shirin faɗaɗa sabis ɗin tunatarwa ta SMS na lafiyar mata masu ciki daga gwajin yanki ɗaya zuwa turawa a ƙasa. Ta amfani da jagorar rarrabuwa ta WHO, an rarraba ɗan tsakanin a fili a matsayin ɗan tsakani na lafiyar dijital da ke fuskantar abokin ciniki, na sadarwa da aka nufa, wanda ke ba da damar kwatanta da irin ɗan tsakani da aka tantance a wani wuri maimakon a tantance shi kamar sabo gaba ɗaya. An gina tsarin sa ido da tantancewa bisa jagorar WHO don bibiyar isa da sakamakon asibiti duka, ba sakamako kaɗai ba. Tantance shirin faɗaɗawa ya gano cewa amincin hanyar sadarwar cellular ta yankin gwajin ya fi matsakaicin ƙasa sosai, ma'ana bitar daidaito da aminci tana nuna haɗari mai ma'ana: faɗaɗawa a ƙasa ba tare da fara tantance rufin cellular na karkara ba na iya tara fa'ida a yankuna masu haɗi da wadata fiye, yana faɗaɗa maimakon rufe giɓin daidaiton lafiyar uwaye da ke akwai. Wannan bincike — wanda ya bayyana musamman domin an ɗauki daidaito a matsayin ma'aunin shinge maimakon tunani na baya — yana jagorantar ma'aikatar ta raba turawa zuwa matakai ta matakin rufin hanyar sadarwa maimakon faɗaɗawa daidai gwargwado.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Takardun jagorar WHO an buga su a bainar jama'a kuma ana samun su kyauta, amma amfani da su yana buƙatar bayanan gida da WHO kanta ba ta bayarwa — bayanan haɗi da ilimin dijital na musamman ga ƙasa ko yankin turawa, bayanan ƙarfin tsarin lafiya na gida, da bayanan sakamako na gida — ma'ana tsarin yana ba da tsarin tantancewa, ba bayanan kansu ba. Domin jagorar WHO da gangan tana da faɗi isa don amfani a yanayin ƙasa daban-daban sosai, amfani da ita da kyau yana buƙatar ƙwarewar gida ta gaske da bayanai don guje wa tantancewa ta gama gari, irin ta alamar dubawa wadda ke rasa haɗarin musamman ga ƙasa; tantancewar da ta dace da WHO da aka gudanar ba tare da bayanan tsarin lafiya na gida da na daidaito ba ba zai bayyana haɗarin da aka ƙera ta don kamawa ba.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ɗaukar rarrabuwa kaɗai a matsayin tantancewa**: rarraba nau'in ɗan tsakani daidai wurin farawa ne na kwatantawa, ba tantance amincin ɗan tsakanin, tasirinsa, ko shirinsa na faɗaɗawa ba.
- **Ɗaukar tantance daidaito a matsayin ƙari na ƙarshe maimakon shinge**: tsarin WHO yana ɗaukar haɗarin daidaito a matsayin dalilin rage gudu ko sake ƙirar turawa, ba bayanin ƙafa da za a bayar da rahoto bayan faɗaɗawa ta riga ta faru ba; tantance shi a baya kawai yana kayar da manufarsa.
- **Amfani da tsarin ba tare da bayanan mahallin gida ba**: jagorar WHO da gangan tana da faɗi; ba tare da bayanan haɗi, ilimi, da ƙarfin tsarin lafiya na gida ba, tantancewa a kanta ta zama motsa jiki na jerin dubawa na gama gari maimakon ainihin tantance haɗari.
- **Zaton daidaitawa da WHO yana maye gurbin bitar ka'ida ko ɗa'a ta ƙasa**: jagorar WHO tana sanarwa da tsara tantancewa amma ba ta maye gurbin hanyoyin amincewa na ka'ida, kariyar bayanai, ko ɗa'a da ƙasa ke buƙata ba.
