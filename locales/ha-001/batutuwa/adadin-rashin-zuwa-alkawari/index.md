# Adadin Rashin Zuwa Alƙawari

Adadin rashin zuwa alƙawari (wanda ake kira "bai halarta ba", ko adadin DNA) shi ne kaso na alƙawuran da aka tsara waɗanda mara lafiya bai halarta ba kuma bai soke su da sanarwa mai ma'ana ba. Yana ɗaya daga cikin ma'aunin aiki mafi tsufa a harkar kiwon lafiya, kuma kayan aikin dijital, musamman tunatarwa, sake tsara alƙawari da kai, da yin ajiyar alƙawari ta shafin marasa lafiya, yanzu suna cikin hanyoyin da suka fi tasiri kuma suka fi samun shaida wajen rage shi.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kowane rashin zuwa yana nufin ɓatar da wani ɓangare na ƙarfin asibiti wanda galibi ba za a iya dawo da shi ba, domin yawancin ayyuka ba za su iya cike gibin rana ɗaya a cikin gajeren lokaci ba, don haka adadin yana tasiri kai tsaye kan tsawon jerin jira, kuɗin kowane alƙawari da aka kammala, da lokacin ma'aikatan asibiti da aka rasa. Halin rashin zuwa ba ya rabuwa daidai: yana da alaƙa da talauci, samun sufuri, nauyin kula da wasu, da nauyin sarrafa cututtuka masu ɗorewa da yawa, don haka kallon babban adadi a matsayin matsalar halayyar marasa lafiya kawai, maimakon wani ɓangare a matsayin alama game da shingen samun kulawa, yakan haifar da matakai (kamar hukunci na bai ɗaya) waɗanda ke ƙarfafa rashin daidaito maimakon rage shi. Tunatarwa ta dijital da sauƙin sake tsara alƙawari ta dijital suna cikin matakan da suka fi tasiri kuma masu araha a koyaushe, shi ya sa wannan ma'auni ya dace sosai da shirin auna lafiyar dijital ba kawai rahoton aiki ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
No-show rate = appointments marked "did not attend" / total scheduled appointments × 100
```

Yawanci ana cire alƙawarin da aka tsara daga ma'ajin rabo, ko a mayar da shi wani rukuni daban, idan ɗaya daga cikin ɓangarorin biyu ya soke shi da sanarwa fiye da wani lokaci da aka ƙayyade (yawanci awa 24). Ana bayar da rahoton sokewa a makare (ƙasa da wannan lokacin sanarwa) daban da ainihin rashin zuwa, domin tasirin aiki da na halayya ya bambanta.

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani asibitin al'umma ya tsara alƙawari 2,000 a wata. Daga cikinsu, an soke 140 da sanarwa fiye da awa 24 (an sake ba su kwanan wata aka cire su daga ma'ajin rabo), an soke 60 a makare (ƙasa da awa 24), kuma an rubuta 180 a matsayin ainihin rashin zuwa ba tare da wata hulɗa ba. Adadin rashin zuwa shi ne 180 / 2,000 × 100 = 9%. Da an haɗa sokewa 60 na makare a rukuni ɗaya da ainihin rashin zuwa, adadin da aka bayar da rahoto zai tashi zuwa 12%, shi ya sa dole a koyaushe a bayyana ma'anar da aka yi amfani da ita tare da adadin.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Tsarin tsara alƙawari ko sarrafa aikin asibiti shi ne babban tushe, ta amfani da lambobin yanayin alƙawarinsa; ingancin ma'aunin ya dogara gaba ɗaya ne kan ko ma'aikata suna amfani da yanayin da ya dace a koyaushe maimakon rukunin "an soke" na gama-gari ga komai. Ya kamata ƙungiyoyin da suka gabatar da tunatarwa ta dijital (SMS, sanarwar turawa ta manhaja, ko faɗakarwa ta shafin marasa lafiya) su auna adadin rashin zuwa kafin da bayan canjin ga haɗin marasa lafiya da ayyuka da za a iya kwatantawa, domin ingancin tunatarwa an rubuta shi sosai a cikin nazarin bazuwar da na lura, amma yana bambanta ta yawan jama'a da hanyar sadarwa.

## Kura-kurai

- **Kwatanta adadi na asali tsakanin asibitoci masu ayyukan yin ajiyar fiye da ƙima daban-daban**: asibitin da ke yin ajiyar fiye da ƙima da gangan don rama adadin rashin zuwa da ake tsammani zai nuna adadi na zahiri daban da wanda bai yi ba, ba tare da la'akari da ainihin halayyar marasa lafiya ba.
- **Haɗa sokewa a makare da ainihin rashin zuwa**: su biyun suna da dalilai daban-daban da mafita ta dijital daban-daban (matsalar sokewa a makare galibi ana magance ta da sauƙaƙa sake tsara alƙawari da kai; matsalar ainihin rashin zuwa galibi ana magance ta da ingantattun tunatarwa da daidaiton bayanan tuntuɓa).
- **Son zuciya na waɗanda suka rage daga manufofin sallama**: ayyukan da ke sallamar marasa lafiya bayan rashin zuwa akai-akai za su ga adadinsu ya inganta ta atomatik, yayin da kawai suke mayar da waɗannan marasa lafiya zuwa wani wuri a cikin tsarin.
- **Zargin mara lafiya kan keɓewar dijital**: mara lafiya da ba shi da wayar salula mai wayo ko amintaccen sabis na saƙon rubutu ba zai amfana daga dabarar tunatarwa ta dijital kaɗai ba, don haka yawanci ana buƙatar tsarin hanyoyi da yawa (wasiƙa, kira, saƙon rubutu, manhaja) don guje wa faɗaɗa giɓin samun kulawa.

## Tushe

- NHS England, alƙawuran da aka rasa a kulawar farko da ta marasa lafiya na waje, ƙididdigar da aka buga da jagora
- Nazarin tsari na Cochrane kan matakan rage alƙawuran kiwon lafiya da aka rasa, ciki har da tsarin tunatarwa
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan alaƙar zamantakewa-tattalin arziki da ta yawan jama'a da rashin halartar alƙawari

Duba kuma: [adadin ziyarar lafiya ta nesa](../adadin-ziyarar-lafiya-ta-nesa/), domin halayyar rashin zuwa yawanci tana bambanta ta hanyar shawarwari, da [adadin karɓar shafin marasa lafiya](../adadin-karbar-shafin-marasa-lafiya/), domin tsara alƙawari da kai da tunatarwa ta shafin marasa lafiya babban mataki ne na dijital.
