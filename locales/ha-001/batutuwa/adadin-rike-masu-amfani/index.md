# Adadin Riƙe Masu Amfani

Adadin riƙe masu amfani shi ne kaso na masu amfani masu aiki a lokacin farawa waɗanda ke ci gaba da aiki a wani lokaci na gaba, kuma akasinsa, adadin barin (churn ko dropout), shi ne kaso waɗanda suka daina amfani da samfurin gaba ɗaya. Inda adadin karɓar shafin marasa lafiya (duba wannan batu) ke auna ko mara lafiya ya taɓa kunna samfurin lafiyar dijital da ma'ana, riƙewa tana auna ko suna ci gaba da amfani da shi — kuma ga kowane samfurin lafiyar dijital irin na biyan kuɗi ko kulawa mai ci gaba, riƙewa yawanci ita ce ma'auni ɗaya da ya fi dacewa da tasirin asibiti da dorewar kasuwanci.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Samfurin lafiyar dijital da ba zai iya riƙe masu amfani ba ba zai iya isar da fa'idar asibiti mai ɗorewa ba, komai ƙarfin adadin karɓarsa ko kunnawarsa na farko: kayan aikin sarrafa cuta mai ɗorewa da aka yi amfani da shi na makonni biyu sannan aka bar shi ba zai yi yuwuwar motsa sakamakon ma'aunin halittar jiki da ya dogara da watanni na ci gaba da canjin halayya ba. Riƙewa kuma tana ɗaya daga cikin ma'aunin da ke da sakamako mafi girma a kasuwanci da kamfanin lafiyar dijital ke bayar da rahoto ga masu zuba jari da masu biyan kuɗi, domin lanƙwasar riƙewa (siffar raguwar a tsawon lokaci, ba kaso ɗaya na riƙewa kawai ba) tana bayyana ko samfurin ya sami tsarin amfani mai dorewa da gaske ko kuma kawai yana ɗaukar sha'awar farko ta sabon abu da ke ɓacewa kamar yadda ake tsammani. Lanƙwasar riƙewa da ke lankwasa bayan raguwa ta farko (marasa lafiyar da suka wuce wata na farko suna zama) alama ce daban, kuma mafi lafiya sosai, fiye da wadda ke ci gaba da raguwa a hankali ba tare da bene ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Retention rate (period N) = users active in period N who were also active
                             in the starting cohort period / users in the
                             starting cohort period × 100

Churn rate = 1 − retention rate (for the same period)

Report as a cohort retention curve (retention at day/week/month 1, 2, 3…),
not a single point-in-time figure, since a single snapshot conflates
recently joined users (who haven't had a chance to churn yet) with
long-tenured ones.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wata manhajar lafiyar dijital tana yi wa ƙungiyar sabbin masu amfani 1,000 rajista a Janairu. Zuwa ƙarshen wata na 1, 640 daga cikin 1,000 na asali har yanzu suna aiki (riƙewar wata na 1 64%). Zuwa ƙarshen wata na 3, 410 suna ci gaba da aiki (riƙewar wata na 3 41%). Zuwa wata na 6, 380 suna ci gaba da aiki (riƙewar wata na 6 38%). Siffar wannan lanƙwasa — raguwa mai tsanani ta farko sai lankwashewa tsakanin watanni 3 da 6 — tana nuna cewa samfurin yana riƙe da tsayayyen ainihin masu amfani da zarar sun wuce shingen karɓa na farko, wanda alama ce daban sosai kuma mafi ƙarfafawa fiye da idan raguwar daga wata na 3 zuwa wata na 6 ta ci gaba da irin yawan watanni 1 zuwa 3.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Ana lissafa riƙewa daga rajistar abubuwan shiga ko aiki na samfurin kansa, ana ayyana "mai aiki" daidai (misali, aƙalla zama ɗaya mai cancanta a cikin lokacin) a kowace ƙungiya da ake kwatantawa. Ya kamata a kwatanta ƙungiyoyi a kan daidaitaccen tushe — ma'anar farawa ta "mai aiki" iri ɗaya, tsawon taga lura iri ɗaya — tun da ko ƙananan bambance-bambancen ma'ana (watannin kwanaki 30 da kwanaki 28, ko ƙofar "mai aiki" mai tsauri da mai sassauƙa) na iya sauya kason riƙewa da aka bayar da rahoto da maki da yawa ba tare da wani ainihin bambancin halayyar masu amfani ba. Tasirin yanayi ya zama ruwan dare a manhajojin lafiya da aka ɗaure da ƙudurin sabuwar shekara ko takamaiman lokutan wayar da kai kan lafiya, don haka kwatancen ƙungiya na shekara-bayan-shekara yawanci ya fi ba da bayani fiye da kwatanta ƙungiyoyi maƙwabta daga lokuta daban-daban na shekara.

## Kura-kurai

- **Bayar da rahoton hoton riƙewa guda ɗaya maimakon lanƙwasa**: adadi guda "X% na masu amfani har yanzu suna aiki" ba tare da siffar raguwar a tsawon lokaci ba ba zai iya bambance samfurin da ke daidaitawa (mai lafiya) da wanda ke raguwa a ci gaba (mara lafiya) ba.
- **Canza ma'anar "mai aiki" tsakanin lokutan rahoto**: sassauta ma'anar mai amfani mai aiki (misali ƙidaya buɗe manhaja ba da gangan ba maimakon aiki da aka kammala) na iya sa riƙewa ta bayyana tana ingantuwa yayin da ainihin amfani bai canza ba kwata-kwata.
- **Yin watsi da yanayin ƙungiya**: kwatanta riƙewar ƙungiyar Janairu (galibi ana ƙara ta da rajistar ƙudurin sabuwar shekara, wanda ke kawo ƙungiya mai ƙarancin himma a matsakaici) da ƙungiyar da aka samu a wani lokaci daban na shekara na iya haifar da ƙarshen yanayi mai ruɗarwa.
- **Haɗa ƙungiyoyin halitta da na samu-da-biya**: masu amfani da aka samu ta hanyoyi daban-daban galibi suna riƙewa daban sosai; haɗa su zuwa adadin riƙewa na haɗaka ɗaya na iya ɓoye matsalar riƙewa ta musamman ga hanya.
