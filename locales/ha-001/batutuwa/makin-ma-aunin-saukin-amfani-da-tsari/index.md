# Makin Ma'aunin Saukin Amfani da Tsari

Makin System Usability Scale (SUS) daidaitaccen jerin tambayoyi 10 ne da ake amfani da shi don ƙididdige yadda software ke da sauƙin amfani, yana samar da maki ɗaya daga 0 zuwa 100 wanda za a iya kwatanta shi da ƙa'idodin masana'antu da aka kafa sosai. Ya bambanta da Net Promoter Score, wanda ke auna yarda ta ba da shawara, ko ma'aunin sakamakon da marasa lafiya ke bayarwa, waɗanda ke auna yanayin asibiti ko aiki, SUS yana auna abu ɗaya na musamman: yadda software kanta ke da sauƙin koyo da amfani, ga marasa lafiya ko ma'aikatan asibiti.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kayan aikin lafiyar dijital na iya samun ƙarfin shaidar asibiti da hujjar kasuwanci mai gamsarwa amma har yanzu ya gaza a aikace saboda marasa lafiya ko likitoci suna ganin mu'amalar tana ruɗarwa, jinkiri, ko takaici don amfani — kuma saboda SUS kayan aiki ne da aka tabbatar, da ake amfani da shi sosai tare da shekarun bayanan kwatantawa da aka buga a duk masana'antu, yana ba ƙungiyar lafiyar dijital damar kwatanta saukin amfani na samfurinta da rarrabawa da aka sani maimakon dogara da ra'ayoyin kai-tsaye ko korafe-korafen labari. SUS da gangan ba shi da alaƙa da fasaha kuma yana da sauri a gudanar (yawanci ƙasa da mintuna biyar), wanda ke sa ya yiwu a gudanar da shi akai-akai a duk maimaitawar ƙira, ba kamar cikakken nazarin saukin amfani ko gwajin asibiti na hukuma ba. Domin gazawar saukin amfani da ke fuskantar likita gudummawa ce da aka rubuta ga gajiyawa (duba adadin gajiyawar likitoci) kuma gazawar saukin amfani da ke fuskantar mara lafiya gudummawa ce da aka rubuta ga barin amfani da rashin ingantaccen ilimin dijital (duba adadin ilimin dijital), SUS yana aiki a matsayin alamar gargaɗin farko, mai arha ta saukin amfani wadda za ta iya kama matsalar ƙira kafin ta bayyana a waɗannan ma'aunin ƙasa masu sakamako fiye.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
SUS score = ((sum of odd-numbered item scores − 5) +
             (25 − sum of even-numbered item scores)) × 2.5

Result is a single score from 0 to 100 (not a percentage, despite the
scale, since it does not represent "percent correct" or similar).

Published benchmark interpretation (Bangor et al.):
  Above 80  — excellent usability
  68        — average, based on the broad industry norm
  Below 51  — poor usability, warranting investigation
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani dandalin telehealth yana gudanar da daidaitattun tambayoyin SUS na abubuwa 10 ga marasa lafiya 150 bayan ziyararsu ta bidiyo ta farko. Matsakaicin makin SUS da aka lissafa a duk masu amsawa shi ne 74. Idan aka kwatanta da matsakaicin masana'antu da ake ambata sosai na 68, wannan yana nuna saukin amfani sama da matsakaici ga wannan yawan marasa lafiya da yanayin amfani, ko da yake har yanzu ƙasa da ƙofar "kyakkyawa" ta 80 sosai wadda za ta nuna ƙarancin shingen saukin amfani da suka rage. Rarraba amsoshi 150 iri ɗaya ta shekaru yana nuna matsakaicin maki na 81 ga marasa lafiya ƙasa da shekaru 50 da 62 ga marasa lafiya masu shekaru 65 zuwa sama — giɓi da ke nuni ga takamaiman matsalar saukin amfani da za a iya magancewa ga tsofaffi maimakon matsalar saukin amfani ta samfur gaba ɗaya, wanda matsakaicin haɗaka ɗaya zai ɓoye.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Bayanan SUS suna zuwa kai tsaye daga marasa lafiya ko likitoci da ke kammala daidaitattun tambayoyin abubuwa 10, kuma dole ne a gudanar da kayan aikin daidai yadda aka tabbatar (abubuwa 10 iri ɗaya, ma'aunin yarjejeniya na maki 5 iri ɗaya, dabarar bayar da maki iri ɗaya) don makin da ya haifar ya zama mai iya kwatantawa da ma'aunin da aka buga; sigar tambayoyin da aka gyara ko aka taƙaita, komai kyakkyawan nufi, tana samar da maki da ba za a iya fassarawa da aminci a kan daidaitaccen rarrabawar ma'auni ba. SUS yana auna saukin amfani da ake ji, wanda ke da alaƙa da, amma ba ɗaya da nasarar kammala aiki ta haƙiƙa ba (duba adadin ilimin dijital don ma'auni bisa kammala aiki); samfur na iya samun kyakkyawan makin SUS daga marasa lafiya da ba su gwada fasali masu rikitarwa ba, don haka haɗa SUS da bayanan kammala aiki na haƙiƙa yana ba da cikakken hoto fiye da kowanne shi kaɗai. Lokacin amsa yana da muhimmanci: gudanar da SUS nan da nan bayan wani takamaiman lamari mai takaici (haɗin da ya gaza, mataki mai ruɗarwa) da bayan zama mai sauƙi na iya sauya maki ba tare da la'akari da gaba ɗaya saukin amfani na samfurin ba.

## Kura-kurai

- **Gyara abubuwan tambayoyi ko bayar da maki na ma'auni**: ko ƙananan canje-canjen kalmomi ko ma'auni suna ɓata kwatance da rarrabawar ma'auni da aka buga da aka kafa sosai; a yi amfani da daidaitaccen kayan aikin abubuwa 10 daidai yadda aka tabbatar.
- **Bayar da rahoton matsakaicin maki kawai ba tare da rarrabawa ba**: saukin amfani yakan bambanta sosai ta shekarun mai amfani, ilimin dijital, ko matsayi (mara lafiya da likita); a rarraba rahoton don gano takamaiman giɓin saukin amfani da za a iya magancewa waɗanda matsakaici ɗaya ke ɓoyewa.
- **Ɗaukar SUS a matsayin ma'aunin tasirin asibiti**: SUS yana auna saukin amfani musamman, ba sakamakon asibiti ko gamsuwa da kulawa ba; kayan aiki mai sauƙin amfani sosai na iya kasa inganta sakamakon asibiti, kuma kada a taɓa rikita ko musanya waɗannan.
- **Gudanar da bincike bayan zama masu sauƙi ko masu takaici sosai kaɗai**: lokaci da mahallin gudanarwa na iya karkatar da makin; a gudanar da shi a koyaushe a samfurin wakilci na zama na gaske, ba kawai masu dacewa ko waɗanda aka zaɓa musamman ba.
