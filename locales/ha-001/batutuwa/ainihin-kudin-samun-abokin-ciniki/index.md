# Ainihin Kuɗin Samun Abokin Ciniki

Ainihin kuɗin samun abokin ciniki (ainihin CAC) shi ne cikakken kuɗin samun sabon abokin ciniki mai biyan kuɗi ɗaya ko mara lafiya da aka yi wa rajista, wanda ya haɗa ba kawai kashe kuɗin talla da aka biya ba amma kowane irin kuɗi da ya ba da gudummawa mai ma'ana ga wannan samu: kuɗin hukuma da na ƙirƙira, kayayyakin more rayuwa na fasahar talla da nazari, da — musamman ga lafiyar dijital — kuɗin aikin asibiti ko na aiki na shiga, tabbatar da cancanta, da shigarwa. Yana wanzuwa a matsayin ma'auni daban domin adadin kuɗin samu da dandalin talla ya bayar yakan ƙididdige ainihin kuɗin ƙungiyar ga kowane abokin ciniki da aka samu ƙasa a bayyane da kuma sosai.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ƙungiyoyin lafiyar dijital da ke sarrafa girma ta amfani da adadin kuɗin-kowane-samu da dandalin talla ya bayar kaɗai suna yawan yanke shawarar rarraba albarkatu a kan lambobin da suka bar 30-50% na ainihin kuɗin samu, domin waɗannan adadin dandali suna ɗaukar kashe kuɗin kafafen watsa labarai kawai kuma suna cire kuɗin hukuma, lasisin fasahar talla, da — musamman ga kiwon lafiya — aikin shiga da tabbatar da cancanta mai buƙatar ma'aikata da ƙungiyar asibiti ko ta aiki ke yi ga kowane sabon mara lafiya kafin a ƙidaya shi a matsayin wanda aka samu. Wannan giɓi yana da muhimmanci a lafiyar dijital fiye da yawancin sauran fannoni daidai saboda aikin shiga na asibiti yana da tsada kuma wajibi ne, ba kamar kasuwancin e-commerce ba, inda "sayarwa" ba ta buƙatar wani aikin ofis na baya makamancinsa. Ƙungiya da ke inganta kashe kuɗin talla a kan adadin CAC da aka ƙasƙantar da shi ba tare da gaskiya ba za ta yi saka jari fiye da kima a tsari a hanyoyin da suke da arha a allon dandali amma masu tsada da zarar an lissafa ainihin CAC.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
True CAC = (paid media spend + agency and creative fees + marketing
            technology and analytics costs + clinical/operational intake
            labour cost) / new customers or patients acquired in period

Clinical/operational intake labour cost should be estimated from
loaded labour cost (salary, benefits, overhead) × average hours spent
per acquired patient on intake, eligibility verification, and onboarding.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani kamfanin lafiyar dijital yana samun sabbin marasa lafiya 500 a wata. Allunan dandalin talla suna bayar da rahoton kuɗin-kowane-samu na haɗaka na $120, bisa kashe kuɗin kafafen watsa labarai da aka biya na $60,000. Ƙara kuɗin hukuma na $9,000, kuɗin fasahar talla na $6,000, da ƙiyasin kuɗin aikin shiga na mintuna 45 ga kowane mara lafiya a farashin ma'aikaci mai cikakken nauyi na $40/awa (500 × 0.75 × $40 = $15,000) yana kawo jimillar kuɗin samu zuwa $60,000 + $9,000 + $6,000 + $15,000 = $90,000. Ainihin CAC shi ne $90,000 / 500 = $180 — 50% fiye da adadin $120 da dandalin talla kaɗai ya bayar, kuma shi ne adadin da ya kamata ya sanar da rarraba kasafin hanya da shawarwarin tattalin arzikin raka'a da gaske.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Kashe kuɗin kafafen watsa labarai da kuɗin-kowane-samu da dandali ya bayar suna zuwa kai tsaye daga dandalin talla kansu (bincike, zamantakewa, programmatic); kuɗin hukuma da na fasahar talla suna zuwa daga bayanan kuɗi ko bashin da za a biya; kuɗin aikin shiga shi ne ɓangaren da ya fi wahalar samu daidai kuma yawanci yana buƙatar ko dai nazarin lokaci-da-motsi ko ƙiyasi mai ma'ana da aka amince da shi tare da shugabancin aiki, tun da yawancin ƙungiyoyi ba sa bibiyar lokacin ma'aikata ga kowane samu a asali. Ya kamata a lissafa ainihin CAC ga kowace hanyar samu inda yawa ya ba da dama, tun da kuɗin aikin shiga ga kowane mara lafiya galibi yana kama da juna a tsakanin hanyoyi yayin da kuɗin kafafen watsa labarai ke bambanta sosai, ma'ana giɓi tsakanin CAC da dandali ya bayar da ainihin CAC ya fi girma a daidaito ga hanyoyin da suka fi kama da arha.

## Kura-kurai

- **Dogara da allunan dandalin talla kaɗai**: kuɗin-kowane-samu da dandali ya bayar yana cire kuɗin hukuma, fasahar talla, da aikin shiga a tsari kuma ba madadin lissafin ainihin CAC ba ne.
- **Mantawa da aikin shiga na asibiti ko na aiki**: wannan koyaushe shi ne ɓangaren kuɗi da aka fi mantawa da shi musamman a lafiyar dijital, kuma galibi shi ne mafi girman mai ba da gudummawa ga giɓi tsakanin kuɗin da dandali ya bayar da ainihin CAC.
- **Matsakaita ainihin CAC a duk hanyoyi**: adadin ainihin CAC na haɗaka na iya ɓoye cewa hanya ɗaya ta fi tsada sosai da zarar an ƙara kuɗin cikakken nauyi, ko da ta bayyana mafi arha a dandalin talla kaɗai.
- **Rashin sabunta ƙiyasin kuɗin aiki yayin da tsarin shiga ke canzawa**: sake ƙirar tsarin shiga (misali sarrafa tabbatar da cancanta ta atomatik) na iya canza ainihin CAC sosai, kuma tsohon ƙiyasin aiki zai bayyana adadin yanzu ba daidai ba.

## Tushe

- Association of National Advertisers (ANA), jagora kan auna kuɗin talla da bayyanar kafafen watsa labarai
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu da na masana'antu kan tattalin arzikin raka'a na lafiyar dijital da tsarin kuɗin shiga kasuwa, misali nazarin da Rock Health da ƙungiyoyin bincike makamantansu na lafiyar dijital suka buga
- Healthcare Financial Management Association (HFMA), jagora kan lissafin kuɗi mai cikakken nauyi a ayyukan kiwon lafiya
