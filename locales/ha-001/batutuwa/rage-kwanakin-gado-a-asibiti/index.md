# Rage Kwanakin Gado a Asibiti

Rage kwanakin gado a asibiti yana auna jimillar kwanakin gadon marasa lafiyar da aka kwantar a asibiti da aka guje wa ta hanyar mayar da wani zangon kulawa da aka ayyana — galibi murmurewa bayan tiyata ko sarrafa cuta mai tsanani — daga zaman asibiti na gargajiya zuwa madadin da ake tallafawa ta dijital kamar sashen kama-da-wane (virtual ward) ko shirin kulawa a gida da asibiti ke jagoranta. Shi ne ma'aunin ƙarfi na farko ga shirye-shiryen virtual ward da hospital-at-home, yana mayar da canjin samfurin kulawa na asibiti kai tsaye zuwa kuɗin (ƙarfin gado) da ayyukan asibiti da masu tsara tsarin ke sarrafawa da gaske.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ƙarfin gado na marasa lafiya da aka kwantar yana ɗaya daga cikin albarkatu mafi ƙaranci da tsada a kowane tsarin asibiti, kuma babban alƙawarin darajar virtual ward ko shirin hospital-at-home shi ne cewa yana iya isar da ƙayyadadden matakin kulawar asibiti cikin aminci ba tare da mamaye gado na zahiri ba, yana 'yantar da wannan ƙarfi ga marasa lafiyar da ba za a iya sarrafa su ta wata hanya ba. Rage kwanakin gado yana mayar da iƙirari da yawanci maras fahimta ("wannan shirin yana inganta kulawa") zuwa lamba ta aiki mai tabbaci wadda masu tsara ƙarfin asibiti, ƙungiyoyin kuɗi, da masu ba da kwangila za su iya yin aiki da ita kai tsaye: ana iya amfani da ita don ƙirƙirar samfurin ko saka jari a shirin sa ido yana biyan kansa a kuɗin gado da aka guje wa, kuma da nawa. Domin rage kwanakin gado yana da daraja ne kawai idan an kiyaye amincin marasa lafiya, ya kamata a bayar da rahotonsa koyaushe tare da, ba maimakon ba, ma'aunin sakamakon aminci (kamar sake kwantarwa ko adadin ƙara girma zuwa kulawar marasa lafiya da aka kwantar) ga yawan jama'a iri ɗaya.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Bed-day reduction = expected bed days under standard inpatient care
                     (based on historical length-of-stay data for a
                     matched patient cohort) − actual bed days used by
                     patients on the virtual/digital pathway

Report per clinical pathway (e.g. post-surgical recovery, acute
respiratory exacerbation), since expected length of stay varies hugely
by condition and a blended figure across unrelated pathways is not
meaningful.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Bayanan tarihi na wani asibiti sun nuna cewa marasa lafiyar da ke murmurewa daga wani takamaiman tsarin tiyata da aka zaɓa suna da matsakaicin tsawon zaman asibiti na kwanaki 4. Shirin virtual ward yana yi wa marasa lafiya 150 da ke murmurewa daga wannan tsari rajista, yana sallamar su bayan matsakaicin kwanaki 1.5 na asibiti tare da sauran murmurewa ana sa ido a kai daga nesa. Rage kwanakin gado shi ne (4 − 1.5) × 150 = kwanakin gado 375 a tsawon lokacin auna. Ya kamata a bayar da rahoton wannan adadi tare da adadin ƙara girma zuwa kulawar marasa lafiya da aka kwantar da adadin sake kwantarwa na kwanaki 30 na ƙungiyar virtual ward ga marasa lafiya 150 iri ɗaya, tun da tanadin kwanakin gado da ke zuwa a kan farashin ƙara girma ko sake kwantarwa mafi girma sosai ba nasarar asibiti ba ce da adadin kanun labarai zai nuna.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Kwanakin gado da ake tsammani suna buƙatar ingantaccen tushe na tarihi, wanda ya fi dacewa daga ƙungiyar marasa lafiya da aka daidaita da aka yi wa magani a ƙarƙashin daidaitaccen kulawar marasa lafiya da aka kwantar tare da halayen asibiti iri ɗaya (shekaru, cututtukan da ke tare, nau'in tsari, tsanani) da yawan virtual ward, tun da kwatanta da matsakaicin tarihi mara daidaitawa yana haɗarin ƙara ko rage ainihin raguwa idan ƙungiyar da aka gudanar ta dijital ta fi lafiya ko ta fi rashin lafiya a tsari fiye da ƙungiyar kwatantawa ta tarihi. Ainihin kwanakin gado da aka yi amfani da su a hanyar dijital suna zuwa daga tsarin shigar-sallama-sauya (ADT) na asibiti kansa; duk wani ƙara girma baya zuwa kulawar marasa lafiya da aka kwantar a lokacin murmurewa da aka sa ido ya kamata a ƙidaya shi da gaskiya a kan shirin (a matsayin kwanakin gado da aka yi amfani da su, ba a cire shi ba), tun da cire ƙara girma daga lissafi zai ƙara raguwar da ake gani ta wucin gadi.

## Kura-kurai

- **Bayar da rahoton rage kwanakin gado ba tare da kwatancen aminci da aka daidaita ba**: virtual ward da ke ceton kwanakin gado amma yana da ƙara girma ko adadin sake kwantarwa mafi muni sosai fiye da daidaitaccen kulawa bai nuna ingantuwa ta gaske ba; koyaushe a bayar da rahoton duka biyun tare.
- **Amfani da tushe na tarihi mara daidaitawa ko da ya tsufa**: kwatanta da ƙungiyar tarihi mai haɗin shari'a, nauyin cututtukan da ke tare, ko zamanin aikin asibiti daban na iya ƙara ko rage ainihin tanadin kwanakin gado sosai.
- **Cire ƙara girma zuwa kulawar marasa lafiya da aka kwantar daga lissafi**: mara lafiyar da ake sa ido a kan sa ta kama-da-wane amma sai aka ƙara girma zuwa gadon asibiti a tsakiyar murmurewa ya kamata a ƙidaya waɗannan kwanakin gado a kan shirin, ba a watsar da su a ɓoye daga nazari ba.
- **Haɗa hanyoyi masu tsayin zama da ake tsammani daban sosai**: tara rage kwanakin gado a hanyoyin asibiti marasa alaƙa (misali haɗa murmurewa bayan tiyata da sarrafa cutar numfashi mai ɗorewa) zuwa adadi ɗaya yana ɓoye wace takamaiman hanya ce ke haifar da tanadin da gaske.

## Tushe

- NHS England, jagorar shirin virtual ward da hospital-at-home da ma'aunin bayar da rahoton tasirin kwanakin gado
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan samfuran hospital-at-home da virtual ward, misali nazarin da aka buga a JAMA Internal Medicine da npj Digital Medicine
- Institute for Healthcare Improvement (IHI), jagora kan sarrafa ƙarfi da madadin samfuran kulawa

Duba kuma: [adadin sake kwantar da marasa lafiya](../adadin-sake-kwantar-da-marasa-lafiya/), ma'aunin aminci da ya kamata a bayar da rahotonsa koyaushe tare da kowane iƙirarin rage kwanakin gado ga yawan marasa lafiya iri ɗaya.
