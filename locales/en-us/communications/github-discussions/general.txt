Say hello and tell us how you use Digital Health Metrics

Welcome to the Digital Health Metrics Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the book.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Digital Health Metrics because I wanted a reference book of digital health metric definitions, examples, and reasoning, written for teams building, evaluating, and commissioning digital health products to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Which digital health metric do you find hardest to define or explain to colleagues?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.

## Where other topics belong

- Suggestions for new or changed topics: Ideas
- Errors or broken links: Issues (https://github.com/digital-health-metrics/digital-health-metrics/issues)
