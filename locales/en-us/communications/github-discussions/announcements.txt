Welcome to Digital Health Metrics Discussions

Digital Health Metrics now has GitHub Discussions, a place to ask questions, suggest ideas, and share what you have done with it.

Digital Health Metrics is a reference book of digital health metric definitions, examples, and reasoning, written for teams building, evaluating, and commissioning digital health products. It is written for teams building, evaluating, and commissioning digital health products.

## What this means for you

- Things you have built or tried with the book: Show and tell
- Everything else: General
- Errors, broken links, or typos: Issues (https://github.com/digital-health-metrics/digital-health-metrics/issues)
