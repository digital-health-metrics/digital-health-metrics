Show and tell: share what you have done with Digital Health Metrics

This is the place to share what you have made, tried, or learned with Digital Health Metrics. Examples include a team practice you adopted, a worked example from your own data, a talk or workshop that used the book, or a tool built around it.

## How to share

Copy this template into a new discussion in Show and tell:

**Title:** the thing and what it does.

**What it is:** one sentence.

**Why you made it:** two or three sentences on the problem or curiosity behind it.

**Show it:** a screenshot, a short excerpt, or a link.

**How to try it:** a few steps, if others can reproduce it.

**One thing that was hard or surprising:** stories invite replies.

**What you want:** feedback, collaborators, or other people's examples.

## Ground rules

- Describe what exists. Do not claim results you cannot show.
- Credit work you built on.
- Reply to feedback kindly.

The book is at https://digital-health-metrics.github.io. Share your first example below.
