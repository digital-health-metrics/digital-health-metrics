# Digital Health Metrics

A reference beuk o digital health metric definitions: whit ilka metric means, hoo tae caulculate it, a warked example, data sources an caveats, an common pitfalls, organisit for fowk biggin, rinnin, an evaluatin digital health products an services.

New here? Stairt wi [rate o patient portal adoption](tapics/rate-o-patient-portal-adoption/) an [rate o missit appointments](tapics/rate-o-missit-appointments/) — twa metrics that turn up in near aa digital health business cases.

## Patient engagement an access

- [Rate o patient portal adoption](tapics/rate-o-patient-portal-adoption/) — registration, activation, an active uise; three different rates that get mixed up as ane ower aften
- [Rate o telehealth visits](tapics/rate-o-telehealth-visits/) — the share o care delivert remotely, an why video an telephone shuid niver be reportit as ae nummer
- [Rate o missit appointments](tapics/rate-o-missit-appointments/) — the auldest operational metric in healthcare, an ane o the best-evidencit targets for digital reminders
- [Rate o patient engagement consistency](tapics/rate-o-patient-engagement-consistency/) — hoo regularly a patient interacts wi a digital tool ower time, as distinct frae whether they've uised it ava
- [Rate o user retention](tapics/rate-o-user-retention/) — the cohort retention curve that tells a product wi a sustainable uise pattern frae ane ridin a wave o novelty
- [DAU/MAU stickiness ratio](tapics/dau-mau-stickiness-ratio-sco/) — the standard product-analytics measure o engagement intensity athort a hale user base
- [Patient Net Promoter Score](tapics/patient-net-promoter-scorrie/) — the widely uised, an widely criticised, single-question satisfaction metric
- [System Usability Scale score](tapics/seestem-uisability-scale-scorrie/) — a standardised 10-item questionnaire quantifyin hoo uisable a piece o software actually is

## Digital care operations an safety

- [Rate o clinical alert owerrides](tapics/rate-o-clinical-alert-owerrides/) — the standard signal for alert fatigue in clinical decision support
- [Digital referral turnaround time](tapics/digital-referral-turnaroond-time/) — the process metric that shaws whether an e-referral system is really savin time
- [Time til intervention](tapics/time-til-intervention/) — hoo quickly a clinical team responds tae an automatit health alert
- [Bed-day cuttin](tapics/bed-day-cuttin/) — inpatient bed days saved bi shiftin recovery tae a virtual ward, aye reportit alangside a safety metric

## Clinical outcomes an quality

- [Rate o biometric betterment](tapics/rate-o-biometric-betterment/) — the share o patients gettin a clinically meaningfu chynge in a trackit biometric like HbA1c or BMI
- [Rate o biometric steadiness](tapics/rate-o-biometric-steadiness/) — sustainit control inside a target range, as distinct frae a yin-aff improvement
- [Triage routing accuracy](tapics/triage-routin-accuracy/) — whether an AI or digital triage tool correctly routes a patient tae the richt level o care
- [Rate o medication adherence](tapics/rate-o-medication-adherence/) — the proportion o days a patient hid access tae their medication as prescribit
- [Rate o hospital readmission](tapics/rate-o-hospital-readmission/) — the metric maist straucht tied tae payer economics an value-based care contracts
- [Rate o ePROM completion](tapics/rate-o-eprom-completion/) — the completion rate for electronic patient-reportit outcome measures, an why a faain rate can itsel be a clinical signal

## Merketin an growth economics

- [True cost o gettin customers](tapics/true-cost-o-gettin-customers/) — the fully loadit cost o gettin ae new patient, commonly undercoontit bi 30-50%
- [LTV til CAC ratio](tapics/ltv-til-cac-ratio/) — lifetime value again true acquisition cost; 3:1 is the widely citit sustainable baseline
- [Ratio o merketin efficiency](tapics/ratio-o-merketin-efficiency/) — total revenue ower total merketin spend, an independent check again platform-reportit ROAS

## Digital health equity

- [Rate o digital access](tapics/rate-o-digital-access/) — the precondition metric for ilka ither digital health measure in this beuk
- [Rate o digital literacy](tapics/rate-o-digital-literacy/) — whether patients that dae hae access can actually uise it unaidit

## Technical an operational infrastructure

- [Rate o physician burnout](tapics/rate-o-doctor-burnout/) — trackit alangside clinician-facin digital tool burden, sin badly designit software is a documentit contributor
- [Rate o device uptime](tapics/rate-o-device-uptime/) — the foundational infrastructure metric aneath ilka remote monitorin programme

## Financial an economic value

- [Cost per episode o care](tapics/cost-per-episode-o-care/) — the standard unit o financial comparison in value-based care contracts
- [Rate o emergency department diversion](tapics/rate-o-ed-diversion/) — patient contacts safely redirectit awa frae the ED, aye reportit alangside a missed-emergency safety metric
- [Return on investment (ROI) an value on investment (VOI)](tapics/roi-an-voi/) — whether a digital investment peyed for itsel financially, an whether it wis worth daein accountin for ilka thing that maitters

## Evaluation framewirks

- [RE-AIM framewirk](tapics/re-aim-framewirk/) — Reach, Effectiveness, Adoption, Implementation, an Maintenance; five dimensions raither than ae metric
- [WHO Digital Health Assessment Framewirk](tapics/who-digital-health-assessment-framewirk/) — WHO's guidance for assessin feasibility, safety, an equity athort national-scale deployments
- [ISO/TS 82304-2](tapics/iso-ts-82304-2/) — the international technical specification ahint maist health an wellness app quality labels
