# Ratio o Merketin Efficiency

The merketin efficiency ratio (MER) is total revenue dividit bi total merketin spend athort ilka channel, for a defined period. It's there as a deliberate independent reality check on platform-reportit return on ad spend (ROAS), which measures ilka channel's ain claimit contribution tae revenue an is structurally prone tae ower-creditin itsel — MER insteid looks at total revenue again total spend, a figure nae single ad platform can distort.

## Why it maitters

Ad platforms ilka report ROAS uisin their ain attribution model, an because maist organizations rin sindry channels at the same time, the same convertin customer is aften creditit bi mair than ane platform, sae the sum o ilka platform's self-reportit ROAS routinely owerstates total merketin contribution when compared again actual total revenue. MER sidesteps this problem entirely bi comparin total revenue tae total spend at the organization level, makkin it faur herder tae distort throu attribution gamin or platform-favourable reportin, which is exactly why finance an executive teams mair an mair treat it as the trustit tap-line efficiency check again which individual platform-reportit figures are calibratit. A merketin team that reports strang ROAS on ilka individual channel while owerall MER is faain haes a real efficiency or attribution-owerlap problem that channel-level reportin alane winna bring tae licht.

## Hoo it's caulculatit

```
MER = total revenue / total marketing spend (all channels, same period)

Unlike ROAS, MER is not calculated per channel — it is a single,
organization-wide figure precisely because its value comes from being
immune to any one platform's attribution claims.

A rising MER over time, at stable or growing spend, indicates improving
overall marketing efficiency; a stable MER at growing revenue can
indicate that growth is coming from non-marketing-driven sources
(e.g. referrals, organic search, word of mouth).
```

## Warked example

A digital health company generates $2,400,000 in revenue in a quarter, wi total merketin spend athort aa paid channels o $480,000. MER is $2,400,000 / $480,000 = 5.0. Individually, the paid search platform reports a ROAS o 6.0, the paid social platform reports a ROAS o 5.5, an a programmatic display platform reports a ROAS o 4.0 — if thae wur juist addit up as a claim on total revenue contribution, they wid imply mair total attributit revenue than the company actually generatit, sin a meaningfu share o convertin customers wur exposit tae mair than ane channel an are bein doubled- or trebled-coontit. The organization-wide MER o 5.0 is the nummer that reconciles this: it canna be inflatit bi attribution owerlap in the wey that platform-level ROAS figures structurally can.

## Data sources an caveats

Total revenue comes frae the organization's ain finance or billin system, an total merketin spend comes frae actual invoicet an paid merketin costs athort ilka channel — baith shuid be sourcit independently o ony ad platform's ain dashboard. MER shuid be trackit ower time as a trend raither than judged again a single universal benchmark, sin a "guid" MER varies enormously bi business model, margin structure, an growth stage: an early-stage company investin heavily in growth may deliberately accept a lawer MER than a mature company optimisin for profitability. MER disnae diagnose which channel is responsible for a chynge in efficiency — that diagnostic wark still needs channel-level analysis, ideally supplementit wi incrementality testin (holdoot groups that get nae merketin exposure) raither than platform-reportit attribution alane.

## Pitfalls

- **Takkin platform-reportit ROAS as additive athort channels**: addin up ilka platform's self-reportit ROAS owerstates total merketin contribution whenever a customer is exposit tae an creditit bi mair than ane channel, which is common; MER avoids this bi design.
- **Comparin MER again a fixed universal benchmark**: an appropriate MER varies bi business model, margin, an growth stage; uise it as a trend for ae organization ower time raither than a fixed pass/fail threshold.
- **Uisin MER as a channel-level diagnostic**: MER is deliberately an organization-wide figure an canna bi itsel identify which channel is drivin an efficiency chynge; pair it wi channel-level analysis an incrementality testin for that purpose.
- **Ignorin non-merketin revenue drivers**: a stable or improvin MER at growin revenue can reflect organic growth (referrals, word o mooth, earnt media) raither than merketin efficiency; break revenue doun bi acquisition source whaur ye can tae avoid ower-creditin merketin.
