# Adadin Bin Umarnin Shan Magani

Adadin bin umarnin shan magani yana auna yadda mara lafiya ke shan maganin da aka rubuta kamar yadda aka umarta, galibi ana bayyana shi a matsayin kaso na kwanakin cikin wani lokaci da aka ayyana da mara lafiya ya samu maganinsa kamar yadda aka rubuta. Yana ɗaya daga cikin ma'aunin lafiyar dijital mafi sakamako domin rashin bin umarni ya zama ruwan dare, ana iya kauce masa sosai da tallafin da ya dace, kuma yana da alaƙa kai tsaye da mummunan sakamakon asibiti da ƙarin kuɗi na ƙasa — wanda shi ne ainihin giɓin da manhajojin tunatarwa na magani, kwalaben magani masu wayo, da tunatarwa ta sake cika magani a kantin magani aka gina don rufewa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Hukumomin lafiyar jama'a sun ƙiyasta cewa rashin bin umarnin magani na cututtuka masu ɗorewa na iya kaiwa 50% ga wasu yanayi, kuma shi ne babban sanadin kwantarwa a asibiti da za a iya kauce wa, ci gaban cuta, da gazawar magani wanda ake danganta shi kuskure ga maganin kansa maimakon amfani mara daidaito. Kayan aikin dijital na bin umarni suna wanzuwa musamman don rufe wannan giɓi, don haka ga kowane shiri da ya ƙunshi ɓangaren magani, adadin bin umarni yawanci shi ne ma'auni mafi dacewa da yanke shawara: yana kan sama a sanadi na ingantuwar ma'aunin halittar jiki, sake kwantarwa, da yawancin sauran ma'aunin sakamakon asibiti da shiri zai iya bayar da rahoto. Shirin da ke inganta hulɗa ko gamsuwa ba tare da motsa bin umarni ba tabbas bai riga ya nuna hanyar da ta dace ta fa'idar asibiti ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Proportion of Days Covered (PDC) = days in period with medication on hand
                                    (based on days' supply from fills) / days
                                    in the measurement period × 100

Medication Possession Ratio (MPR) = total days' supply obtained during period
                                    / days in period × 100 (can exceed 100%
                                    with early refills; PDC is generally
                                    preferred for this reason)

A patient is typically classed "adherent" at a PDC threshold of ≥ 80%,
following widely used quality-measure convention.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

An rubuta wa mara lafiya magani na yau da kullum na cuta mai ɗorewa a tsawon lokacin auna na kwanaki 90. Rajistar karɓar magani a kantin magani ta nuna mara lafiya ya samu isasshen magani don rufe kwanaki 76 daga cikin 90, tare da giɓi biyu: giɓi na kwanaki 9 bayan maganin ya ƙare kafin sake cikawa, da giɓi na kwanaki 5 a kewayen shigar asibiti. PDC shi ne 76 / 90 × 100 = 84%, wanda ya wuce ƙofar bin umarni ta 80% ta al'ada. Idan an auna giɓin ɗaya ta amfani da MPR bisa kwanakin samarwa da aka bayar maimakon kwanakin da aka rufe da gaske, sake cika da wuri a wani wuri a cikin lokacin zai iya tura rabon sama da 100%, wanda ke nuna dalilin da ya sa PDC shi ne ma'auni mafi taka-tsantsan kuma wanda aka fi so gabaɗaya.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Da'awar kantin magani ko bayanan karɓar magani (ko daga mai sarrafa fa'idar kantin magani ko tsarin kantin magani da aka haɗa) shi ne daidaitaccen tushe, domin yana nuna abin da mara lafiya ya samu da gaske maimakon abin da aka rubuta masa; bayanan rubutaccen magani kaɗai suna ƙara bin umarni domin ba su tabbatar da cewa mara lafiya ya taɓa karɓar maganin ba. Kayan aikin bin umarni na dijital — kwalaben magani masu wayo, na'urorin da za a haɗiye, na'urorin shaƙar magani masu wayo da aka haɗa da ke rubuta kowane amfani ga cututtukan numfashi kamar asma da COPD, da duba-ta-manhaja — suna ba da bayanai masu ƙuduri mafi girma kan ko an sha kashi da gaske, ba kawai an samu ba, amma ƙaramin ɓangare, mai yuwuwar ba wakilci ba, na marasa lafiya ne ke amfani da su, don haka haɗa bin umarni da aka tabbatar ta na'ura da PDC na bisa da'awa a yawan jama'a yana buƙatar kulawa wajen fassara. Ya kamata a auna bin umarni a tsawon lokacin da ya isa ya daidaita kashi ɗaya da aka rasa amma gajere don gano raguwa mai ma'ana kafin ta haifar da cutarwa ta asibiti — tagogin birgima na kwanaki 90 sun zama ruwan dare ga magunguna masu ɗorewa.

## Kura-kurai

- **Amfani da MPR ba tare da bayyana cewa zai iya wuce 100% ba**: rabo sama da 100% da ba a bayyana ba daga sake cika da wuri ko tara magani yana sa kwatance tsakanin marasa lafiya da lokuta ya zama marar aminci sai an yi amfani da PDC ko an iyakance rabon a fili.
- **Ɗaukar bayanan rubutaccen magani ko oda a matsayin hujjar bin umarni**: magani da aka rubuta ko aka aika zuwa kantin magani ba ya faɗi komai game da ko mara lafiya ya karɓa ko ya sha maganin; bayanan karɓa ko na'ura ne kaɗai ke rufe wannan giɓi.
- **Amfani da ƙofar bin umarni ɗaya ga duk yanayi ba tare da bambanci ba**: sakamakon asibiti na rasa 20% na kashi ya bambanta sosai ta ajin magani (misali magungunan hana daskarewar jini da statins), don haka ƙofar 80% ɗaya da ake amfani da ita a duniya na iya ƙididdige haɗarin asibiti ƙasa ko sama ga wasu magunguna.
- **Yin watsi da sauya magani da dainawa**: mara lafiyar da aka sauya wa magani daban a asibiti da kuma daidai na iya bayyana a matsayin babbar raguwar bin umarni ga maganin farko idan ba a yi la'akari da sauyawar a lissafi ba.
