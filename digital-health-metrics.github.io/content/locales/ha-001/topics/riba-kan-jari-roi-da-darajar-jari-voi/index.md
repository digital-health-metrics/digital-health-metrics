# Riba kan Jari (ROI) da Darajar Jari (VOI)

Riba kan jari (ROI) ita ce ribar kuɗi ta net da dandalin lafiyar dijital ke samarwa idan aka kwatanta da jimillar kuɗinsa — lasisi, tura kayan aiki, aiwatarwa, da ma'aikata masu ci gaba — wanda aka bayyana a matsayin rabo ko kaso. Darajar jari (VOI) ma'auni ne mai faɗi, abokin tafiya, wanda ke ɗaukar dawowa da lissafin ROI na kuɗi tsantsa ba zai iya ɗauka ba: ingantuwar ingancin kulawa, gamsuwar marasa lafiya da likitoci, tasirin daidaiton lafiya, da martabar ƙungiya, babu ɗayansu da ke canzawa zuwa adadin dala cikin tsabta amma duk suna da muhimmanci ga ko ya kamata a ɗauki jarin lafiyar dijital a matsayin nasara. Tare suna amsa tambayoyi biyu daban-daban, masu cikawa: ROI tana tambaya "shin wannan ya biya kansa a kuɗi?"; VOI tana tambaya "shin wannan ya cancanci a yi, idan aka yi la'akari da duk abin da ke da muhimmanci?"

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Jarin lafiyar dijital da ke nuna ROI mara kyau ko mara kyau a shekararsa ta farko ba lallai ne gazawa ba ce, kuma wanda ke nuna ROI mai ƙarfi ba lallai ne nasara ba ce, idan lissafin ROI ya yi watsi da muhimmiyar daraja da shirin ke samarwa a wajen rage kuɗi tsantsa — wannan shi ne daidai giɓin da VOI ke wanzuwa don rufewa, kuma ƙungiyoyin da ke dogara da ROI kaɗai suna haɗarin dakatar da kuɗin shirye-shirye masu daraja ta gaske waɗanda kawai ba su bayyana ba tukuna, ko ba za su taɓa bayyana ba, a matsayin dawowar kuɗi kai tsaye. A lokaci guda, VOI ya fi na zahiri kuma ya fi wahalar kwatantawa fiye da ROI, wanda ke sa shi mai saurin amfani don tabbatar da ci gaba da saka jari a shirin da ba ya samar da daraja mai aunuwa ta kowace irin ba; ladabin bayar da rahoton duka tare, tare da abubuwan VOI an mai da su bayyanannu kuma masu aunuwa gwargwadon iko, shi ne ke kiyaye wannan tsari da gaskiya maimakon zama hanyar guje wa lissafin kuɗi. Shawarwarin saka jarin lafiyar dijital na dogon lokaci — musamman a kulawa bisa ƙima, inda sakamakon inganci da daidaito ke da alaƙa kai tsaye da biyan kuɗi — suna ƙara buƙatar duka adadin biyu gefe-da-gefe.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
ROI = (financial benefit − total investment cost) / total investment cost × 100

Financial benefit sources: cost per episode of care reduction, bed-day
reduction (converted to a cost figure), avoided ED visits (see ED
diversion rate), reduced readmission penalties, and any direct revenue.

Total investment cost: platform licensing, hardware/device deployment
and maintenance, implementation and integration cost, ongoing clinical
and technical staffing.

VOI is not a single formula, but a structured, multi-dimensional
scorecard combining ROI with defined, tracked non-financial value
domains, for example:
  Clinical quality  (e.g. biometric improvement rate, readmission rate)
  Patient experience (e.g. patient net promoter score, SUS score)
  Equity           (e.g. digital access rate, digital literacy rate)
  Workforce         (e.g. physician burnout rate)
Each domain is reported on its own terms, not artificially converted
to a dollar figure, alongside the financial ROI figure.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Shirin sa ido daga nesa yana kashe $600,000 a shekararsa ta farko (lasisin dandali, tura na'ura, da ƙarin ma'aikatan asibiti) kuma yana samar da fa'idar kuɗi mai ƙididdigewa ta $750,000 (rage kwanakin gado da guje wa hukuncin sake kwantarwa). ROI shi ne ($750,000 − $600,000) / $600,000 × 100 = 25%. Tare da wannan adadi, katin maki na VOI na ƙungiyar yana bayar da rahoton ingantuwar maki 5 a makin Net Promoter na marasa lafiya, adadin daidaituwar ma'aunin halittar jiki na 68% ga marasa lafiyar hawan jini da aka yi wa rajista, da raguwa da aka auna a lokacin rubutu bayan awanni na likitocin da aka yi wa rajista. Babu ɗayan waɗannan ƙarin binciken guda uku da ke canza adadin ROI na 25%, amma tare suna gina hujja mafi ƙarfi sosai don ci gaba da saka jari fiye da yadda adadin ROI kaɗai zai goyi baya, musamman ga masu ruwa da tsaki (shugabancin asibiti, ƙungiyoyin kare marasa lafiya) waɗanda ke ba sakamakon inganci da daidaito nauyi kamar dawowar kuɗi.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Ɓangaren fa'idar kuɗi na ROI yana ɗaukar bayanan kuɗi da sakamako iri ɗaya da aka yi amfani da su a wani wuri a wannan littafi — kuɗin kowane zangon kulawa, rage kwanakin gado, adadin karkatar da ED — da aka mayar zuwa adadin dala ta amfani da hanyar lissafin kuɗi ta ƙungiyar kanta, kuma ya kamata ya yi amfani da zato masu taka-tsantsan, masu kariya maimakon na fata mafi kyau, tun da adadin ROI da aka gina a kan zato masu tsanani ba zai tsira daga bincike na ƙungiyar kuɗi mai shakka ba. Ya kamata kowane fannin VOI na rashin kuɗi ya yi amfani da ma'aunin da aka riga aka tabbatar daga wani wuri a wannan littafi (adadin daidaituwar ma'aunin halittar jiki, makin Net Promoter na marasa lafiya, adadin samun damar dijital, adadin gajiyawar likitoci) maimakon wani madadin da aka ƙirƙira ba bisa ƙa'ida ba, don katin maki na VOI kansa ya kasance mai iya dubawa da kwatantawa a tsawon lokaci maimakon zama labari maras ma'ana.

## Kura-kurai

- **Bayar da rahoton ROI kaɗai ga shirin da ke da muhimmiyar daraja ta rashin kuɗi**: wannan yana haɗarin dakatar da kuɗin shirin da ke da ingancin asibiti, daidaito, ko ma'aikata na gaske kawai saboda wannan darajar ba ta bayyana a lissafin kuɗi tsantsa ba; a haɗa ROI da katin maki na VOI mai tsari.
- **Ɗaukar VOI a matsayin hanyar guje wa lissafin kuɗi**: ya kamata VOI ya yi amfani da ma'auni bayyanannu, da aka tabbatar a baya ga kowane fanni na rashin kuɗi, ba iƙirarin daraja maras tabbas ko wanda ba a auna ba, ko ya zama hanyar tabbatar da ci gaba da kashe kuɗi ba tare da shaida ta gaske ba.
- **Amfani da zato na fata ko wanda ba a tabbatar ba a lissafin guje wa kuɗi na ROI**: adadin ROI da aka gina a kan zato masu tsanani game da kuɗin da aka guje wa ba zai jure bincike ba kuma zai iya lalata amincin hujjojin saka jari na gaba.
- **Kwatanta adadin ROI tsakanin shirye-shirye masu tsarin kuɗi daban-daban sosai ba tare da daidaitawa ba**: shirin da ke da babban kuɗin jari na farko da dogon lokacin dawowa bai kamata a kwatanta shi a adadin ROI na shekara ɗaya da wanda ke da ƙaramin kuɗi na farko da saurin dawowa ba tare da la'akari da lokutan hangen nesa daban-daban da abin ya shafa ba.
