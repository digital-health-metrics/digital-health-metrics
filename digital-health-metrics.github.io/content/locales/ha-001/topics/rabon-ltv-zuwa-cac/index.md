# Rabon LTV zuwa CAC

Rabon LTV zuwa CAC yana kwatanta darajar rayuwar abokin ciniki (LTV) — jimillar kuɗin shiga ko riba da ƙungiya ke tsammanin samu daga mara lafiya ko abokin ciniki a tsawon dukan alaƙarsa da samfurin — da ainihin kuɗin samun wannan abokin ciniki (duba ainihin kuɗin samun abokin ciniki). Shi ne mafi muhimmin ma'aunin tattalin arzikin raka'a don tantance ko girman ƙungiyar lafiyar dijital yana dorewa a kuɗi, domin ƙaruwar tushen abokan ciniki da aka samu a asara ba alamar lafiya ba ce, komai kyawun lanƙwasar girma.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ƙungiyar lafiyar dijital na iya ƙara tushen masu amfani a hankali yayin da take lalata daraja a hankali a kowane sabon abokin ciniki, idan kuɗin samu ya wuce darajar rayuwa; rabon LTV zuwa CAC shi ne ma'aunin da ke sa wannan ya bayyana ta hanyar da adadin girma ko ƙididdigar abokan ciniki ɗanye kaɗai ba za su iya ba. Rabo na 3:1 (darajar rayuwa aƙalla sau uku na kuɗin samu) shi ne ma'aunin tushe da ake ambata sosai ga kasuwancin biyan kuɗi ko kuɗin shiga mai maimaitawa mai dorewa, wanda ke ba da isasshen riba don biyan kuɗin aiki ban da samu kuma har yanzu ya samar da dawowa; rabo ƙasa da 1:1 yana nufin ƙungiyar tana asara a kowane abokin ciniki da ta samu, kuma rabo fiye da 3:1 sosai (misali 10:1 ko fiye) na iya nuna ƙarancin saka jari a girma, tun da yana nuna cewa ƙungiyar za ta iya samun ƙarin abokan ciniki da riba fiye da yadda take yi a yanzu. Masu zuba jari, allon gudanarwa, da masu biyan kuɗi da ke tantance dorewar kuɗi na kamfanin lafiyar dijital suna ɗaukar wannan rabo a matsayin ɗaya daga cikin lambobin farko da suke nema.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
LTV = average revenue (or margin) per customer per period × average
      customer lifetime in that same period unit

LTV to CAC ratio = LTV / true CAC

A ratio of 3:1 is the commonly cited sustainable baseline; below 1:1
indicates the organization loses money on acquisition; well above 3:1
(e.g. 10:1+) can indicate under-investment in growth.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani sabis na biyan kuɗi na lafiyar dijital yana samar da matsakaicin kuɗin shiga na wata-wata na $40 ga kowane mara lafiya, kuma matsakaicin mara lafiya yana ci gaba da biyan kuɗi na watanni 18, wanda ke ba da LTV na $40 × 18 = $720. An lissafa ainihin CAC na wannan sabis (duba hanyar misali da aka yi aiki na wannan batu) a $180 ga kowane mara lafiya da aka samu. Rabon LTV zuwa CAC shi ne $720 / $180 = 4:1, sama da ma'aunin dorewa na 3:1 sosai. Idan an lissafa ainihin CAC ta amfani da kuɗin da dandalin talla ya bayar kawai ($120, kafin ƙara kuɗin hukuma da aikin shiga), rabon zai bayyana a matsayin 6:1 — hoto mai fa'ida sosai, kuma mai ruɗarwa, na tattalin arzikin raka'a fiye da ainihin adadin 4:1.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

LTV ya dogara ne kan zato game da matsakaicin rayuwar abokin ciniki, wanda shi kansa ya samo asali ne daga bayanan riƙewa ko barin ƙungiyar kanta (duba adadin riƙe masu amfani) — kasuwanci mai yawan barin yana da gajeriyar matsakaicin rayuwa mai tasiri don haka ƙaramin LTV, ko da kuɗin shiga na kowane lokaci ga kowane abokin ciniki yana kama da lafiya. Domin LTV ƙiyasin gaba ne ba gaskiyar tarihi da aka gani ba, ya kamata a sake lissafa shi akai-akai yayin da bayanan riƙewa ke taruwa kuma a sake duba shi idan zato game da barin ya tabbata kuskure, maimakon a kafa shi sau ɗaya a bar shi ya tsufa. Amfani da CAC da dandali ya bayar maimakon ainihin CAC a cikin wannan rabo yana ɗaya daga cikin hanyoyin da suka fi yawa da ƙungiya za ta iya gamsar da kanta cewa tattalin arzikin rakunanta ya fi lafiya fiye da yadda yake, tun da CAC da aka ƙididdige ƙasa yana ƙara rabon a tsari.

## Kura-kurai

- **Amfani da CAC da dandali ya bayar maimakon ainihin CAC**: wannan yana ƙara rabon a tsari kuma yana iya sa dabarar samu mara dorewa ta yi kama da mai dorewa; koyaushe a yi amfani da cikakken adadin ainihin CAC.
- **Amfani da zaton matsakaicin rayuwar abokin ciniki da ya tsufa ko mai fata fiye**: LTV da aka lissafa daga lanƙwasar riƙewa da ta tsufa ba za ta nuna halayyar barin ta yanzu ba, musamman bayan canjin samfur, farashi, ko kasuwa da ke sauya riƙewa.
- **Ɗaukar rabo mai girma sosai a matsayin mai kyau ba tare da shakka ba**: rabo fiye da 3:1 sosai na iya nuna ƙarancin saka jari a girma maimakon ingancin da ya wuce, tun da yana nuna cewa ƙungiyar za ta iya samun ƙarin abokan ciniki da riba fiye da yadda take yi a yanzu.
- **Lissafin rabo ɗaya na haɗaka a kan ɓangarorin abokan ciniki masu bambanci sosai**: ɓangare mai kuɗin shiga mai yawa da ƙarancin barin na iya ɓoye wani ɓangare da ke da mummunan tattalin arzikin raka'a; a lissafa rabon a kowane muhimmin ɓangare (misali ta hanyar samu ko layin samfur) a duk inda yawa ya ba da dama.
