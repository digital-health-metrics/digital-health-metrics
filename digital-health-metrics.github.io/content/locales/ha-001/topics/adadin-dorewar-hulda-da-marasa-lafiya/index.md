# Adadin Dorewar Hulɗa da Marasa Lafiya

Adadin dorewar hulɗa da marasa lafiya yana auna yadda mara lafiya da aka yi wa rajista ke mu'amala akai-akai da samfurin lafiyar dijital a tsawon lokaci — misali shigar da abinci ko alamomi, rubuta motsa jiki, ko duba bayanan lafiya — maimakon kawai ko ya taɓa amfani da shi kwata-kwata. Ma'aunin tsawon lokaci ne, wanda ya bambanta da ƙidayar amfani mai aiki na lokaci ɗaya: marasa lafiya biyu na iya samun matsayin "ya yi amfani da manhaja a wannan wata" iri ɗaya yayin da ɗaya ke shigar da bayanai akai-akai kowace rana ɗayan kuma ya shigar da sau ɗaya ya ɓace na makonni uku, kuma ma'aunin dorewa ne kaɗai ke bambance su.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Hulɗa mai ɗorewa, ta yau da kullum da kayan aikin lafiyar dijital tana ɗaya daga cikin alamomin farko mafi aminci na fa'idar asibiti, musamman ga yanayin da suka dogara da halayya kamar ciwon sukari, sarrafa nauyi, da lafiyar hankali, inda darajar kayan aikin ke zuwa daga al'adar da yake tallafawa maimakon kowane zaman guda ɗaya. Samfur na iya bayar da rahoton adadin masu amfani masu aiki na wata-wata mai lafiya yayin da yake hidimtawa yawan jama'a da ke shiga sau ɗaya su yi ɓacewa, domin amfani mai aiki na wata-wata ƙaramin ma'auni ne wanda ba ya faɗi komai game da tsarin amfani a cikin watan; ma'aunin dorewa yana kama wannan ta hanyar da ƙidayar aiki mai sauƙi ba za ta iya ba. Domin dorewa kuma yana ɗaya daga cikin abubuwa masu wahala don ci gaba da shi a tsawon watanni maimakon makonni, alama ce mafi gaskiya ta ingancin samfur da dacewar asibiti fiye da adadin hulɗa na gajeren taga, waɗanda ke da saurin tasirin sabon abu kai tsaye bayan shiga.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Engagement consistency rate = weeks with at least one qualifying interaction
                               / total weeks enrolled × 100

A "qualifying interaction" should be defined explicitly and consistently
(e.g. a food log entry, a symptom check-in, or a completed activity sync) —
never a passive event like an app open with no logged action.

Report as a distribution, not only a population mean:
  e.g. share of patients with ≥ 80% weekly consistency,
       share with 50-79%, share with < 50%
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wata manhajar horon abinci mai gina jiki tana yi wa mara lafiya rajista na makonni 12. Mara lafiyar yana shigar da aƙalla shigar abinci ɗaya mai cancanta a makonni 9 daga cikin 12, wanda ke ba da adadin dorewar hulɗa na mutum ɗaya na 9 / 12 × 100 = 75%. A duk ƙungiyar manhajar ta marasa lafiya 2,000 da aka yi wa rajista aƙalla makonni 12, marasa lafiya 600 (30%) suna kiyaye dorewar mako-mako ≥ 80%, 900 (45%) suna cikin kewayen 50-79%, kuma 500 (25%) suna ƙasa da 50%. Bayar da rahoton matsakaicin ƙungiya kaɗai (wanda zai iya kasancewa kusa da 65%) zai ɓoye cewa cikakken kwata na marasa lafiya da kyar suke mu'amala — ɓangare da ya cancanci bincike daban maimakon a narkar da shi a matsakaicin gaba ɗaya.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Bayanan dorewa suna zuwa daga rajistar abubuwan da suka faru na samfurin kansa (shigar abinci, daidaita aiki, duba-ta-manhaja), kuma ma'anar "hulɗa mai cancanta" tana da tasiri mai girma sosai kan adadin da ya haifar — ma'anar sassauƙa (kowane buɗe manhaja) koyaushe za ta yi kyau fiye da mai tsauri (shigar da bayanai kammalalle, mai ma'ana), don haka dole a bayyana ma'anar da aka yi amfani da ita a fili tare da kowane adadi da aka bayar da rahoto. Ya kamata a bayar da rahoton bayanan da aka daidaita ta atomatik (misali na'urar bibiyar motsa jiki da aka haɗa tana daidaita aiki a bango) daban da bayanan da aka shigar da hannu, tun da daidaitawa ta atomatik na iya ƙara dorewa da ake gani ba tare da nuna wani ƙoƙarin mara lafiya na gaske ko hulɗa da jagorar samfurin ba.

## Kura-kurai

- **Rikita buɗe manhaja da hulɗa mai ma'ana**: buɗe manhaja ba da gangan ba (misali sanarwar turawa ta haifar da shi) ba ɗaya yake da shigar abinci ko duba-ta-manhaja da aka kammala ba; a ayyana a kuma bayar da rahoto kan hulɗa masu cancanta kawai.
- **Bayar da rahoton matsakaicin yawan jama'a kawai**: matsakaicin adadin dorewa mai kama da lafiya na iya ɓoye yawan jama'a masu rabi biyu na marasa lafiya masu hulɗa sosai da kusan gabaɗaya marasa hulɗa; a bayar da rahoton rarrabawa a kewayen dorewa, ba kawai matsakaici ba.
- **Yin watsi da mai raba tsawon rajista**: kwatanta adadin dorewa tsakanin marasa lafiyar da aka yi wa rajista na tsawon lokuta daban-daban ba tare da la'akari da tsawon rajista ba zai karkata zuwa ƙungiyar da ke da gajeren taga auna mai sauƙin ci gaba.
- **Daidaita bango ta atomatik yana ƙara adadin**: rafin bayanan na'urar sawa da aka daidaita a hankali na iya sa mara lafiya mara hulɗa ya bayyana yana aiki akai-akai ba tare da wani canjin halayya na gaske ko hulɗa da samfur daga gare shi ba.
