# Adadin Daidaituwar Ma'aunin Halittar Jiki

Adadin daidaituwar ma'aunin halittar jiki shi ne kaso na marasa lafiyar da aka yi wa rajista waɗanda suka cimma kuma suka kiyaye kewayen manufa da aka ayyana a asibiti don ma'aunin halittar jiki — galibi hawan jini ƙasa da ƙofa kamar 130/80 mmHg — ta amfani da na'urar sa ido da aka haɗa, a tsawon lokaci mai ɗorewa ba a lokaci ɗaya kawai ba. Ya bambanta da adadin inganta ma'aunin halittar jiki (duba wannan batu): ingantuwa tana auna girman canji daga matakin farko, yayin da daidaituwa ke auna ko ana kiyaye mara lafiya a cikin kewayen aminci da dogaro bayan an fara magani ko sa ido, wanda shi ne sakamakon da ya fi muhimmanci ga marasa lafiyar da suka riga suka kusa da manufa ko suke kan magani.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ga babban kaso na marasa lafiya a shirye-shiryen cututtuka masu ɗorewa — musamman hawan jini, inda manufofin hawan jini na jagora aka kafa su sosai kuma suna da alaƙa kai tsaye da haɗarin zuciya da jijiyoyin jini — manufar asibiti ba ingantuwa ta sau ɗaya ba ce amma sarrafa mai ɗorewa, kuma mara lafiyar da ke shiga da fita daga kewayen manufa yana da haɗari daban sosai da wanda ya inganta sau ɗaya ya tsaya a can. Na'urorin da aka haɗa (ma'aunin hawan jini na cellular, na'urorin sa ido kan sukari a ci gaba) suna ba da damar auna daidaituwa a ci gaba maimakon kawai a ziyarar asibiti, suna gano marasa lafiyar da karatun asibiti ya nuna an sarrafa su amma karatun gida ba shi da tabbaci — tsari da ake kira hawan jini ɓoyayye (masked hypertension) wanda auna ido-da-ido lokaci-lokaci kaɗai ba zai iya ganowa ba. Bayar da rahoton adadin daidaituwa maimakon kawai hoton "a manufa" na lokaci ɗaya yana tilasta wa shiri fuskantar yadda yake kiyaye marasa lafiya a kewayen da daidaito, ba kawai sau nawa ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Biometric stabilization rate = patients with ≥ 80% of readings within
                                target range over the measurement period /
                                patients with a minimum number of valid
                                readings in that period × 100

Example thresholds:
  Blood pressure — target < 130/80 mmHg (or the applicable clinical
                   guideline threshold for the patient's risk profile)
  Glucose        — target range per continuous glucose monitoring
                   guidance, reported as "time in range"

A minimum reading-frequency threshold (e.g. at least 3 readings per
week) should be set before a patient is included in the denominator,
to avoid infrequent readers appearing artificially stable.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani shirin sa ido daga nesa kan hawan jini ya yi rajistar marasa lafiya 600 masu ma'aunin hawan jini na cellular, kowannensu ana tsammanin zai ɗauki karatu aƙalla 3 a mako. Daga cikinsu, 540 sun cika mafi ƙarancin ƙofar yawan karatu a tsawon lokacin auna na watanni 3 kuma an haɗa su a mai raba. Daga cikin waɗannan 540, 350 suna da aƙalla kashi 80% na karatunsu ƙasa da 130/80 mmHg, wanda ke ba da adadin daidaituwar ma'aunin halittar jiki na 350 / 540 × 100 = 65%. Marasa lafiya 60 da aka cire saboda karatu bai isa ba ana bayar da rahotonsu daban a matsayin gibin cikar bayanai, ba a haɗa su a cikin mai lissafi ko ƙungiyar "marasa daidaituwa" ba, domin ainihin yanayin sarrafa su ba a sani ba da gaske maimakon mara kyau.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Karatu yana zuwa kai tsaye daga rafin bayanan na'urar da aka haɗa kanta, wanda ya fi zama na haƙiƙa kuma ya fi yawa fiye da auna a asibiti, amma kurakuran sanya na'ura da dabarar amfani (ma'aunin hawan jini mai girman da bai dace ba ko wanda aka sanya a wuri mara kyau) na iya haifar da son zuciya na tsari wanda karatun tabbatarwa guda ɗaya a asibiti ba lallai ne ya kama shi ba. Zaɓin kewayen manufa ya kamata ya bi jagorar asibiti na yanzu da ya dace da takamaiman bayanin haɗarin mara lafiya da cututtukan da ke tare da shi maimakon ƙofa ɗaya ta duniya, tun da manufofin jagora sun bambanta da shekarun mara lafiya, aikin koda, da haɗarin zuciya da jijiyoyin jini. Bai kamata a ƙidaya mara lafiya mai ƙarancin yawan karatu a matsayin "mai daidaituwa" ba ta tsohuwa a ɓoye; cire shi daga mai raba tare da bayyananne rahoton cirewar ya fi gaskiya fiye da ƙidaya shi a matsayin wanda aka sarrafa ko wanda ba a sarrafa ba bisa bayanai kaɗan.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ɗaukar karatu ɗaya a cikin kewaye a matsayin daidaituwa**: daidaituwa tana nufin sarrafa mai ɗorewa a tsawon lokaci da aka ayyana, ba hoto na lokaci ɗaya ba; koyaushe a buƙaci mafi ƙarancin kaso na karatu a cikin kewaye a tsawon wannan lokacin, ba ma'auni ɗaya mai cancanta ba.
- **Cire waɗanda ba sa yawan karatu a ɓoye ba tare da bayar da rahoto ba**: marasa lafiyar da da wuya suke ɗaukar karatu ba su da daidaituwa ko rashin daidaituwa ta atomatik; a cire su a fili daga mai raba a kuma bayar da rahoton adadin cirewa a matsayin ma'aunin cikar bayanai daban.
- **Yin watsi da daidaitawar na'ura da kuskuren dabara**: ma'aunin da bai dace da hannu ba ko na'urar da ba a daidaita ba na iya karkatar da karatu a tsari zuwa wata hanya, wanda adadin daidaituwa da aka lissafa kai tsaye daga ɗanyen bayanan na'ura ba zai kama ba tare da tabbatarwa lokaci-lokaci ba.
- **Amfani da kewayen manufa ɗaya na duniya ga duka marasa lafiya**: manufofin jagorar asibiti sun bambanta da bayanin haɗarin mara lafiya da cututtukan da ke tare da shi; amfani da ƙofa ɗaya ga yawan jama'a masu bambancin asibiti zai ɓata rarraba wasu marasa lafiya a matsayin masu daidaituwa ko marasa daidaituwa bisa ainihin manufarsu ta kowane mutum.

## Tushe

- American Heart Association (AHA) / American College of Cardiology (ACC), manufofin jagorar hawan jini da jagorar sa ido kan hawan jini a gida
- International Diabetes Federation da American Diabetes Association (ADA), jagorar yarjejeniya kan sa ido kan sukari a ci gaba "lokaci a cikin kewaye" (time in range)
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan sa ido kan ma'aunin halittar jiki daga nesa da sarrafa cuta mai ɗorewa, misali nazarin da aka buga a npj Digital Medicine
