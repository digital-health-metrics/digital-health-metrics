# Adadin Inganta Ma'aunin Halittar Jiki

Adadin inganta ma'aunin halittar jiki shi ne kaso na marasa lafiyar da aka yi wa rajista a shirin lafiyar dijital waɗanda suka samu ingantuwa mai muhimmanci a asibiti a wani ma'aunin halittar jiki da ake bibiya a tsawon lokacin rajista da aka ƙayyade — galibi haemoglobin mai glycated (HbA1c) a shirye-shiryen ciwon sukari da na cututtukan zuciya da na sinadarai a jiki, ko ma'aunin nauyin jiki (BMI) a shirye-shiryen sarrafa nauyi. Shi ne ma'aunin sakamako da ke tabbatar da iƙirarin asibiti na samfurin lafiyar dijital a ƙarshe: adadin hulɗa da karɓa suna bayyana yadda ake amfani da samfur, amma ingantuwar ma'aunin halittar jiki ya fi kusa da shaidar cewa yana aiki.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ana sayar da shirye-shiryen lafiyar dijital da kuma ba da kwangilarsu bisa alƙawarin ingantattun sakamakon lafiya, kuma adadin inganta ma'aunin halittar jiki shi ne hanya mafi kai tsaye kuma mai ƙididdigewa ta gwada wannan alƙawari da wani ƙayyadadden matakin da aka san shi a asibiti, maimakon iƙirari maras tushe na "ingantacciyar lafiya". Masu biyan kuɗi, ma'aikata, da tsarin kiwon lafiya suna ƙara haɗa biyan kuɗi ko sabunta kwangila da canjin ma'aunin halittar jiki da aka nuna, don haka shirin da ba zai iya bayar da rahoton wannan adadi cikin sahihanci ba yana cikin rashin fa'ida ta kasuwanci da kuma ta asibiti. Ma'aunin kuma yana zama bincike na ladabi kan ƙirar shirin: ya fi sauƙi a bayar da rahoton hulɗa (shiga, saƙonnin da aka aika) fiye da sakamako, don haka ya kamata ƙungiya ta yi shakku kan duk wani shiri da ke bayar da rahoton na farko cikin sha'awa yayin da yake rashin bayyana na biyun.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Biometric improvement rate = patients achieving a defined clinically meaningful
                              improvement / patients with a valid baseline and
                              follow-up measurement × 100

Common clinically meaningful thresholds:
  HbA1c   — a reduction of ≥ 0.5 percentage points, or reaching a defined
            target (e.g. < 7.0%) from an out-of-range baseline
  BMI     — a reduction of ≥ 5% of baseline body weight, sustained to the
            follow-up measurement point

Report separately for each biometric tracked; never blend HbA1c and BMI
improvement into a single combined "improvement" percentage.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani shirin lafiyar dijital na cututtukan zuciya da sinadarai a jiki ya yi rajistar marasa lafiya 800 masu HbA1c na farko wanda ya wuce kewayen da aka yarda da shi. Daga cikinsu, 620 suna da ma'aunin farko mai inganci da ma'aunin bibiya a watanni 6 (180 sun ɓace daga bibiya kuma an cire su daga mai raba, ba a ƙidaya su a matsayin waɗanda suka gaza ba). Daga cikin 620 masu ma'auni biyu, 340 sun samu raguwa ta aƙalla maki 0.5 na kaso. Adadin inganta ma'aunin halittar jiki shi ne 340 / 620 × 100 = 55%. Bayar da rahoton wannan adadi bisa duka 800 da aka yi wa rajista (340 / 800 = 42.5%) zai haɗa ɓacewa daga bibiya da gazawar magani, yana rage ƙimar adadin ga marasa lafiyar da suka kammala auna da gaske.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Ƙimar ma'aunin halittar jiki ta farko da ta bibiya yawanci tana zuwa ne daga na'ura da aka haɗa (na'urar auna sukari ko ma'aunin nauyi mai wayo ta Bluetooth), sakamakon dakin gwaji da aka shigo da shi daga tarihin lafiya na lantarki, ko ƙima da mara lafiya ya bayar da kansa — kuma waɗannan tushe uku suna da amincewa daban-daban, don haka ya kamata a bayar da rahoton tushen tare da adadin. Ɓacewa daga bibiya da wuya ya zama bazuwar: marasa lafiyar da suka daina hulɗa da shiri galibi su ne kuma waɗanda ba su da yuwuwar samun ingantuwa, don haka babban adadin ingantuwa da aka lissafa kawai kan marasa lafiyar da suka kammala bibiya na iya ƙara ƙimar tasirin shirin na gaskiya a matakin yawan jama'a. Tasirin yanayi da komawa zuwa matsakaici na gaske ne ga HbA1c da nauyi duka, don haka ya kamata shiri ya kwatanta da ƙungiyar sarrafawa ta lokaci guda ko ta tarihi idan zai yiwu maimakon ɗaukar duk wani ingantuwa a matsayin hujjar tasirin shirin.

## Kura-kurai

- **Cire, maimakon bayar da rahoton, ɓacewa daga bibiya**: cire marasa lafiyar da ba su da ma'aunin bibiya daga mai raba a ɓoye na iya ƙara adadin ingantuwa da ake gani sosai; koyaushe a bayar da rahoton adadin kammala ma'aunin bibiya tare da adadin ingantuwa kansa.
- **Haɗa ma'aunin da mara lafiya ya bayar da kansa da na na'ura ba tare da yi musu lakabi ba**: nauyin da aka bayar da kai ba shi da amincewa kamar karatun ma'aunin nauyi mai wayo da aka haɗa, kuma haɗa tushen biyu yana ɓoye yawan abin da ake gani a matsayin ingantuwa wanda ke da kuskuren aunawa kawai.
- **Rashin ƙungiyar sarrafawa ko kwatancen hasashe**: ma'aunin halittar jiki na ɗorewa da yawa suna canzawa ko suna komawa zuwa matsakaici da kansu; adadin ingantuwa na ƙungiya ɗaya ba tare da wata ƙungiyar kwatantawa ba shaida ce mai nuni, ba mai tabbatarwa ba, ta tasirin shirin.
- **Ɗaukar ƙaramin canjin matsakaici a matsayin shaidar ingantuwa mai faɗi**: ƙaramin ingantuwar matsakaici a matakin yawan jama'a na iya kasancewa saboda ƴan masu amsa sosai yayin da yawancin marasa lafiya ba sa ganin canji; a bayar da rahoton rarrabawa (misali kason da suka tsallaka ƙofar muhimmanci ta asibiti), ba kawai canjin matsakaici ba.
