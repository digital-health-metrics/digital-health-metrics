# Rabon Ingancin Talla

Rabon ingancin talla (MER) shi ne jimillar kuɗin shiga da aka raba da jimillar kashe kuɗin talla a kowace hanya, na wani lokaci da aka ayyana. Yana wanzuwa a matsayin bincike mai zaman kansa na gaskiya da gangan kan dawowar kashe kuɗin talla (ROAS) da dandali ke bayarwa, wanda ke auna gudummawar kowace hanya da ita kanta ta yi iƙirari ga kuɗin shiga kuma a tsari yana da saurin ƙara wa kansa daraja — MER maimakon haka yana duba jimillar kuɗin shiga akan jimillar kashe kuɗi, adadi da babu dandalin talla ɗaya da zai iya karkatarwa.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kowane dandalin talla yana bayar da rahoton ROAS ta amfani da tsarin danganta nasa, kuma saboda yawancin ƙungiyoyi suna gudanar da hanyoyi da yawa a lokaci guda, abokin ciniki ɗaya da ya canza yana yawan samun yabo daga dandali fiye da ɗaya, don haka jimillar ROAS da kowane dandali ya bayar da kansa yakan ƙara gudummawar talla gaba ɗaya idan aka kwatanta da ainihin jimillar kuɗin shiga. MER yana kawar da wannan matsala gaba ɗaya ta hanyar kwatanta jimillar kuɗin shiga da jimillar kashe kuɗi a matakin ƙungiya, yana sa ya fi wahalar karkatarwa ta wasan danganta ko rahoto mai fifita dandali, wanda shi ne daidai dalilin da ya sa ƙungiyoyin kuɗi da na gudanarwa ke ƙara ɗaukarsa a matsayin binciken inganci na saman da za a daidaita adadin kowane dandali da shi. Ƙungiyar talla da ke bayar da rahoton ROAS mai ƙarfi a kowace hanya yayin da MER gaba ɗaya ke raguwa tana da matsalar inganci ko haɗuwar danganta ta gaske wadda rahoton matakin hanya kaɗai ba zai bayyana ba.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
MER = total revenue / total marketing spend (all channels, same period)

Unlike ROAS, MER is not calculated per channel — it is a single,
organization-wide figure precisely because its value comes from being
immune to any one platform's attribution claims.

A rising MER over time, at stable or growing spend, indicates improving
overall marketing efficiency; a stable MER at growing revenue can
indicate that growth is coming from non-marketing-driven sources
(e.g. referrals, organic search, word of mouth).
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani kamfanin lafiyar dijital yana samar da kuɗin shiga na $2,400,000 a cikin kwata, tare da jimillar kashe kuɗin talla a duk hanyoyin da aka biya na $480,000. MER shi ne $2,400,000 / $480,000 = 5.0. Daban-daban, dandalin binciken da aka biya yana bayar da rahoton ROAS na 6.0, dandalin zamantakewa da aka biya yana bayar da rahoton ROAS na 5.5, kuma dandalin nunin talla na programmatic yana bayar da rahoton ROAS na 4.0 — idan aka ƙara su kawai a matsayin iƙirari kan gudummawar jimillar kuɗin shiga, za su nuna ƙarin kuɗin shiga da aka danganta fiye da abin da kamfanin ya samar da gaske, tun da wani muhimmin kaso na abokan ciniki da suka canza an nuna musu hanyoyi fiye da ɗaya kuma ana ƙidaya su sau biyu ko uku. MER na ƙungiya gaba ɗaya na 5.0 shi ne lambar da ke daidaita wannan: ba za a iya ƙara shi ta haɗuwar danganta kamar yadda adadin ROAS na matakin dandali ke iya yi a tsari ba.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Jimillar kuɗin shiga tana zuwa daga tsarin kuɗi ko lissafin kuɗi na ƙungiyar kanta, kuma jimillar kashe kuɗin talla tana zuwa daga ainihin kuɗin talla da aka aika da lissafi aka biya a kowace hanya — duka biyun ya kamata a samo su ba tare da dogara da allon kowane dandalin talla ba. Ya kamata a bibiyi MER a tsawon lokaci a matsayin yanayi maimakon a yi hukunci da ma'auni ɗaya na duniya, tun da MER "mai kyau" ya bambanta sosai da tsarin kasuwanci, tsarin riba, da matakin girma: kamfani na farko da ke saka jari sosai a girma na iya yarda da ƙaramin MER da gangan fiye da kamfani mai girma da ke inganta riba. MER ba ya gano wace hanya ce ke da alhakin canjin inganci — wannan aikin gano har yanzu yana buƙatar nazarin matakin hanya, wanda ya fi dacewa a ƙara masa gwajin ƙarin tasiri (ƙungiyoyin riƙewa da ba sa samun talla) maimakon danganta da dandali ya bayar kaɗai.

## Kura-kurai

- **Ɗaukar ROAS da dandali ya bayar a matsayin mai ƙarawa a tsakanin hanyoyi**: ƙara ROAS na kowane dandali da ya bayar da kansa yana ƙara gudummawar talla gaba ɗaya a duk lokacin da abokin ciniki ya fuskanci kuma aka yaba masa ta hanya fiye da ɗaya, wanda ya zama ruwan dare; MER yana guje wa wannan ta ƙira.
- **Kwatanta MER da ma'auni na duniya mai tsayayye**: MER da ya dace ya bambanta da tsarin kasuwanci, riba, da matakin girma; a yi amfani da shi a matsayin yanayi na ƙungiya ɗaya a tsawon lokaci maimakon ƙofar cin nasara/faɗuwa tsayayye.
- **Amfani da MER a matsayin binciken matakin hanya**: MER da gangan adadi ne na ƙungiya gaba ɗaya kuma ba zai iya gano wace hanya ce ke haifar da canjin inganci kaɗai ba; a haɗa shi da nazarin matakin hanya da gwajin ƙarin tasiri don wannan dalili.
- **Yin watsi da abubuwan kuɗin shiga marasa alaƙa da talla**: MER mai kwanciyar hankali ko mai ingantuwa a kuɗin shiga mai girma na iya nuna girma na halitta (turawa, baki-da-baki, labarai da aka samu) maimakon ingancin talla; a rarraba kuɗin shiga ta tushen samu a duk inda zai yiwu don guje wa ƙara wa talla daraja fiye da kima.

## Tushe

- Association of National Advertisers (ANA), jagora kan auna talla, danganta, da bayyanar kafafen yaɗa labarai
- Marketing Accountability Standards Board (MASB), jagora kan ma'anar ma'aunin talla da ƙa'idodin aunawa
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu da na masana'antu kan ƙirar haɗin talla da gwajin ƙarin tasiri a matsayin ƙari ga danganta da dandali ya bayar

Duba kuma: [ainihin kuɗin samun abokin ciniki](../ainihin-kudin-samun-abokin-ciniki/) da [rabon LTV zuwa CAC](../rabon-ltv-zuwa-cac/), sauran ma'aunin tattalin arzikin girma guda biyu na tushe da ake bayar da rahoton wannan rabo tare da su yawanci.
