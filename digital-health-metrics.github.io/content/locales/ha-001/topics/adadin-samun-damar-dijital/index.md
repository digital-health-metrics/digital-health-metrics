# Adadin Samun Damar Dijital

Adadin samun damar dijital yana auna kaso na yawan marasa lafiyar da suka cancanta waɗanda ke da ainihin hanyar amfani da samfurin lafiyar dijital kwata-kwata: haɗin broadband ko bayanan wayar hannu mai aminci, na'ura mai iya intanet, da asusu mai aiki a shafin marasa lafiya ko manhajar da ta dace. Shi ne ma'aunin sharaɗin farko ga kowane ma'aunin lafiyar dijital a wannan littafi — yawan jama'a ba zai iya yin rajista, mu'amala da, ko amfana daga kowane samfurin lafiyar dijital da ba zai iya isa gare shi a tsari ba, komai kyawun ƙirar samfurin.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Ma'aunin karɓa da hulɗar lafiyar dijital a bayyane suna ɗaukar cewa yawan jama'a ya riga ya sami damar dijital, kuma bayar da rahoton adadin karɓa ko hulɗa ba tare da fara kafa adadin samun dama na ƙasa ba yana haɗarin keɓe marasa lafiyar da ba su da damar a ɓoye — waɗanda galibi su ne kuma marasa lafiyar da ke da mafi girman buƙatar lafiya. Tsarin Auna Daidaiton Lafiyar Dijital na HIMSS (DHEMF) da makamantansa suna ɗaukar samun damar dijital a matsayin ma'aunin daidaito na tushe, na matakin farko saboda haka daidai: ɗan tsakani da aka gina ba tare da la'akari da giɓin samun dama ba yakan ƙarfafa, maimakon rufe, rashin daidaiton lafiya da ake da shi — dabarar telehealth-da-farko na iya rage samun kulawa ba da gangan ba ga marasa lafiyar da ba su da haɗi ko na'ura mai aminci, ko da yake tana ƙara ingantaccen ƙwarewa ga marasa lafiyar da suka riga suna da duka biyun. Ya kamata a bibiya a kuma bayar da rahoton adadin samun damar dijital ta ɓangaren alƙaluma da yanki, domin matsakaicin ƙasa ko na ƙungiya gaba ɗaya yakan ɓoye manyan giɓi ga takamaiman yawan jama'a.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Digital access rate = patients with broadband/mobile connectivity AND an
                       internet-capable device AND an active patient portal
                       or app account / total eligible patient population × 100

Report each sub-component separately as well as the combined rate:
  Connectivity rate     = patients with a reliable internet connection /
                           eligible population × 100
  Device ownership rate = patients with an internet-capable device /
                           eligible population × 100
  Portal activation rate = patients with an active portal/app account /
                           eligible population × 100 (see patient portal
                           adoption rate for the fuller adoption funnel)
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani tsarin kiwon lafiya yana hidimtawa yawan marasa lafiya da suka cancanta 40,000. Binciken marasa lafiya da bayanan kayayyakin more rayuwa sun nuna 34,000 (85%) suna da amintaccen broadband ko haɗin wayar hannu, 33,000 (82.5%) suna da na'ura mai iya intanet, kuma daga cikin marasa lafiyar da suka cika sharuɗɗan biyu, 27,000 (67.5% na cikakken yawan da suka cancanta) suna da asusun shafin marasa lafiya mai aiki. Rarraba ta shekaru yana nuna marasa lafiya masu shekaru 65+ suna da adadin samun damar dijital na haɗaka na 48% kawai, idan aka kwatanta da 78% ga marasa lafiya ƙasa da 65 — giɓi da matsakaicin 67.5% na ƙungiya gaba ɗaya ya ɓoye gaba ɗaya, kuma wanda ya kamata ya sanar kai tsaye ko za a iya ba da wani sabis na dijital kaɗai cikin aminci ga wannan yawan jama'a.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Bayanan haɗi da mallakar na'ura yawanci suna zuwa ne daga haɗin bayar da rahoton kai na marasa lafiya (ta bincike ko tambayoyin shiga), bayanan taswirar samuwar broadband na ƙasa na Federal Communications Commission (FCC) ko makamancin haka ga yankin marasa lafiya, da bayanan kunna shafi daga tsare-tsaren ƙungiyar kanta. Samuwar broadband a matakin yanki (ko mai bayar da sabis yana hidima a wani lambar ZIP ko lambar gidan waya) wakili ne mai rauni fiye da haɗin matakin gida, tun da bayanan samuwa na matakin yanki ba su faɗi ko wani takamaiman mara lafiya zai iya biya ko ya zaɓi yin rajista ga wannan sabis ba — kada a rikita adadin samun dama na matakin yanki da na matakin gida. Samun na'ura da haɗi kuma na iya zama na haɗin gwiwa a cikin gida (misali, wayar hannu ɗaya da ƴan uwa da yawa ke amfani da ita), wanda bayanan bincike na matakin gida ke ɗauka da kyau fiye da bayanan shiga shafi na matakin mutum kaɗai.

## Kura-kurai

- **Bayar da rahoton matsakaicin ƙungiya gaba ɗaya kawai**: wannan yana ɓoye manyan giɓin samun dama ga tsofaffi, masu ƙarancin kuɗin shiga, na karkara, ko wasu ɓangarorin marasa lafiya da aka ware a dijital; koyaushe a rarraba ta ɓangaren alƙaluma da yanki.
- **Rikita samuwar broadband na matakin yanki da ainihin samun damar gida**: lambar ZIP ko lambar gidan waya da mai bayar da broadband ke "hidima" ba yana nufin kowane gida a cikinta yana yin rajista ko zai iya biyan wannan sabis ba.
- **Ɗaukar mallakar na'ura a matsayin gaskiyar lokaci ɗaya, ta tsaye**: samun na'ura na iya zama na wucin gadi (tsohuwar na'ura, wayar da aka rasa ko ta lalace, na'urar iyali da aka sake rabawa), don haka a auna adadin samun dama akai-akai, ba a ɗauka cewa yana da kwanciyar hankali da zarar an tantance shi ba.
- **Ƙera hanyar dijital kaɗai kafin kafa adadin samun dama na yawan jama'ar da abin ya shafa**: mayar da sabis zuwa dijital kaɗai ba tare da fara tabbatar da ainihin adadin samun damar dijital na yawan da aka nufa ba yana haɗarin keɓe marasa lafiyar da suka fi rashin iya isa ga wata hanya ta daban a ɓoye.
