# Rabon Mannewar Masu Amfani na DAU/MAU

Rabon mannewar masu amfani na DAU/MAU yana kwatanta masu amfani masu aiki na yau da kullum (DAU) da masu amfani masu aiki na wata-wata (MAU) — ma'aunin tushe ɗaya da ake amfani da shi ga masu amfani masu aiki na mako-mako (WAU) da MAU — don bayyana kaso na babban tushen masu amfani na samfur da ke mu'amala da shi a kowace rana. Shi ne daidaitaccen ma'aunin nazarin samfur na ƙarfin hulɗa, wanda ya bambanta da ko an riƙe mai amfani kwata-kwata (duba adadin riƙe masu amfani) ko yadda wani takamaiman mara lafiya da aka yi wa rajista ke mu'amala akai-akai a tsawon lokaci (duba adadin dorewar hulɗa da marasa lafiya): mannewa tana bayyana yanayin amfani a matakin yawan jama'a, ba tsarin kowane mutum ba.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Samfuran lafiyar dijital guda biyu na iya bayar da rahoton adadin masu amfani masu aiki na wata-wata iri ɗaya yayin da suke da ƙarfin hulɗa daban sosai: ɗaya inda yawancin masu amfani suke buɗe manhajar kusan kullum, da wani inda yawancinsu suke buɗe ta sau ɗaya a wata kafin ta zama ba ta aiki. Rabon mannewar DAU/MAU yana bambance waɗannan yanayi biyu masu bambanci sosai da adadi ɗaya, mai sauƙi, wanda aka fahimta sosai wanda ƙungiyoyin samfur da na asibiti za su iya bibiya a tsawon lokaci kuma su kwatanta da kewayen masana'antu da aka sani — rabo na kusan 20% ma'auni ne da ake ambata sosai da ake ɗauka mai ma'ana ga yawancin manhajojin masu amfani, yayin da samfuran al'ada ta yau da kullum (littafin abinci ko alamomin cuta da ake tsammanin mara lafiya zai yi amfani da shi kowace rana) ya kamata a auna su da ma'auni mafi girma sosai. Domin mannewa tana da hankali ga yadda ake ayyana "mai aiki", ta fi amfani a matsayin yanayi na samfur ɗaya a tsawon lokaci, da kuma kwatancen samfuran da aka ƙera don irin tsarin amfani iri ɗaya, maimakon ma'auni na cikakke tsakanin masana'antu.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
DAU/MAU stickiness ratio = average daily active users in period /
                            monthly active users in same period × 100

WAU/MAU ratio (weekly, same principle) is a softer variant, more
appropriate for products expected to be used a few times per week
rather than daily.

"Active" must be defined precisely and consistently (e.g. a completed
qualifying action, not a passive app open) across both the numerator
and denominator.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wata manhajar sarrafa ciwon sukari ta dijital tana da masu amfani masu aiki na wata-wata 10,000 a wata da aka bayar, wanda aka ayyana a matsayin duk mai amfani da ya kammala aƙalla aiki ɗaya mai cancanta (shigar da sukari, shigar da abinci, ko alamar shan magani) a wannan watan. Matsakaicin ƙididdigar masu amfani masu aiki na yau da kullum a kwanaki 30 na wannan watan yana ba da matsakaicin DAU na 2,200. Rabon mannewar DAU/MAU shi ne 2,200 / 10,000 × 100 = 22%, wanda ke nuna cewa a rana ta yau da kullum, kusan 22% na tushen masu amfani na wata-wata na manhajar suna mu'amala da ita — adadi mai ma'ana ga kayan aikin cuta mai ɗorewa na al'ada ta yau da kullum, ko da yake ƙungiyar samfur za ta so ganin yana ƙaruwa a tsawon lokaci yayin da kyakkyawar halayya (shigar da bayanai kullum) ke zama al'ada ga marasa lafiyar da aka yi wa rajista.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Ana lissafa DAU, WAU, da MAU duka daga rajistar abubuwan da suka faru iri ɗaya, ta amfani da ma'anar "abin da ya faru mai aiki mai cancanta" ɗaya mai daidaito a kowane taga; canza wannan ma'ana tsakanin lissafin mai lissafi da mai raba (misali ƙidaya duk buɗe manhaja don DAU amma kammalallen aiki kawai don MAU) zai haifar da rabo mai karkacewa wanda ba ya nuna ƙarfin hulɗa na gaske. Ma'aunin da ya dace na mannewa ya dogara sosai kan tsarin amfani da aka nufa na samfur: kayan aikin da aka nufa don amfani sau ɗaya a mako (duba alamomin cuta na mako-mako) zai kuma ya kamata ya sami ƙaramin rabon DAU/MAU fiye da kayan aikin da aka nufa don amfani kullum (manhajar abokiyar na'urar sa ido kan sukari a ci gaba), don haka ya kamata a fassara mannewa koyaushe bisa tsarin amfani da aka nufa na samfur kansa, ba manufa ɗaya ta duniya ba.

## Kura-kurai

- **Kwatanta rabon mannewa tsakanin samfuran da ke da yawan amfani da aka nufa daban-daban**: kayan aikin da ake amfani da su sau ɗaya a mako zai nuna ƙaramin rabon DAU/MAU a tsari fiye da kayan aikin yau da kullum ko da duka biyun suna aiki daidai yadda aka nufa don amfaninsu; a auna da tsarin da aka nufa na samfur kansa, ba manufa ɗaya ta duniya ba.
- **Amfani da ma'anonin aiki marasa daidaito tsakanin mai lissafi da mai raba**: wannan na iya haifar da rabon mannewa wanda ba ya nuna ƙarfin hulɗa na gaske kuma ba za a iya kwatanta shi cikin ma'ana a tsawon lokaci ko da wasu samfuran ba.
- **Ɗaukar ƙaruwar rabon mannewa a matsayin mai kyau ba tare da shakka ba tare da duba yanayin MAU gaba ɗaya ba**: ƙaruwar rabo da ƙarancin tushen masu amfani na ainihi mai al'ada ke haifarwa yayin da MAU gaba ɗaya ke raguwa yanayi ne da ya bambanta sosai — kuma ya fi damuwa — da wanda ƙaruwar hulɗa ta yau da kullum ta gaske a tsakanin tushen masu amfani mai kwanciyar hankali ko mai girma ke haifarwa.
- **Yin watsi da tasirin ranar mako da na yanayi kan DAU**: DAU na iya bambanta sosai da ranar mako (ranakun aiki da ƙarshen mako) ko yanayi ga yawancin samfuran lafiya; a yi amfani da matsakaicin DAU a tsawon lokacin da ya ƙunshi cikakken zagayen halitta maimakon ɗan gajeren taga da zai iya karkacewa.
