# Makin Net Promoter na Marasa Lafiya

Makin Net Promoter (NPS) na marasa lafiya yana auna yarda ta marasa lafiya na ba da shawarar samfurin lafiyar dijital ko sabis na telehealth ga wasu, bisa tambaya ɗaya ta bincike — "Yaya yuwuwar za ku ba da shawarar wannan sabis ga aboki ko abokin aiki?" — da ake ba da maki daga 0 zuwa 10. Masu amsa masu maki 9-10 "masu bayar da shawara" ne (promoters), 7-8 "marasa ɓangare" ne (passives), kuma 0-6 "masu ƙin" ne (detractors); NPS shi ne kaso na masu bayar da shawara debe kaso na masu ƙi. Shi ne ma'aunin gamsuwar marasa lafiya da aka fi amfani da shi sosai, kuma ake suka sosai, a lafiyar dijital, ana daraja shi saboda sauƙinsa amma yana da iyaka a abin da zai iya ganowa shi kaɗai.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

NPS yana ba ƙungiyoyin lafiyar dijital alamar gamsuwa mai sauƙi, ta daidaito, wadda za a iya kwatantawa wadda ba ta da tsada a tattara kuma mai sauƙi ga masu ruwa da tsaki da ba ƙwararru ba (shugabanni, allon gudanarwa, masu ba da kwangila) don fassara a kallo ɗaya, shi ya sa yake ci gaba da shahara duk da iyakokin hanya da aka rubuta sosai. Musamman ga telehealth da samfuran ƙofar gaba ta dijital, NPS galibi shi ne alamar farko ta ko marasa lafiya za su ci gaba da zaɓar hanyar dijital maimakon ta ido-da-ido idan duka biyun suna nan, wanda ke da tasiri kai tsaye ga shirin haɗin hanyoyi da ƙarfi. Duk da haka, NPS adadi ne guda ɗaya, na taƙaitaccen matakin sama: raguwar NPS tana gaya wa ƙungiya cewa wani abu ba daidai ba ne amma ba menene ba, don haka ya kamata a haɗa shi koyaushe da ra'ayoyin rubutu na buɗe ko kayan aikin saukin amfani mafi cikakken bayani don ya zama mai aiki maimakon lambar makin kawai.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
NPS = % promoters (score 9-10) − % detractors (score 0-6)

Result is a number from −100 to +100, not a percentage, despite being
derived from percentages — never append a "%" sign to an NPS figure.

Report alongside:
  response rate (% of patients surveyed who responded)
  sample size
  the exact question wording used
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani dandalin telehealth yana bincikar marasa lafiya 1,000 bayan shawarwarin bidiyo kuma yana karɓar amsoshi 400 (adadin amsa 40%). Daga cikin masu amsawa 400, 220 sun ba da maki 9-10 (masu bayar da shawara, 55%), 100 sun ba da 7-8 (marasa ɓangare, 25%), kuma 80 sun ba da 0-6 (masu ƙi, 20%). NPS shi ne 55 − 20 = 35. Wannan adadi yana da ma'ana ne kawai a cikin mahallin: NPS na 35 na iya zama sakamako mai ƙarfi idan aka kwatanta da masana'antar telehealth gabaɗaya, ko raguwa mai damuwa idan aka kwatanta da makin wannan dandali na 48 a kwata da ya gabata — NPS ya fi amfani sosai a matsayin yanayi a tsawon lokaci ga samfur ɗaya fiye da ma'auni na cikakke, na sau ɗaya akan wani daban.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Ana tattara NPS ta hanyar bincike bayan hulɗa, yawanci ana haifar da shi kai tsaye bayan ziyarar bidiyo, zaman manhaja, ko zangon kulawa, kuma adadin amsa yana da muhimmanci sosai: ƙarancin adadin amsa (ƙasa da ~40% da aka gani a misalin da aka yi aiki) yana haɗarin son zuciya na rashin amsa, inda marasa lafiya masu gamsuwa sosai ko rashin gamsuwa sosai ne kawai ke damuwa su amsa, suna jan makin zuwa iyakoki da nesa da ainihin ra'ayin yawan jama'a. Kwatanta NPS tsakanin ƙungiyoyi ko ma tsakanin hanyoyin ƙungiya ɗaya daban-daban (misali telehealth da ido-da-ido) yana da inganci ne kawai idan kalmomin tambaya, lokaci, da yawan jama'ar bincike suna iya kwatantawa da gaske; ƙananan canje-canje na kalmomi an san suna sauya maki a bayyane. Ya kamata a ɗauki NPS a matsayin sakamako da za a bayyana, ba ƙarshe a kansa ba — ra'ayoyin rubutu na buɗe da galibi ke tare da binciken NPS yawanci sun fi aiki fiye da makin.

## Kura-kurai

- **Kwatanta adadin NPS da aka tattara da kalmomin tambaya ko lokaci daban-daban**: ko ƙananan bambance-bambancen ƙirar bincike na iya sauya maki da maki da yawa, yana sa kwatancen NPS tsakanin ƙungiyoyi ya zama marar aminci fiye da yadda yake bayyana.
- **Yin watsi da adadin amsa**: NPS na kanun labarai da aka lissafa daga adadin amsa na 10% ba shi da aminci sosai fiye da wanda aka lissafa daga 60%, tun da ƙarancin adadin amsa yana da saurin son zuciya na rashin amsa zuwa ra'ayoyi mafi tsanani.
- **Ɗaukar NPS a matsayin kayan aikin ganowa maimakon ma'aunin taƙaitawa**: raguwar NPS tana cewa wani abu ba daidai ba ne amma ba ta taɓa cewa menene ba; ya kamata a haɗa shi koyaushe da ra'ayoyin inganci ko kayan aikin gamsuwa ko saukin amfani mafi cikakken bayani don gano dalilin.
- **Bin NPS a matsayin manufa a kansa**: inganta matsatsi don adadin NPS (misali ta hanyar bincikar marasa lafiya kawai bayan hulɗa masu kyau sosai) na iya inganta makin da aka bayar da rahoto yayin da ƙwarewar mara lafiya ta ƙasa ba ta fi kyau ba, ko ma ta fi muni.
