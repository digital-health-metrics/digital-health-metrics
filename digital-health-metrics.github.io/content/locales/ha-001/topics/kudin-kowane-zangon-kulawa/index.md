# Kuɗin Kowane Zangon Kulawa

Kuɗin kowane zangon kulawa shi ne jimillar kuɗin da aka kashe wajen magance wani zangon asibiti da aka ayyana — misali sauya ƙashin ƙugu da murmurewarsa, ko lokacin sarrafa ciwon sukari — idan aka kwatanta da ƙungiyar tushe ta tarihi da aka yi wa magani ba tare da ɗan tsakani na dijital da ake tantancewa ba. Shi ne daidaitaccen ma'aunin kwatanta kuɗi a kulawa bisa ƙima, domin yana ɗaukar cikakken hoton tattalin arziki na zangon maimakon kowane layin kuɗi shi kaɗai, kuma shi ne ma'aunin da masu biyan kuɗi da tsarin kiwon lafiya suka fi buƙata kafin su amince da ba da kuɗin shirin lafiyar dijital a babban sikeli.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Kwangilolin kulawa bisa ƙima suna ƙara biyan kuɗi don sakamako da zangon kulawa maimakon ayyuka ɗaya-ɗaya, wanda ke nufin dole ne a kafa hujjar kuɗi ta shirin lafiyar dijital da kuɗi iri ɗaya: jimillar kuɗi na kowane zango, idan aka kwatanta da abin da irin wannan zangon ya kashe kafin ɗan tsakanin ya wanzu. Shirin da ke rage rukunin kuɗi ɗaya (misali ƙarancin ziyarar bibiya ido-da-ido) yayin da yake ƙara wani (ƙarin kuɗin na'ura, ƙarin lokacin ma'aikatan sa ido na asibiti) ba lallai ne ya rage jimillar kuɗin kowane zango ba, kuma cikakken lissafin kuɗi na matakin zango ne kaɗai ke ɗaukar wannan musayar; duba kowane layin kuɗi shi kaɗai yana haɗarin ƙarewa da ƙarshe mai ruɗarwa a kowace hanya. Saboda ana iya gina ma'anar zango da lokutan tushe ta hanyoyin da ke fifita wani ƙarshe, wannan ma'auni yana buƙatar bayyana hanya fiye da yawancin sauran a wannan littafi don ya zama abin dogaro ga mai biyan kuɗi ko ƙungiyar kuɗi mai shakka.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Cost per episode of care = total cost of all care delivered within a
                            defined episode window (all care settings,
                            all cost categories) / number of episodes

Compare against a historical baseline cohort's cost per episode for
the same clinically defined episode type, adjusted for case mix
(age, comorbidity, severity) between the two cohorts.

Include, not just direct clinical costs: technology platform and
device costs, additional clinical staffing time, and any care that
shifted setting (e.g. from inpatient to home) rather than
disappearing entirely.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Kuɗin tushe na tarihi na wani tsarin kiwon lafiya don zangon sauya ƙashin ƙugu (daga tiyata zuwa murmurewa na kwanaki 90) shi ne $28,000 kowane zango, bisa zango 200 na tarihi. An gabatar da sabon shirin sa ido na dijital bayan tiyata, kuma sabbin zango 150 da ke amfani da shirin suna nuna matsakaicin kuɗi na $24,500 kowane zango — raguwa ta $3,500 kowane zango, galibi saboda ƙarancin ziyarar ɗakin gaggawa a lokacin murmurewa da gajeren matsakaicin zaman asibiti. Bayan daidaita haɗari saboda ƙungiyar da aka sa ido ta dijital tana da ƙarami a shekaru da ƙarancin cututtukan da ke tare da shi idan aka kwatanta da tushen tarihi, tanadin da aka daidaita ya ragu zuwa $2,100 kowane zango — har yanzu ingantuwa ce ta gaske, amma ƙarami sosai fiye da kwatancen ɗanyen da ba a daidaita ba ya nuna.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Yawanci ana haɗa jimillar kuɗin zango daga tsarin lissafin kuɗi ko kuɗi na tsarin kiwon lafiya kansa, ana haɗa bayanan da'awa, rarraba kuɗi na ciki, da, idan akwai dandalin dijital, kuɗin lasisi da na kayan aikinsa — haɗa wannan adadi daidai galibi shi ne mafi wahala kuma mafi ɗaukar albarkatu a kowane nazarin darajar lafiyar dijital, domin ana yawan rubuta kuɗi a tsare-tsare daban waɗanda ba a taɓa ƙera su don haɗawa a matakin zango ba. Daidaita haɗin shari'a yana da muhimmanci a duk lokacin da ba a ware ƙungiyar da aka gudanar ta dijital da ƙungiyar tushe ta tarihi ta hanyar bazuwar gaskiya ba, tun da yawanci ana ba da shirye-shiryen dijital ga marasa lafiya masu hulɗa sosai, ko lafiyayyu gabaɗaya, ko masu himma tukuna, wanda zai iya haifar da tanadin kuɗi da ake gani wanda a gaskiya tasirin zaɓi ne ba tasirin shirin na gaskiya ba.

## Kura-kurai

- **Kwatanta kuɗin da ba a daidaita ba tsakanin ƙungiyoyi masu haɗin shari'a daban-daban**: ƙungiyar da aka gudanar ta dijital wadda ta zama lafiyayya ko ƙarancin haɗari fiye da tushen tarihi za ta nuna ƙaramin kuɗin kowane zango saboda dalilai da ba su da alaƙa da ɗan tsakanin dijital kansa; koyaushe a daidaita haɗari kafin a kwatanta.
- **Mantawa da kuɗin fasaha da na ma'aikata a ɓangaren "dijital" na kwatancen**: nazarin kuɗi da ke bibiyar rage amfani da asibiti kawai yayin da yake watsi da kuɗin dandali, na'ura, da ma'aikatan gudanar da shirin dijital zai ƙara tanadin net.
- **Ayyana taga zango ba daidai ba tsakanin ƙungiyoyi**: kwatanta taga zango na kwanaki 90 ga ƙungiya ɗaya da na kwanaki 60 ga wata zai haifar da kwatancen kuɗi da ba ya auna abu ɗaya a zahiri.
- **Ɗaukar sauya kuɗi a matsayin rage kuɗi**: kuɗin da aka mayar daga wurin kulawa ɗaya zuwa wani (misali daga asibiti zuwa gida mai sa ido) bincike ne na gaske kuma mai daraja, amma ya bambanta a nazari da kuɗin da aka kawar gaba ɗaya, kuma ya kamata a bayar da rahoton su biyun daban.

## Tushe

- Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), Bundled Payments for Care Improvement (BPCI) da jagorar tsarin biyan kuɗi bisa zango
- Healthcare Financial Management Association (HFMA), jagora kan hanyar lissafin kuɗin zangon kulawa
- Wallafe-wallafen da aka duba takwarorinsu kan nazarin kuɗin kulawa bisa ƙimar lafiyar dijital, misali nazarin da aka buga a Health Affairs da American Journal of Managed Care

Duba kuma: [riba kan jari (ROI) da darajar jari (VOI)](../riba-kan-jari-roi-da-darajar-jari-voi/), wanda ke amfani da kuɗin kowane zangon kulawa a matsayin ɗaya daga cikin muhimman abubuwan da yake shigarwa.
