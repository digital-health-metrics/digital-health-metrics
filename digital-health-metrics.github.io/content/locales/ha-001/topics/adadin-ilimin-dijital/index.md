# Adadin Ilimin Dijital

Adadin ilimin dijital yana auna kaso na yawan marasa lafiya da ke iya kammala ayyukan da aka saba a dandalin lafiyar dijital da kansu kuma cikin nasara — shiga, tsara alƙawari, shiga ziyarar bidiyo, ko karanta sakamakon gwaji — ba tare da buƙatar taimakon wani mutum ba. Ya bambanta da, kuma ya kamata a auna shi daban da, adadin samun damar dijital: mara lafiya na iya samun wayar hannu mai wayo da haɗin broadband amma har yanzu ba zai iya tafiyar da dandalin telehealth ba tare da taimako ba, kuma rikita ma'aunin biyu yana ɓoye daidai yawan jama'ar da wannan ma'auni ke nufin ya bayyana.

## Dalilin da ya sa yake da muhimmanci

Samun damar dijital kaɗai ba ya tabbatar da cewa mara lafiya zai iya amfani da sabis na lafiyar dijital yadda ya kamata: marasa lafiya masu ƙarancin ilimin lafiya, ƙarancin ƙwarewar fasaha gabaɗaya, nakasar fahimta ko gani, ko shingen harshe da mu'amalar dandali na iya samun cikakkiyar damar fasaha amma har yanzu su kasa kammala aiki da kansu, kuma wannan giɓi yana da alaƙa a tsari da ƙungiyoyin alƙaluma da suka riga suke fuskantar wasu rashin daidaiton lafiya. Tsarin Auna Daidaiton Lafiyar Dijital na HIMSS yana ɗaukar ilimin dijital a matsayin ginshiƙi daban da samun dama saboda haka daidai: rufe giɓin samun dama ba tare da magance giɓin ilimi ba na iya barin yawan jama'a a haɗe ta fasaha amma ba su iya amfana ta aiki. Ƙungiyoyin da ke auna kammala aiki da lokacin kammalawa ga ayyukan dandali na gama gari, a rarrabe ta harshe da alamomin zamantakewa-tattalin arziki, suna iya gano shingen ilimi da ba da tallafi da aka nufa (mu'amala mai sauƙi, shiga tare da taimako, abun ciki a wasu harsuna) da daidaito fiye da ƙungiyoyin da ke dogara da ma'aunin samun dama ko makin gamsuwa gaba ɗaya kaɗai.

## Yadda ake lissafa shi

```
Digital literacy rate = patients who independently complete a defined
                         task without assistance / patients who attempt
                         that task × 100

Common tasks measured: account login, appointment scheduling, joining
a video visit, viewing a test result, completing an intake form.

Report per task, not as a single blended score, since literacy for
simple tasks (login) and complex tasks (completing a multi-step intake
form) differ substantially and blending them obscures where the
specific barrier lies.
```

## Misali da aka yi aiki

Wani tsarin kiwon lafiya yana bibiyar shiga ziyarar bidiyo a matsayin aiki da aka ayyana a alƙawuran telehealth 5,000 da aka tsara a wata. Daga cikinsu, marasa lafiya 4,100 sun shiga cikin nasara ba tare da kiran tallafi ko taimakon fasaha yayin ziyara ba (adadin ilimin dijital na wannan aiki: 82%). Rarrabawa ta harshe na farko yana nuna adadin 89% ga marasa lafiya masu jin Turanci idan aka kwatanta da 61% ga marasa lafiyar da harshensu na farko ya bambanta da harshen mu'amala na asali na dandalin — giɓin maki 28 wanda ba za a iya gani ba idan an bayar da rahoton adadin 82% na haɗaka kaɗai, kuma wanda ke nuni kai tsaye ga takamaiman ɗan tsakani da za a iya aiwatarwa (fassarar mu'amala da umarni) maimakon wata matsalar ilimi ta gaba ɗaya maras tabbas.

## Tushen bayanai da gargaɗi

Ana ɗaukar bayanan kammala aiki yawanci daga rajistar abubuwan da suka faru na dandalin kansa (shin mara lafiya ya isa ziyarar bidiyo, shin tsarin tsara alƙawari ya kammala ba tare da barin ba), tare da ƙarin bayanan kiran tallafi ko tuntuɓar teburin taimako don gano ayyukan da "suka kammala" ta fasaha kawai saboda mara lafiya ya sami taimako kai tsaye a tsakiya. Aikin da aka ƙidaya a matsayin "an kammala" daga rajistar tsari kaɗai na iya ɓoye cewa mara lafiya yana buƙatar kira daga ɗan uwa ko ma'aikacin tallafi don isa can — ya kamata a ayyana a kuma bibiya kammalawa mai zaman kansa na ilimi da gaske daban da wanda aka taimaka a duk inda dandalin zai iya bambance su. Ilimin dijital yana da alaƙa da, amma ya bambanta a nazari da, ilimin lafiya da ilimin gama gari; ya kamata a yi amfani da kayan aikin da aka tabbatar (maimakon zato na yau da kullum bisa shekaru ko alƙaluma kaɗai) a duk inda ake buƙatar tantancewa a hukumance.

## Kura-kurai

- **Rikita ilimin dijital da samun damar dijital**: mara lafiya mai cikakkiyar damar fasaha na iya rashin ilimin amfani da ita yadda ya kamata; waɗannan ma'auni ne daban masu buƙatar tsoma baki daban, kuma kada a taɓa bayar da rahotonsu a matsayin adadi ɗaya na haɗaka.
- **Ƙidaya kammalawa da aka taimaka a matsayin nasara ba taimako**: idan mara lafiya ya kammala aiki ne kawai da kiran tallafi ko taimakon ɗan uwa, wannan giɓin ilimi ne da dandalin ya rufe, ba ya warware ba; a bambance kammalawa da aka taimaka da wadda ba a taimaka ba a duk inda bayanai suka ba da dama.
- **Bayar da rahoton makin kammala aiki na haɗaka ɗaya**: ilimi ga aiki mai sauƙi (shiga) da mai rikitarwa (kammala fom ɗin shiga dalla-dalla) ya bambanta sosai; a bayar da rahoto ga kowane aiki don gano daidai inda shingen yake.
- **Zaton shekaru kaɗai suna hasashen ilimin dijital**: ko da yake shekaru suna da alaƙa da ƙarancin ilimin dijital gabaɗaya, ƙwarewar harshen mu'amalar dandali da sanin fasaha gabaɗaya galibi su ne masu hasashe mafi ƙarfi na kowane mutum kuma ya kamata a auna su kai tsaye maimakon a ɗauka daga shekaru.
