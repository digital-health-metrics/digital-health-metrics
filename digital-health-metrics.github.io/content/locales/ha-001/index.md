# Ma'aunin Lafiyar Dijital

Littafin tunani na ma'anonin ma'aunin lafiyar dijital: abin da kowane ma'auni yake nufi, yadda ake lissafa shi, misali da aka yi aiki, tushen bayanai da gargaɗi, da kura-kuran da aka saba yi, an tsara shi ne don mutanen da ke ginawa, gudanarwa, da tantance kayayyaki da ayyukan lafiyar dijital.

Sabon zuwa? Fara da [adadin karɓar shafin marasa lafiya](topics/adadin-karbar-shafin-marasa-lafiya/) da [adadin rashin zuwa alƙawari](topics/adadin-rashin-zuwa-alkawari/) — ma'auni biyu da ke fitowa a kusan kowace hujjar kasuwanci ta lafiyar dijital.

## Hulɗar marasa lafiya da samun damar kulawa

- [Adadin karɓar shafin marasa lafiya](topics/adadin-karbar-shafin-marasa-lafiya/) — rajista, kunnawa, da amfani mai ɗorewa; adadi uku daban-daban da ake haɗa su a matsayin ɗaya sau da yawa
- [Adadin ziyarar lafiya ta nesa](topics/adadin-ziyarar-lafiya-ta-nesa/) — kaso na kulawar da ake bayarwa daga nesa, da dalilin da ya sa bai kamata a taɓa bayar da rahoton bidiyo da waya a matsayin lamba ɗaya ba
- [Adadin rashin zuwa alƙawari](topics/adadin-rashin-zuwa-alkawari/) — ma'aunin aiki mafi tsufa a harkar kiwon lafiya, kuma ɗaya daga cikin manufofin da ke da mafi kyawun shaida na tunatarwa ta dijital
- [Adadin dorewar hulɗa da marasa lafiya](topics/adadin-dorewar-hulda-da-marasa-lafiya/) — yadda mara lafiya ke mu'amala da kayan aikin dijital akai-akai a tsawon lokaci, ba tare da la'akari da ko ya taɓa amfani da shi kwata-kwata ba
- [Adadin riƙe masu amfani](topics/adadin-rike-masu-amfani/) — lanƙwasar riƙewar ƙungiya wadda ke bambance samfurin da ke da tsarin amfani mai ɗorewa da wanda ke hawa kan igiyar ruwa ta sabon abu
- [Rabon mannewar masu amfani na DAU/MAU](topics/rabon-mannewar-masu-amfani-dau-mau/) — ma'aunin nazarin samfur na yau da kullum na ƙarfin hulɗa a tsakanin dukkan masu amfani
- [Makin Net Promoter na marasa lafiya](topics/makin-net-promoter-na-marasa-lafiya/) — ma'aunin gamsuwa na tambaya guda da ake amfani da shi sosai, kuma ake suka sosai
- [Makin ma'aunin saukin amfani da tsari](topics/makin-ma-aunin-saukin-amfani-da-tsari/) — daidaitaccen jerin tambayoyi 10 da ke ƙididdige yadda manhaja ke da sauƙin amfani a zahiri

## Gudanar da kulawar dijital da aminci

- [Adadin wuce gargaɗin asibiti](topics/adadin-wuce-gargadin-asibiti/) — daidaitaccen alamar gajiyawar gargaɗi a cikin tallafin yanke shawara na asibiti
- [Lokacin sarrafa turawar marasa lafiya ta dijital](topics/lokacin-sarrafa-turawar-marasa-lafiya-ta-dijital/) — ma'aunin tsari da ke nuna ko tsarin tura marasa lafiya na lantarki yana adana lokaci da gaske
- [Lokacin ɗaukar mataki](topics/lokacin-daukar-mataki/) — yadda ƙungiyar asibiti ke amsa da sauri ga gargaɗin lafiya na atomatik
- [Rage kwanakin gado a asibiti](topics/rage-kwanakin-gado-a-asibiti/) — kwanakin gadon marasa lafiya da aka ajiye ta hanyar mayar da murmurewa zuwa sashen kwantar da marasa lafiya na kama-da-wane, ana bayar da rahotonsa koyaushe tare da ma'aunin aminci

## Sakamakon asibiti da inganci

- [Adadin inganta ma'aunin halittar jiki](topics/adadin-inganta-ma-aunin-halittar-jiki/) — kaso na marasa lafiya da suka samu canji mai muhimmanci a asibiti a ma'aunin halittar jiki da ake bibiya kamar HbA1c ko BMI
- [Adadin daidaituwar ma'aunin halittar jiki](topics/adadin-daidaituwar-ma-aunin-halittar-jiki/) — kula mai ɗorewa a cikin kewayen manufa, ba tare da la'akari da ingantawa sau ɗaya ba
- [Ingancin raba marasa lafiya ta triage](topics/ingancin-raba-marasa-lafiya-ta-triage/) — ko kayan aikin triage na AI ko na dijital yana tura mara lafiya zuwa matakin kulawa da ya dace daidai
- [Adadin bin umarnin shan magani](topics/adadin-bin-umarnin-shan-magani/) — adadin kwanakin da mara lafiya ya samu maganinsa kamar yadda aka rubuta
- [Adadin sake kwantar da marasa lafiya](topics/adadin-sake-kwantar-da-marasa-lafiya/) — ma'aunin da ya fi dacewa kai tsaye da tattalin arzikin masu biyan kuɗi da kwangilolin kulawa bisa ƙima
- [Adadin kammala ePROM](topics/adadin-kammala-epro/) — adadin kammala ma'aunin sakamakon da marasa lafiya ke bayarwa ta lantarki, da dalilin da ya sa raguwar adadin kanta na iya zama alama ta asibiti

## Tattalin arzikin talla da girma

- [Ainihin kuɗin samun abokin ciniki](topics/ainihin-kudin-samun-abokin-ciniki/) — cikakken kuɗin samun sabon mara lafiya ɗaya, wanda galibi ake rage ƙidayarsa da kashi 30-50%
- [Rabon LTV zuwa CAC](topics/rabon-ltv-zuwa-cac/) — darajar rayuwa idan aka kwatanta da ainihin kuɗin samun abokin ciniki; 3:1 ita ce ma'aunin tushe mai ɗorewa da ake ambata sosai
- [Rabon ingancin talla](topics/rabon-ingancin-talla/) — jimillar kuɗin shiga akan jimillar kashe kuɗin talla, bincike mai zaman kansa kan ROAS da dandamali ke bayarwa

## Daidaiton lafiyar dijital

- [Adadin samun damar dijital](topics/adadin-samun-damar-dijital/) — ma'aunin sharaɗin farko ga kowane ma'aunin lafiyar dijital a cikin wannan littafi
- [Adadin ilimin dijital](topics/adadin-ilimin-dijital/) — ko marasa lafiyar da ke da damar samun shi za su iya amfani da shi da kansu

## Kayayyakin fasaha da aiki

- [Adadin gajiyawar likitoci](topics/adadin-gajiyawar-likitoci/) — ana bibiyarsa tare da nauyin kayan aikin dijital da ke fuskantar ma'aikatan asibiti, domin software da aka ƙera ba daidai ba tana ba da gudummawa, kamar yadda aka rubuta
- [Adadin aikin na'urori](topics/adadin-aikin-na-urori/) — ma'aunin kayayyakin more rayuwa na tushe a ƙarƙashin kowane shirin sa ido daga nesa

## Ƙimar kuɗi da tattalin arziki

- [Kuɗin kowane zangon kulawa](topics/kudin-kowane-zangon-kulawa/) — daidaitaccen ma'aunin kwatanta kuɗi a cikin kwangilolin kulawa bisa ƙima
- [Adadin karkatar da marasa lafiya daga ɗakin gaggawa](topics/adadin-karkatar-da-marasa-lafiya-daga-dakin-gaggawa/) — hulɗar marasa lafiya da aka tura lafiya daga ɗakin gaggawa (ED), ana bayar da rahotonsa koyaushe tare da ma'aunin aminci na gaggawar da aka rasa
- [Riba kan jari (ROI) da darajar jari (VOI)](topics/riba-kan-jari-roi-da-darajar-jari-voi/) — ko jarin dijital ya biya kansa a kuɗi, da ko ya cancanci a yi shi idan aka yi la'akari da dukkan abubuwan da ke da muhimmanci

## Tsare-tsaren tantancewa

- [Tsarin RE-AIM](topics/tsarin-re-aim/) — Isa, Tasiri, Karɓa, Aiwatarwa, da Dorewa; girma biyar maimakon ma'auni ɗaya
- [Tsarin tantance lafiyar dijital na WHO](topics/tsarin-tantance-lafiyar-dijital-na-who/) — jagorar WHO don tantance yiwuwar aiwatarwa, aminci, da daidaito a cikin aiwatarwa na matakin ƙasa
- [ISO/TS 82304-2](topics/kayyadaddun-fasaha-iso-ts-82304-2/) — ƙayyadaddun fasaha na ƙasa da ƙasa da ke bayan yawancin alamomin ingancin manhajojin lafiya da jin daɗi
