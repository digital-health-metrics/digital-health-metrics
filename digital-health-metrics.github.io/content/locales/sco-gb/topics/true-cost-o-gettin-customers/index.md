# True Cost o Gettin Customers

The true cost o gettin customers (true CAC) is the fully loadit cost o gettin ae new peyin customer or enrolled patient, includin no juist paid advertisin spend but ilka ither cost that materially contributit tae that acquisition: agency an creative fees, merketin technology an analytics infrastructure, an — specific tae digital health — the clinical or operational labour cost o intake, eligibility verification, an onboardin. It's there as a separate metric because ad-platform-reportit acquisition cost figures routinely an muckle unnerstate the organization's true cost per customer gotten.

## Why it maitters

Digital health organizations that manage growth uisin only ad-platform-reportit cost-per-acquisition figures are routinely makkin resource-allocation decisions on nummers that leave oot 30-50% o the real cost o acquisition, because thae platform figures capture only media spend an leave oot agency fees, merketin technology licensin, an — critically for healthcare — the labour-heavy intake an eligibility-verification wark that a clinical or operational team daes for ilka new patient afore they can be coontit as gotten. This gap maitters mair in digital health than in maist ither sectors precisely because clinical intake labour is expensive an mandatory, unlike in e-commerce, whaur a "sale" needs essentially nae comparable back-office labour. A team optimisin merketin spend again an artificially low CAC figure'll systematically ower-invest in channels that look cheap on a platform dashboard but are expensive ance true CAC is caulculatit.

## Hoo it's caulculatit

```
True CAC = (paid media spend + agency and creative fees + marketing
            technology and analytics costs + clinical/operational intake
            labour cost) / new customers or patients acquired in period

Clinical/operational intake labour cost should be estimated from
loaded labour cost (salary, benefits, overhead) × average hours spent
per acquired patient on intake, eligibility verification, and onboarding.
```

## Warked example

A digital health company gets 500 new patients in a month. Ad-platform dashboards report a blendit cost-per-acquisition o $120, based on $60,000 o paid media spend. Addin agency fees o $9,000, merketin technology costs o $6,000, an an estimatit intake labour cost o 45 minutes per patient at a fully loadit staff cost o $40/hour (500 × 0.75 × $40 = $15,000) brings total acquisition cost tae $60,000 + $9,000 + $6,000 + $15,000 = $90,000. True CAC is $90,000 / 500 = $180 — 50% higher than the $120 figure the ad platform alane reportit, an the figure that shuid actually inform channel budget allocation an unit-economics decisions.

## Data sources an caveats

Paid media spend an platform-reportit cost-per-acquisition come straucht frae the advertisin platforms themsels (search, social, programmatic); agency fees an merketin technology costs come frae finance or accounts payable records; intake labour cost is the hardest component tae source accurately an usually needs either a time-an-motion study or a reasonable estimate agreed wi operational leadership, sin maist organizations dinnae track staff time per acquisition natively. True CAC shuid be caulculatit per acquisition channel whaur volume allous, sin intake labour cost per patient is aften similar athort channels while media cost varies enormously, meanin the gap atween platform-reportit an true CAC is proportionally biggest for the cheapest-lookin channels.

## Pitfalls

- **Relyin solely on ad-platform dashboards**: platform-reportit cost-per-acquisition structurally leaves oot agency fees, merketin technology costs, an intake labour, an isnae a substitute for a true CAC caulculation.
- **Leavin oot clinical or operational intake labour**: this is consistently the maist commonly missit cost component in digital health specifically, an is aften the single biggest contributor tae the gap atween platform-reportit cost an true CAC.
- **Averagin true CAC athort aa channels**: a blendit true CAC figure can hide that ae channel is dramatically mair expensive ance loadit costs are includit, even though it seemed cheapest on the ad platform alane.
- **No updatin labour cost estimates as intake processes chynge**: an intake process redesign (for example, automatin eligibility verification) can materially chynge true CAC, an a stale labour estimate'll misstate the current figure.
